Immense Tactical Intellect: As previously stated, Kenjaku is a thousand-year-old individual with a vast amount of knowledge acquired over hundreds of years of existence. This includes general expertise, a comprehensive understanding of sorcery, and strategic intellect. He is extraordinarily clever and a masterfully calculating criminal mastermind. This is due to his wealth of knowledge on jujutsu sorcery and his talent for manipulation and tactical forecasting.

Kenjaku's intellect, along with his focus and patience are what makes him most cunning. He was willing to wait hundreds of years to create the proper conditions to accomplish his goals. His careful consideration and planning are what allowed Kenjaku's schemes to overcome the Six Eyes after losing to it multiple times in the past. Kenjaku analyzed Satoru Gojo to come up with a proper strategy to get the nigh-unbeatable sorcerer out of his way. He saw that Satoru was strongest when fighting alone and as a countermeasure, Kenjaku orchestrated the Shibuya Incident to put him in unfavorable conditions. While Satoru was stifled by non-sorcerers and forced to combat several special grade cursed spirits at the same time, Kenjaku waited for just the right moment to seal him.(Chapter 84, Episode 32)(Chapter 90, Episode 33) Kenjaku also had the foresight to make sure nobody from Jujutsu High saw him as Suguru Geto, as this would've ruined the surprise that was key to distracting and sealing Satoru Gojo.(Chapter 16, Episode 7)(Chapter 25, Episode 11)

With his primary obstacle removed, Kenjaku immediately moved on to his next well-thought-out and extremely convoluted scheme. The Culling Game was created as a means for Kenjaku to achieve his primary goal of merging the Japanese population with Tengen. He launched the Culling Game directly after the Shibuya Incident, having fully prepared for it beforehand.(Chapter 136, Episode 47) Throughout numerous generations, Kenjaku recruited sorcerers from different time periods to participate.(Chapter 176)(Chapter 187) He is the only sorcerer who knows how to transform someone into a cursed object after death, a major testament to his unmatched knowledge of jujutsu sorcery.(Chapter 220) Kenjaku even accounted for the return of Satoru Gojo and avoided a surely fatal encounter with him by allying with Sukuna.(Chapter 221)(Chapter 222)

Kenjaku's aforementioned mastery of sorcery is appropriately applied to battle. He is a cerebral fighter who can accurately analyze his opponent's abilities and use his own to overcome them in clever and complex ways. Yuki Tsukumo is one of the few sorcerers that Kenjaku encountered whom he didn't have any prior information. He was well aware of Choso's weaknesses and took advantage of them to easily overpower the Death Painting Womb, but this didn't apply to Yuki.(Chapter 203)(Chapter 204) Kenjaku analyzed Yuki throughout their battle and had to take numerous risks to overcome her. He began their battle by trying to keep her at a distance to observe her skills, a solid strategy that ended up backfiring. Even though he lost the exchange, Kenjaku recognized that his opponent still found his abilities troublesome. Immediately after seeing Yuki's hesitation to open a domain, Kenjaku expanded his own domain and ended up dealing massive damage.(Chapter 205)(Chapter 206)

Kenjaku displayed a talent for thinking on his feet when Choso joined Yuki's side and ambushed him. Although Choso's ambush surprised Kenjaku, he quickly adapted and avoided a fatal blow.(Chapter 207) Kenjaku ultimately defeated Yuki with deception, a sorcerer's greatest weapon. He had her believe that he was using cursed spirit manipulation to buy time for a gravity attack. This lured Yuki into range, where Kenjaku surprised her with a miniature Uzumaki and dealt the finishing blow.(Chapter 207)

Kenjaku came up with clever countermeasures for even his worst matchup. Fumihiko Takaba, whose abilities defy all logic and reasoning. Kenjaku quickly recognized that his techniques were useless against Takaba and he would have to keep his guard up or else his thousand years of jujutsu knowledge would go to waste. Despite his disadvantage, Kenjaku persisted because he was confident every technique has a weakness. He eventually figured out Takaba needed certainty to activate his technique, so Kenjaku responded by targeting his confidence as a comedian. Kenjaku willingly stooped to Takaba's level of foolish antics to regain the advantage in their fight.(Chapter 240) When Takaba regained his confidence and reactivated his technique, Kenjaku understood Takaba well enough to realize the only way to win would be to satisfy his desire for comedy. Rather than fight against Takaba, Kenjaku joined him as a comedy duo and helped manifest his greatest fantasy.(Chapter 242) This led to Kenjaku ultimately avoiding defeat against Takaba.(Chapter 243) Although he was caught off guard and executed by Yuta Okkotsu immediately after, Kenjaku naturally always had a contingency plan in place. He passed on his will, transferring authority to activate the Great Merger to Sukuna, as a failsafe.(Chapter 249)

Innate Techniques
Brain Transplant: Kenjaku's innate technique allows him to swap bodies with another person, even if they are dead, by transplanting his brain into the body of his target. After the transplant, this technique leaves a stitched scar running horizontally through the forehead, which he cannot heal as part of a binding vow.(Fanbook) By undoing the stitches, Kenjaku can remove the top part of his head to reveal his original brain.(Chapter 90, Episode 33) Once inside a body, Kenjaku gains access to the host's cursed energy, all their innate techniques, and memories. Even the Six Eyes cannot recognize him as an imposter as he retains the host's cursed energy.(Chapter 90, Episode 33) Kenjaku can live more than a thousand years thanks to his cursed technique while inhabiting different bodies. As shown with his usage of Kaori Itadori’s technique while fighting Choso and Yuki, it's hinted that even when switching to a new body, Kenjaku retains access and usage of his former host’s innate techniques. However, even if Kenjaku could collect different cursed techniques from his previous hosts, there's a limit to how much his brain can store before overloading.(Chapter 205)(Unnamed)

400 years ago, Kenjaku transplanted his brain with his innate technique into an unknown person's body. He used this vessel to make binding vows with past sorcerers, particularly with Hajime Kashimo.
Noritoshi Kamo: At some point in the past, Kenjaku transplanted his brain with his innate technique into Noritoshi's body. As Noritoshi Kamo, Kenjaku experimented with the fusion of human flesh and curses, resulting in the Death Paintings.
Kaori Itadori: At some point in the past, Kenjaku transplanted his brain with his innate technique into the body of Jin Itadori's partner, Kaori. Posing as her, Kenjaku used her body to mate and give birth to her son, Yuji, to serve as Sukuna's vessel in the Culling Game. It remains unclear how long he inhabited her body.
Suguru Geto: After Suguru Geto's death at the hands of Satoru Gojo, Kenjaku transplanted his brain with his innate technique into Suguru's body and has taken Geto as his host body, effectively giving Kenjaku access to all of Geto's memories, skills, and jujutsu.

Cursed Spirit Manipulation: A technique where the user can summon and control naturally formed cursed spirits for a desired purpose. Due to gaining Geto's body, Kenjaku can utilize this technique as if it is his own innate technique. Thanks to this technique, Kenjaku was shown to have acquired numerous cursed spirits as his personal familiars. He has deployed them to fight off multiple opponents and bombard them with a variety of different cursed techniques that each curse possesses. One of the cursed spirits he acquired was the Smallpox Deity, which was a powerful special grade cursed spirit capable of casting its own Domain Expansion. As shown during its battle against Mei Mei, it was powerful enough to nearly corner the grade 1 sorcerer if not for her brother's assistance to distract it and give her a chance to finish it off. Later, he absorbs Mahito after he is weakened by Yuji Itadori.

Uzumaki: The maximum technique of Curse Spirit Manipulation combines a massive number of collected curses into one powerful attack. Uzumaki extracts a curse's innate technique when used with cursed spirits of semi-grade 1 or higher, allowing the user to keep the techniques for one-time uses.(Chapter 134, Episode 46)(Chapter 205) Kenjaku first used this technique against Kasumi Miwa using Mahito, allowing him to extract Idle Transfiguration.

Antigravity System: A cursed technique that negates gravity when used. Kenjaku primarily uses a cursed technique reversal of this ability, allowing him to intensify the gravitational pull around him. This technique originally belonged to Kaori Itadori.(Chapter 208)

Reverse Cursed Technique
Reverse Cursed Technique: Kenjaku is capable of using reverse cursed technique to heal his body.(Chapter 205) He is also able to use positive energy to fuel a cursed technique reversal.

Domain Expansion
Womb Profusion: Kenjaku's domain expansion takes the form of a giant pillar made up of cursed spirits. It is an open barrier type domain, similar to Malevolent Shrine.(Chapter 205)(Chapter 206) The effect of this domain is unclear, though its guaranteed-hit severely injured Yuki Tsukumo, a special grade jujutsu sorcerer.(Chapter 206)

SECTION: Trivia | level 2 | path []

Kenjaku's name contains the kanji for "net" and "string, thread".
This name is likely a reference to Infallible Lasso Avalokitesvara, also known as Amoghapāśa, who is one of six commonly depicted forms of the Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara. His name is 不空羂索観音 (Fukūkenjaku Kannon) in Japanese. Amoghapāśa is described as a powerful savior who uses his infallible lasso to capture living beings drifting in the ocean of suffering and settle them on the shore of Liberation.
Yuki Tsukumo makes this connection regarding Kenjaku's name and points out its sarcastic nature, as Kenjaku's name implies "compassion" and "salvation" despite his inhumane actions.(Chapter 145, Episode 50)
