As he reminisces on Panda’s birth, Yaga was confronted by Yoshinobu Gakuganji over his cursed corpses, as while they usually rely on their creator’s cursed energy, his can sustain their own without his influence. As a result, the jujutsu higher-ups are attempting to designate him as a Special Grade and sentence him to indefinite imprisonment, fearing his capability to form his own powerful army. Gakuganji asks again if he truly does not know how Panda came to be, which Yaga acknowledges that he does not.

Speaking in a forest with a cursed corpse named Takeru, Yaga is told about how everyone is worried about how down he is feeling, and that they must cheer him up to rectify the situation. Yaga thanks Takeru but asks that he inform them he will not be coming home for some time, claiming it is a long work trip when asked. He tells him not to worry as the forest is protected inside Tengen’s barrier, and he will see his mother soon as well. Before he leaves, Yaga is told that times will be lonely without him.

After cutting away his restraints, Kusakabe tells Panda not to reveal who freed him; he had been captured in an attempt to lure Yaga out of hiding. Owing Yaga a favour, Kusakabe tells him to leave. In the past, Yaga had created Takeru from his nephew’s soul information, as his sister was deeply grieving his death. When he and Yaga met with Kusakabe and his sister, Takeru saw the woman in a wheelchair before them and asked if she was his mother, to which Yaga commended him for guessing right. Takeru’s action of pointing his thumb at himself and declaring he is a genius caused his mother to get up from her wheelchair and crawl to him, recognising the mannerisms of her son. As she hugged Takeru and began to cry, Yaga attempted to apologise but was cut off by Kusakabe, knowing that he has to keep his cursed corpses’ existence a secret. Yaga is saddened knowing he must keep Takeru away from her, but Kusakabe rejects his apology, thanking him for creating Takeru to help her cope with his death.

On his way to see Panda, Yaga was confronted by a jujutsu sorcerer with his death warrant, who gave him one chance to save himself by revealing how he creates his autonomous cursed corpses. Yaga became bemused by their boldness to face him, acting only after Satoru had been sealed, but as he took off his jacket to fight the assailant, he heard Gakuganji playing his guitar in the distance, who began to strum louder and louder. In the aftermath of their clash, while he managed to break his guitar, Yaga is severely wounded with two deep slashes to his chest, and rests against a wall, while the Kyoto principal orders his accompanying sorcerer to leave.

Gakuganji listens as Yaga explains that a body’s information can be used to replicate a soul’s information, which can be placed into a cursed corpse to act autonomously. However, the process by itself isn’t enough to make them powerful; these cores must contain three highly compatible souls into one corpse, allowing them to observe each other and allowing true self-awareness. Three months after the process, the corpse will be able to supply its own cursed energy. Gakuganji is stunned by the revelation and demands to know why he is only now telling his secrets, to which Yaga weakly responds that it is a curse that he is now placing onto him. Panda bursts from the trees and sees the dying Yaga slumping over, running past Gakuganji to check on him. Gakuganji asks why he doesn’t intend to fight him, to which he explains that pandas aren’t bound to fight, and he was never on bad terms with Yaga; he is just the knife to kill him. The anguished Panda only tells him to remember that even pandas can cry, before wailing at the death of his creator.

Later on Yuji and Megumi prepare to go find Kinji Hakari after recieving information from Tengen regarding that Hakari runs an illegel fighting club consisting of sorcerers and non-sorcerers as the spectators inside an abandoned parking garage in Tochigi Prefecture .
