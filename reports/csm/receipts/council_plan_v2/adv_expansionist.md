# Expansionist — council_plan_v2

The plan is priced as a campaign and sells it as a product. Three
undervalued upsides.

**R-5 is a product asset, not a campaign item.** `router.py:55`:
`MODES=("roster","qa","narrative")` — shared, series-agnostic. The 4th
`relations` mode lands once and serves every corpus with a fact graph, on
every door (HTTP `/context`, CLI, MCP). CSM's frozen bank proves the gap:
`CSM1B_RELATIONS_BANK.md`, 11 questions, `expect_mode: relations`, 0/11
complete today, all mis-routed to roster. The bank builder
(`build_relation_bank.py`, `--series`, structure-driven) measures CSM as
the best corpus (14 named subjects) and OPM partial (3). But the mode is
graph-driven: CSM's 11,261 cross-page edges (96.7 % cross-document) are
the fuel. The plan books R-5 at ~1 day of CSM cost; it amortizes 8×.

**The census factory is the real deliverable — P6 gets a 7× runbook.**
CSM-6 parameterized `census_dimensions.py` / `placement_audit.py` /
`census_doors.py` on `--series` (JJK byte-identical proof), CSM's frozen
sets are on disk, T0 pilot 843/843. So post-CSM-100 %, the marginal cost
of D1–D5 for the other seven corpora = crawl + index + ~9-min GPU census
+ red-class-only extraction. CSM's 51 GPU-h is the upper bound on the
most expensive corpus. P2.5's declaration is the 2nd instance of the
`DONE_DECLARATION.md` template — P6 becomes a 7× runbook, every corpus
cheaper than the CSM that paid for the factory.

**P2.4 is the differentiator, nearly free.** One `script-gen` + `verify`
is already in the plan. If verify-clean, the claim moves from "JJK works"
to "the engine works": eight corpora, one annotation contract, every line
traceable to a chapter. Adjacent upside: the 16,226-point JJK transcript
pattern (separate `qdrant-transcripts` collection, idempotent embed) —
`transcripts.py` says "JJK only, for now." The three-layer contract's
`[E n mm:ss]` timestamps need a transcript layer; CSM caption
availability is unverified — if the wiki has it, one import + embed leg
gives CSM scripts verbatim-timestamp quotes.
