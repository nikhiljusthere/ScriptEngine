## Where the report is wrong or mislabelled

1. **"section p50 3570 (corrected from 3573)" — the correction is wrong, and the
   stat is mislabelled.** It is a *per-event-row* statistic (each row weighted by
   the length of the section it came from): the two middle values are 3,567 and
   3,573, so 3,570 is the even-count median and 3,573 the upper median — neither
   was an error. The real mistake is the label: "**970 sections over 5,000**" is
   970 *rows*. Distinct sections: 144 of the read sections and 205 of all
   extractable sections exceed 5,000 chars; the extractable-section median is
   656 chars.
2. **"read 838 (22.0%)" + "never 2,965" do not sum to 3,801.** 838 distinct
   `section_id`s hold rows, but 2 of them are below the 20-word floor; 836
   extractable sections were read. (2,965 is right.)
3. **"119 rows (4.3%) touch ambiguous aliases" — 114 by name.** The raw
   mentions are NULL on every JJK row (they predate `df0c1066`), so "touch" can
   only be measured on the resolved name: 114 rows (Noritoshi Kamo 57 end-hits,
   Rika 38, Ryu 26, Akari 14, Angel 1). The defensible misresolution count, 35
   (9 + 26), stands. Rika → node 765 (the cursed spirit) is a correct
   resolution, not luck.
4. **G4 counts `organizations` as a failure (736).** The plan's own G4 says
   "a PLACE or a GROUP", so the one organization-object row passes: 735 fail,
   79 pass. Trivial, but the gate must be defined before it is scored.
5. **G5 "fragment < 20 chars" contradicts the plan's G5 (≥ 12).** `quoted_in`
   already refuses under 12, so under the plan's definition G5 is 0. The
   20-char cut flags real events ("Bayer takes on Gojo", 19 chars). G5 is a soft
   count, not a drop.

## What the report missed (all verified)

1. **Stale rows survive a re-extraction.** `extract_events_series` never touches a
   page whose extraction *failed*: its old rows stay. MCU/Marvel shard 1 failed
   22 + 15 pages. "Discard, don't clean" therefore needs an explicit purge of every
   row whose `prompt_version` is not the new one, after the run, counted by
   cause. (All 508 JJK pages that hold rows are inside the `all` scope, so
   out-of-scope leftovers are 0 for JJK — only failures can leave stale rows.)
2. **The recall loss is in the resolver, not the verbs.** On shard 1 the
   production 27B's proposals were dropped as `unknown_participant` at **47%**
   (Marvel 6,955 / 14,821) and **50%** (MCU 5,739 / 11,518); verb drops were
   **1.4% / 1.0%**. P1 (constrained decoding) addresses the 1%.
3. **The Akari/Ryu misresolution is live in today's code**, not only in the old
   rows. `pick_candidate` returns the exact-title page before it looks at the
   page's own wikilinks, so bare "Akari" → the song, "Ryu" → the shikigami,
   even on a page that links only to Akari Nitta / Ryu Ishigori. A re-extraction
   without a fix reproduces the 35 rows. Bare "Gojo" is ambiguous between
   Satoru Gojo and the Sorcerer Clan page (its alias).
4. **Retrieval reads `events`.** `retrieve/narrative.py` reorders beats on the
   events' time axis and builds character beats from them (`:331`, `:453`), so
   re-extracting JJK can move `eval` and the span-recall probe. The four gates
   must be run before and after, and a moved gold is a finding to adjudicate,
   not an automatic regression or an automatic win.

## Sibling-wipe: real, narrower than stated

`_section_passage` sets `passage = ''` for every section row and `save_events`
deletes `WHERE arc_key = ? AND passage = ?`, so replacing a page's section rows
replaces *all* of them. But a page is always read whole under its scope
(`by_page[page]`), so this bites only when the same page is re-read under a
*narrower* scope with `--refresh` (e.g. `placement` after `all`). A consistent
`--scope all --refresh` run is unaffected. Recorded as a hazard, not fixed here.
