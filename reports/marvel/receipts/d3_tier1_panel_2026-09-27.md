## Rebuildability

The pin is offline-deterministic from the on-disk corpus (`series/` is
git-ignored; backups are the safety net). Any corpus change produces a
different pin — that is the design: the pin prevents sample churn *within*
a campaign leg (a re-index cannot swap the panel under a running sweep), not
across corpus updates.
