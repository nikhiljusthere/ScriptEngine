## Decision rules applied (same as SUMMARY.md, plus)
- Stopped / prevented / foiled / thwarted a named person or plot -> defeated (2026-10-14), other_verb copy removed.
- A passive outcome whose doer is the subject of the same sentence or the stated party of the same fight
  ("All these threats were stopped by the Human Torch", "battling Iron Man and being arrested in the process",
  "they uncovered ... they were captured", "the team on Titan was defeated") -> written for that doer. A passive
  whose doer is only implied by context stays for the owner (list below, A8).
- "outmatched", "no match for", "ended up failing and losing", "forfeit the battle and flee", "surrendered" ->
  stated loser of the fight -> defeated.
- escaped is added only where the text says the side fled / retreated / went into hiding / was driven away /
  got away; bare "repelled" / "fought off" get defeated only (as b1988, b11793).
- A wound with a named doer replaces the passive `X|wounded|-` fact (who did it vs who suffered it).
