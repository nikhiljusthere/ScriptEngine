## Decision rules applied (unchanged from SUMMARY.md)
Restraint/disarm/trap/possession that ends the encounter -> defeated; a pause or a rescue mid-fight -> fought/wounded
only. Stopped/thwarted/foiled/prevented plot or person -> defeated (replaces other_verb facts). Forced to retreat /
fled / fought off / drove off / "send him fleeing" -> defeated + escaped (A74). Wound always a fact; + defeated only
when nothing continues (Klaue). Surrender -> defeated. A stated winner ("declared the winner", "Following this
victory", "emerged victorious") -> defeated next to the fight. One act -> fought dropped (A145); a fight in its own
clause/sentence keeps fought. A wound row with a stated doer is written `Doer|wounded|Victim` (the doer-less
`Victim|wounded|-` it replaces is removed).

## New patterns (not yet in BIG_REVIEW_PATTERNS.md)
1. Doer-less wound facts (`Victim|wounded|-`) where the sentence names the doer ("Romanoff managed to injure Glaive",
   "Rumlow ... cut Carter's arm", "slashing him open" by Rahne, "Vulture had ... thrown a car at him"): b4656, b12610,
   b9379, b12794. A corpus scan of `X|wounded|-` facts whose quote contains "by <Name>" / a named subject would find
   the rest.
2. "Send him fleeing" / "who then fled" / "who then retreated" after a stated defeat: the defeated row is written but
   the beaten side's escaped row is not (b2046 Giganto, b3327 Mole Man, b9049 Enchantress/Executioner, b10288 Wyatt).
3. Stopped plots written as `met` (b9530 "stopped ... the Red Skull's attempt", b263 "stopping mobster Big Joe").
4. A team-up stop/defeat credited to one doer only: the partner named in the same clause is missing ("By teaming up
   with Spider-Man, Ms. Marvel was able to stop Minerva", "Bucky assisted Captain America in ... stopping the Red
   Skull's attempt", "Marko helped defeat the Sinister Syndicate" with Spider-Man and Sable in the battle,
   "She aided the group in defeating the Blue Bullet"): b3434/b6724, b3004, b6108, b2092, b1780.
5. Possession/mind control that ends the encounter written as fought (b4647 Demon Bear and Ghost Owl possess Bishop,
   b3145 Kilgrave stops Cage in his tracks) -> defeated.
6. Scanner noise in this band: most P1 "stop"/"trap"/"escape" cues sit in a purpose clause ("went to stop him", "to
   stop his rampage") or a capability section and need no change; P4 rows are almost all correct hedges.
