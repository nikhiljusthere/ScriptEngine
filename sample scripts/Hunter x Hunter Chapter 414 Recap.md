**Hunter x Hunter Chapter 414 Recap**

Chapter 414 titled “Comrades” just dropped and it has the entire fandom losing their minds for good reason. The title alone is basically a giant hint about what's coming, and trust me, it makes perfect sense by the time everything wraps up. So let's get straight into how this whole chapter plays out. 

With just five minutes left before the Special Martial Law is declared, Prince Benjamin's private soldier, Kanjidol, sneaks into Prince Luzurus' residential area. His mission is to quietly take out Luzurus' guards while they're fast asleep inside the guards' quarters, which he does with chilling precision. However one of them is still alive and tries defending himself before we cut away.

The scene then cuts to outside Luzurus' room, where his guards, Bharate and Rice, are standing watch. They spot Yushohi and Chamashi heading their way and quickly inform Luzurus that two of Benjamin's private soldiers are waiting outside his door. Luzurus immediately realizes what that probably means. The Special Martial Law is about to begin, and he figures Benjamin is getting ready to spin Halkenburg's death into whatever story benefits him the most. Maybe he'll call it a terrorist attack, or maybe he'll paint Halkenburg as the mastermind behind it all. Rather than fighting back, Luzurus orders all of his private soldiers not to resist under any circumstances. He doesn't want anyone on his side getting caught up in unnecessary bloodshed. Instead, he tells the Hunters assigned to protect him to stall Benjamin's soldiers, knowing they can't casually make a move against members of the Hunter Association. Before anyone barges into his room, he gives one last order: clear out every bit of the drugs hidden inside the master bedroom before Benjamin's men get the chance to search the place.

As one of the Hunter bodyguards heads off to round up the other Hunters and carry out Luzurus' orders, he walks into a shocking sight. One of his fellow guards is already lying lifeless on the floor, while Kanjidol stands nearby with a knife still in hand. When the Hunter confronts him, Kanjidol insists he was only defending himself, claiming the other guard attacked him first. However Ridge isn’t buying it and it seems they charge up their aura to fight.jjj

Back outside Luzurus’ room, Yushohi and Chamashi are getting impatient since nobody's answering the door. Yushohi accuses Luzurus' entire faction of committing treason against the royal military. In his head though, Yushohi's mind is running a mile a minute. He realizes the incoming military sweep from the Special Martial Law actually works in his favor. His whole ability revolves around using En alongside his Stinger Ball to mark and eliminate a target, but that setup is basically useless in a chaotic group fight between multiple Nen users. While his En is active, he's essentially wide open with no defense of his own. Letting anyone step inside his En is already a gamble on its own, and since he can't use Gyo while maintaining it, even something as simple as a pistol becomes a serious threat to him. Throw in a crowded situation where he can't tell who's a friend, who's a foe, or who's even a Nen user at all, and keeping his focus on En becomes an absolute mental workout. The wider he stretches his En, the higher his odds of accidentally bumping into another Nen user's ability, whether that's someone who auto-retaliates the moment they sense En or Gyo, or some unlucky pairing where one guy keeps watch with En while his partner fires off a ranged Emission attack from a distance. He even throws some blame at Kurapika for making his job harder, since Kurapika's been out here teaching random people Nen and basically flooding the field with more potential rivals. On top of all that, his ability only really shines when he can isolate and monitor a Prince somewhere private and controlled. But now that a full-on military detainment might happen anyway thanks to the martial law, he starts questioning whether his ability is even necessary anymore in this entire Succession Contest. Chamashi snaps him out of his own head, letting him know they're charging in together the second the declaration hits. Yushohi agrees without much hesitation, silently reminding himself exactly why he decided to team up with this guy in the first place. Chamashi then places his hand on Yushohi's shoulder and activates his ability, which goes by the name Lord of the Flies \- Muteking. It's a semi-coercive Manipulation type ability, and here's the deal with how it works. By keeping physical contact with a willing participant for a set amount of time, Chamashi can grant that person a state of full invincibility that lasts exactly as long as the contact does. Sounds amazing until you hit the fine print, because once that duration ends, the person gets locked into a forced Zetsu for a hundred times as long as the invincibility lasted. And the moment that Zetsu finally lifts, they take recoil damage from their own aura equal to a third of every single attack that got nullified during their invincible window. Oh, and this whole ability can't stop joint attacks pulled off by multiple Nen users working together. Yushohi confirms there's only one target within range of his En, and based on how much damage ends up getting blocked, he calmly predicts that he probably won't be walking away from this once his invincibility runs out.

The scene cuts over to Woble's room, where Queen Oito, Kurapika, Bill, and Shimanu are having a conversation. Shimanu drops a wild confession of her own, revealing that back when she changed the baby's diaper right after Kurapika and Oito collapsed, she noticed the baby had actually been swapped. But after staring at the child for a moment, she decided it honestly didn't matter anymore, and she made peace with the fact that her real mission is simply serving the queen and the prince, whoever that ends up being. She does voice some worry about what happens to the actual Prince Woble once the Succession Contest wraps up. Kurapika then lays out two major problems for Queen Oito to chew on. The first one involves the curse that Beyond Netero placed on the prince. If the trigger condition for becoming a target is officially participating in the Succession Contest, then the real 14th Prince might actually be in the clear. But if the trigger is simply having gone through the seed urn ceremony, then she could still be marked as a target even without stepping foot into the competition. Furthermore, unidentified Nen fluctuations were previously picked up near the baby currently posing as the 14th Prince on the ship, which raises a genuinely unsettling possibility that the curse might not even be aimed at the real prince at all, but instead at the baby who took her place. 

While there is a suggestion to extract information directly from Beyond, Kurapika points out the danger in that approach, since attempting to steal an ability and failing to meet its activation requirements could cost him his own life. Bill steps in with an alternative plan, proposing they use Longhi's contract abilities to extract information by dangling temporary amnesty in front of Beyond, or by offering to remove his restraints altogether. Kurapika questions why Bill, someone who technically still works under Beyond, would go this far to help. Bill's answer is straightforward. He no longer wants to be used as Beyond’s pawn. After learning everything Beyond has done — and everything he's planning to do — he has completely lost whatever loyalty and commitment he once had. His priorities have shifted toward protecting Queen Oito and Prince Woble, with the bigger goal of ending the entire Succession Contest if it's within his power. Bill believes the Hunter Association would probably support this plan because, at the end of the day, no Hunter wants to watch people taking each other out. The ones with the most to lose if the Succession Contest's Limitation and the Guardian Spirit Beasts fall apart are King Nasubi, Beyond, and the upper-ranked princes who are fully invested in claiming the throne. From Bill's perspective, the Association would have every reason to unite against Beyond's curse, weaken the Kakin monarchy's grip, and back the princes who haven't been consumed by the power struggle. As long as they frame it as rescuing the princes from being trapped by a curse, he believes the V5 would have no problem approving it. Even Beyond's temporary release isn't a dealbreaker either. They could simply let him out long enough to get the information they needed, then detain him again under a different charge afterward.

Oito, who's been listening this whole time, finally asks about the second problem Kurapika hasn't explained yet. He apologizes for the delay and reveals that the second issue is the simple fact that Oito herself has no idea where the real Prince Woble actually is. Oito confirms this is true, and Kurapika reasons that she was likely kept in the dark on purpose during the swap, purely to protect her from anyone trying to pry the information out of her. Since her location remains unknown, there's no way to prove the real 14th Prince isn't secretly still on the Black Whale 1 ship. No matching infant shows up anywhere in the official passenger records, but Kurapika clarifies that this alone doesn't confirm she's off the vessel either. From the perspective of the higher-ranking princes, she could very well be hiding somewhere on board, and since she already went through the seed urn ceremony, her eligibility for the contest might still be fully intact. Even if she somehow left the ship entirely, there's still no guarantee that leaving automatically strips away her eligibility. As long as she is not on the ship, the real 14th Prince essentially becomes a grey existence, someone whose location and status remain completely unclear within the context of the contest. Because the other princes can't definitively determine whether she needs to be dealt with, her priority as a target actually drops. Her mystery location basically works as an accidental shield, giving her a strange kind of invincibility purely through uncertainty. The problem is that none of this applies to Beyond's curse. If she does turn out to be the actual target, her unknown whereabouts make it impossible for anyone to step in and protect her or lift the curse in time.

With that laid out, Kurapika proposes two possible paths forward. The first option is figuring out exactly how Beyond's curse functions and identifying the biological child responsible for carrying it out, which conveniently ties back into the contract with Longhi. The second option is simply locating the real 14th Prince directly so she can be protected and have the curse exorcised. Kurapika decides to focus his efforts on the first method for now, though he also plans to quietly ask the Hunter Association to search for the missing prince at the same time, just as a backup plan in case things go badly. Before wrapping up, Kurapika asks Oito one more thing, wanting to know if she has any way to reach the real prince during an emergency, even without knowing her precise location. Bill is genuinely impressed that Kurapika doesn't blindly trust the answers his own ability gives him, and instead keeps questioning everything to dig for the actual truth.

Oito reveals that she had been living in fear this whole time. If it ever came to light that the real 14th Prince wasn't actually participating in the Succession Contest, her entire clan would be executed as felons who fled the Succession Contest. That's why she had been placing all her hopes on Prince Halkenburg becoming king. But with that possibility now gone, all she can do is hope Beyond's curse never reached her child. Kurapika tells Oito he can't expect her to blindly trust everyone in the Hunter Association. Even so, he promises with complete confidence that there are Hunters he trusts with his life, and he believes they'll do everything they can to protect the prince. Right as Kurapika finishes reassuring Oito, the story briefly cuts away to show Gon back on Whale Island and Killua hanging out in some town alongside Alluka. Just a quick glimpse, but an extremely meaningful one considering how long it's been since we've seen either of them doing anything at all in this current arc. It’s also really important to note that they were shown right after Kurapika mentioned hunters he fully trusts which would of course include them. Is this a possible tease that Killua and Gon might somehow get involved in this whole mess? I guess we’ll see, and let us know in the comments how they can get brought into the fold here.

Back with Oito, the Queen finally reveals her secret method for reaching the real 14th Prince in an emergency. Apparently she has a relative on her side of the family working at a post office over in the Yamat Kingdom, and by sending a letter with a specific code attached, they can disguise it as undeliverable mail. That trick lets the message reach the prince's side of things without ever showing up on any official delivery record, keeping the whole operation completely under the radar.

The chapter wraps up with a display showing exactly 49 days left until the Succession Contest reaches its end, putting extra emphasis on just how many allies are quietly backing Kurapika at this point in the story. With the Special Martial Law about to officially begin within minutes, the remaining Kakin Princes are all scrambling to figure out how they're supposed to survive a full military takeover, especially with Benjamin positioning himself above everyone else and clearly using the chaos to his advantage.

But if we're being real, the biggest highlight of this entire chapter has to be Gon and Killua finally popping back up after being completely missing in action for what feels like forever in this arc. These two have basically been absent since the story shifted its full focus onto Kakin politics, so even a single panel of them existing somewhere in the world hits different at this point. Seeing those two again immediately gets everyone wondering how they're going to fit back into everything happening on the Black Whale, especially now that the search for the real Prince Woble looks like it's about to become a much bigger priority. Are they about to get roped into protecting her once that search officially kicks off, or is the story setting up something even bigger for them down the line? That's honestly the question everyone's going to be buzzing about until the next chapter drops.
