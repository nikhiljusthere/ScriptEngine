**The Full Story of ONE PUNCH MAN Explained**

1. **Saitama Introduction Arc** (*Episode 1*)

A quiet and peaceful A-City shatters when a massive explosion wipes out everything in sight. At the center of the blast stands Vaccine Man, who takes to the skies and unleashes a barrage of energy blasts on the city. Buildings crumble, civilians are wiped out, and the destruction spirals out of control. The Hero Association quickly sends A-Class heroes Lightning Max and Smile Man to stop him, but they’re taken down almost instantly. In his apartment, Saitama watches the chaos on the news. He decides it’s time to step in. Just as Vaccine Man prepares to kill a young girl, Saitama arrives and saves her. The monster declares itself as Vaccine Man, born from humanity’s pollution, destined to wipe humans out. He mutates into a hulking form, but Saitama ends the fight immediately with a single punch. Furious at how easy it was, Saitama lets out a scream of frustration. Meanwhile, King wanders into the scene, only looking for a video game he dropped. He trips over what’s left of Vaccine Man’s body. By the time Hero Association reinforcements show up, they assume King was the one who defeated the monster. The story then shifts into a flashback, three years before Saitama became a hero. Back then, he was unemployed and crossed paths with Crablante, a man transformed into a crab monster after overeating crab. Crablante spares him but reveals he’s after a boy with a large, weird-looking chin. Not long after, Saitama meets the child, who admits he once doodled nipples on Crablante while the monster slept. When Crablante finds them, Saitama decides to defend the kid. He strangles the monster with his tie and rips out its eyestalk, killing it. What Saitama doesn’t know is that the boy is the grandson of Agoni, a wealthy multi-millionaire. Hearing what happened, Agoni is inspired to create the National Superhero Registry. Using his fortune, along with business support and donations, he establishes the Hero Association. Heroes are then organized into four classes — C, B, A, and S — with ranks in each class. Back in the present, Saitama recalls all this while shopping. Suddenly, the city is attacked by a colossal man named Marugori mutated through an experimental steroid called Biceps Brachii King, made by his older brother, a mad scientist. The brothers plan to conquer the world, but things go wrong when the scientist spots Saitama casually standing on his brother’s shoulder. The older brother panics and orders the giant to swat him. Instead, the giant accidentally strikes the wrong shoulder, killing his own brother. Enraged and heartbroken, the giant fights Saitama until the bald hero delivers another effortless punch, killing the giant and leveling B-City in the process. That night, Saitama returns home, only to be approached by Super Custom YO649Z Mk. II. This self-proclaimed car enthusiast turned himself into a monster obsessed with custom vehicles. He mocks Saitama’s bald head, and without hesitation, Saitama ends him in one punch. The next morning, Saitama wakes up in the middle of an invasion by the Subterranean People, who have already wiped out most of humanity. Unlike his usual battles, Saitama feels genuinely excited. He clashes with the invaders, cutting down hordes of them and preparing to face the Subterranean King. But right before the fight, he wakes up — it was only a dream. Stepping outside, he finds a real invasion taking place, but it’s nothing like what he imagined. The Subterraneans are weak, and after their king is instantly defeated, they surrender with a flag that reads, “Sorry About That.” 

2. **House of Evolution Arc** (*Episodes 2 – 3*)

Later, Saitama spends his day casually watering his cactus when the TV cuts in with urgent news: a massive mosquito swarm is moving straight toward Z-City. Annoyed but unfazed, he tries to kill a mosquito buzzing around his apartment, only to fail over and over. Meanwhile, in the abandoned parts of the city, chaos breaks out. A looter taking advantage of the empty streets becomes the swarm’s latest victim, drained of blood in seconds. The swarm isn’t acting on its own — it’s under the command of Mosquito Girl, a deadly mutant who thrives on consuming blood. A cyborg named Genos steps in to confront her. Unlike normal humans, he isn’t vulnerable to the mosquitoes, making him the perfect counter. The battle escalates quickly, with both of them trading blows so viciously that Genos loses an arm and Mosquito Girl loses a limb of her own. She then summons her swarm to drain blood from animals in the surrounding wilderness, consuming it all to transform into a stronger, more terrifying form. Powered up, she completely overwhelms Genos, burning through his systems and leaving him on the ropes. Just as she’s about to finish the fight, Saitama casually intervenes. With a single slap, he swats her out of the sky and ends the battle instantly. Stunned by what he’s seen, Genos immediately asks Saitama to take him on as his disciple. Saitama agrees, though without much enthusiasm. A few days later, Genos shows up at his apartment uninvited, determined to follow through. He launches into a long, drawn-out explanation about his past, detailing the destruction caused by the Mad Cyborg that destroyed his village and killed his family. Since then, he’s been searching for that cyborg to get revenge. The endless monologue drives Saitama to visible frustration. Elsewhere, in a secret laboratory, the mysterious House of Evolution observes Mosquito Girl’s defeat. The organization’s leader, Dr. Genus, decides Saitama is too interesting to ignore and orders his capture. Back in Z-City, Saitama and Genos’ conversation is interrupted when a messenger from the House of Evolution arrives. The intruder doesn’t last long, quickly destroyed by the pair. Soon after, Saitama gets caught in an underground trap while Genos faces off against Armored Gorilla, a combat cyborg sent by the organization. Saitama easily breaks free and wipes out the other enemies, leaving Armored Gorilla as the only survivor. Cornered and realizing his squad is gone, Armored Gorilla reluctantly explains what he knows about Dr. Genus. He reveals that Genus was once a brilliant young scientist with radical ideas about artificially advancing human evolution. His theories isolated him from the scientific community, but he pressed forward, eventually uncovering the secret to immortality. Using that knowledge, he cloned himself countless times, creating the army of subordinates working under him. As the story drags on, Saitama loses patience and demands that Armored Gorilla get to the point. Pressed further, the cyborg explains that Genus is specifically interested in Saitama’s body. Saitama misunderstands the intent, jumping to the wrong conclusion, until Genos clarifies that the scientist simply wants him for experimentation. With discount day at the supermarket fast approaching, Saitama decides they’ll deal with the House of Evolution right away. Before they leave, Genos asks if Armored Gorilla knows anything about the cyborg who destroyed towns four years earlier — the one responsible for his tragedy. Armored Gorilla, however, denies any knowledge. At the House of Evolution, preparations begin. The facility sets up traps to deal with the incoming threat, but as the battle draws closer, Dr. Genus reluctantly agrees to unleash his ultimate creation: Carnage Kabuto. When Saitama and Genos arrive, Genos wastes no time. He obliterates the entire above-ground portion of the facility with a single blast before heading underground. There, they come face to face with Carnage Kabuto, who immediately tests Genos. Despite Genos unleashing everything he has, his attacks don’t even scratch the monster. Then it’s Saitama’s turn. Carnage Kabuto senses something off about him and hesitates, unnerved by the sheer pressure he gives off. During their face-off, Saitama casually reveals his daily training routine: 100 push-ups, 100 sit-ups, 100 squats, and a 10-kilometer run, every single day, with no shortcuts. Genos is left in disbelief at the simplicity of it. Carnage Kabuto then unleashes his Carnage Mode, a transformation that multiplies his strength and speed many times over. He appears to dominate the fight, but Saitama isn’t worried about him at all. Instead, he suddenly realizes that discount day at the supermarket is today, not tomorrow. Annoyed at nearly missing it, he ends the battle instantly with a single punch, reducing Carnage Kabuto to nothing. Dr. Genus witnesses the entire thing. The overwhelming gap between Saitama and his own creations forces him to rethink everything. He abandons his dream of artificial evolution, realizing that real change must come from within himself. Saitama and Genos leave immediately, heading to the store to catch the discounts before they expire. Later, officials from the Hero Association investigate the ruins of the House of Evolution. They discuss the massive destruction and take note of Genos’ devastating capabilities.

3. **Paradise Group Arc** (*Episode 4*)

Meanwhile, a man named Hammerhead has a mission: he wants to tear down modern society’s grind of overworked, underpaid lives and replace it with his own idea of a utopia. He gathers a gang of like-minded followers, all bald like him, and begins making speeches in public. The problem is, no one on the street takes him seriously. His big announcement about targeting Zeniru, a wealthy man who owns a luxurious apartment complex, barely gets noticed. While this unfolds, Saitama is at home having a bizarre dream where he’s stuck playing rock-paper-scissors against someone with a booger on their finger. He wakes up only to discover something that really bothers him — Hammerhead’s group, called Paradisers, is giving bald men a bad reputation, making people think being bald automatically means you’re dangerous. Annoyed, he storms out to put an end to it. Zeniru, aware that Hammerhead has set his sights on him, decides not to take chances. He hires his own personal bodyguard, a skilled and deadly ninja named Speed-o’-Sound Sonic. Sonic intercepts Hammerhead’s bald gang on their way to Zeniru’s mansion. Without hesitation, he warns them that those who fight him rarely survive. Within moments, he proves it by slicing through nearly the entire group, decapitating them with ease. Elsewhere, Saitama is running into his own problem — civilians mistake him for one of the terrorists simply because he’s bald. Meanwhile, the battle between Sonic and Hammerhead reaches its end, leaving only the two of them alive. Hammerhead barely escapes with his life and flees into the forest, where he crosses paths with Saitama. Hammerhead powers up his combat suit, ready to fight, but it doesn’t matter. Saitama throws one punch and shatters the entire suit, leaving Hammerhead vulnerable. Instead of finishing him, Saitama lets him run off, naked and humiliated, almost pitying him for his failed cause. Not long after, Sonic arrives. He mistakes Saitama for one of Hammerhead’s men and immediately attacks. Saitama blocks his strikes and tries to explain, but Sonic isn’t satisfied. Since his attacks were stopped, he now feels obligated to defeat Saitama. Their brief clash ends in an awkward moment when Saitama accidentally lands a punch to Sonic’s crotch. Sonic crumbles in pain but swears revenge, promising to train until he can defeat Saitama. Back at his apartment, Saitama tells Genos to leave him alone, insisting he has more important issues to think about. His concern isn’t another villain — it’s the fact that nobody knows who he is. Despite defeating countless enemies, his achievements have gone unnoticed. Genos suggests a solution: sign up for the official Hero Registry. Together, they go online and apply, which means they’ll need to pass a formal exam to be recognized as licensed heroes.

4. **National Superhero Registry Arc** (*Episodes 5 – 6*)

Saitama and Genos finally take the step to become registered heroes, but to get in, they have to pass both a physical and a written test. When it comes to the physical part, Saitama wipes out the competition without breaking a sweat. No one else even comes close to his results. The written exam, though, isn’t as easy for him. He scrapes by with the bare minimum, passing by just a single point. Because of that, he gets assigned to C-Class, the lowest rank. Genos, on the other hand, aces everything perfectly. His flawless score earns him the highest spot right away — S-Class. Out of all the applicants, only Saitama and Genos land in a class instructed by Sneck, an A-Class hero tasked with training recruits. Sneck carries himself with confidence, but it quickly becomes obvious that Saitama and Genos are far above his level. During class, Saitama zones out, which annoys Sneck enough to challenge him. The result backfires completely, and Sneck embarrasses himself in front of everyone. Later that day, Genos asks Saitama for a sparring match. With his latest upgrades, Genos wants to push his limits and maybe uncover what Saitama’s true secret is. He doesn’t believe the simple training routine Saitama mentioned before could really be the whole answer. The fight begins with Genos giving it everything he has, while Saitama casually avoids and holds back. Genos insists that Saitama fight seriously, and when Saitama finally does, the battle ends instantly without him even landing a blow. Genos is left stunned, realizing that Saitama is operating on a level he can’t reach through training alone. Afterward, the two head to an udon shop. They end up in an eating contest. Genos easily finishes his meal, while Saitama nearly collapses from the effort. While they’re there, Amai Mask, the Rank 1 hero of A-Class, suddenly appears and asks to speak with Genos outside. Genos assumes Amai Mask is there to challenge him and gets ready for a fight, but that’s not the case. Amai Mask simply congratulates Genos on joining the hero ranks and welcomes him, leaving without any conflict. The encounter reignites Genos’s determination to grow stronger, and he decides he needs Saitama’s mentorship more than ever. The very next day, Genos shows up at Saitama’s apartment with all of his belongings in tow. He asks if he can move in. Saitama immediately refuses, but when Genos offers to pay rent, his mind changes fast. From then on, the two begin living together. Saitama spends his time lounging and reading manga while Genos meticulously studies his “training methods,” writing down every little detail and gesture. Saitama grows increasingly frustrated, realizing that keeping up the act of being a master is going to be a hassle. During one of their conversations, Genos brings up a problem. He explains that heroes who go a week without activity are removed from the Hero Registry, especially in C-Class where the competition is heavy. Saitama, who has already been inactive for six days, panics. He immediately decides to go on patrol before time runs out. Genos offers to tag along, but Saitama brushes him off, telling him to train in patience. In reality, Saitama has no idea how to properly teach and just blurts out random advice. For the next two days, Saitama patrols without finding much to do. By the last day, he’s nervous about getting struck off the registry. That’s when Sonic shows up, determined to settle the rivalry he insists exists between them. Saitama, uninterested, brushes him off. This only enrages Sonic, who attacks, only for Saitama to casually bite his sword in half. The sight shocks Sonic, who grows nervous as Saitama threatens to finally hit him. At the same time, Tanktop Tiger, another C-class hero, interrupts. He accuses Saitama of acting crazy in public while trying to gain attention for himself. Tiger uses the crowd’s cheers as a way to boost his own image, but before anything escalates further, Sonic rains down a barrage of explosive shuriken. Panic erupts in the streets as the crowd scatters. Realizing Sonic is behind the chaos, Saitama finally sees this as the “hero work” he needs. He takes Sonic down effortlessly, ending the fight in an instant and sending the ninja assassin to jail. Later that day, in the quieter outskirts of Z-City, a mysterious monster begins to terrify the few residents still living there. At Hero Association HQ, S-class Rank 2 Tatsumaki scolds the staff for not sending her to deal with it, since A-Class heroes Golden Ball and Spring Mustachio were dispatched instead. Meanwhile, Golden Ball and Mustachio patrol the streets of Z-City. They recall rumors about a dangerous monster living there but find nothing at first. Eventually, they stumble across evidence of the creature and soon come face-to-face with Kombu Infinity. Kombu is also interested in rumors of powerful monsters in the area, but instead of allies, he finds prey. He makes short work of both heroes, swatting aside their best attacks with ease. Realizing they’re outmatched, Mustachio calls for reinforcements but is defeated before help can arrive. Kombu, disappointed with how weak his opponents were, decides to create the very rumor he came seeking, determined to spread fear of a dangerous monster in Z-City. Not long after, Saitama returns home from a market sale. Kombu notices him and grumbles about failing to get kombu stalks for soup before attacking. By the time Genos returns home, the fight is already over — Saitama is calmly cooking kombu soup in the kitchen, using the defeated monster as an ingredient.

5. **Giant Meteor Arc** (*Episode 7*)

Meanwhile, scientists and astronomers track a meteor passing over Earth when its course suddenly shifts toward Z-City. A citywide alert goes out warning of imminent impact. Meanwhile, Saitama and Genos are at home talking about their hero rankings. Saitama has jumped a few spots in C-Class from 388th to 345th, while Genos hasn’t moved in rank but is gaining popularity fast. Their conversation is cut short when the Hero Association sends an emergency call to all S-Class heroes. Only a few respond, with Bang and Genos rushing to the scene. Bang tells Genos that evacuation orders have already been issued. He advises the young hero to pull out while there’s still time, but Genos refuses to abandon the city. He thinks of Saitama living there and pushes forward, determined to fight. Bang admires his resolve but warns that this meteor will destroy not just Z-City but surrounding areas as well. Genos leaps across rooftops toward the impact site and sees civilians frozen in despair. He realizes evacuation is impossible now, reinforcing his decision to stay. Just then, Metal Knight arrives to test out new weapons against the meteor. He shows no interest in teamwork, focusing only on his personal data collection. His weapons fail to make a dent, and Genos decides to step up. He launches everything he has at the meteor, but doubts begin to cloud his focus. Bang arrives to steady his resolve, reminding him not to falter. Despite his best efforts, Genos can’t stop the meteor. In the end, Saitama arrives and shatters it in one punch. The meteor is destroyed, but fragments rain down across Z-City, leveling neighborhoods and leaving countless civilians homeless. The next day, Saitama finds out his rank skyrocketed from 345th place to 5th in the C-Class roster while Genos has been raised from 17th to 16th of the S-class because of his part in the mission. Later, while patrolling the damaged city, Saitama faces a crowd stirred up by Tanktop Tiger and his brother Tanktop Black Hole. They blame him for the destruction left by the meteor, ignoring the fact that his intervention saved them all. The brothers rally the mob against him, trying to turn Saitama into a scapegoat. Instead of defending himself, Saitama flips the situation. He openly tells them he’s not a hero for their praise or approval but because he enjoys it. He declares they can hate him all they want because their opinion doesn’t matter. Bang, watching from the sidelines, notices how Saitama calmly takes control of the situation, shutting down the mob’s anger with nothing but his conviction. Genos arrives and tells his master to ignore and just leave the angry crowd. He reminds his master that he’s still an incredible hero, no matter what others say, and assures him that he’ll continue to follow him even without the public’s recognition. Saitama downplays the compliment, but as he turns away, a quiet, satisfied smile slips across his face.

6. **Sea Monster Arc** (*Episodes 8 – 9*)

Later, a Seafolk messenger suddenly shows up in J-City, attacking and declaring that the Seafolk are coming to wipe out humanity. On his way back from shopping, Saitama casually stops by and erases the messenger with one punch. When the C-class Rank 1 Mumen Rider arrives, all that’s left is the mangled body of the creature and a crowd buzzing about Saitama. Curious, Mumen Rider looks him up online and sees that while Saitama is clearly powerful, people are still split on whether he’s genuine or a fraud. At the coast, huge Seafolk monsters begin emerging from the ocean and threaten J-City. A-Class hero Stinger steps up with his Bamboo Shoot weapon to hold them back. Meanwhile, Genos is drying dishes with his built-in gear while Saitama watches a news report on Stinger’s fight. The two decide to head out. Stinger wipes out the last four Seafolk with his Gigantic Drill Stinger Quadruple Thrust, but then the leader of the Seafolk, the Deep Sea King, appears and crushes him. Saitama, Genos, and Mumen Rider all set out toward the fight, and Genos splits off to scout ahead. Lightning Max takes on the Sea King but is quickly defeated. S-class hero Rank 17 Puri-Puri Prisoner, who escaped from prison to help, joins in but also loses to the monster. Sonic, having escaped alongside Puri-Puri Prisoner, battles the Sea King to a stalemate, but the rain transforms the monster into his true form, forcing Sonic to retreat. On the way, Saitama and Mumen Rider run into each other. Saitama goes off when he spots Sonic running naked through the rain, while Mumen Rider gets a call from the Hero Association about the Sea King’s location. He heads there, only to find the monster breaking into an emergency shelter, defeating heroes inside like Sneck, and tearing through defenders. When Saitama returns to their meeting spot, all he finds is a discarded cell phone. As the Association debates whether to send Saitama in, Genos begins his fight with the Deep Sea King. He nearly defeats him with a massive attack but leaves himself open and loses an arm. Even so, he keeps the crowd safe, telling them to escape while he distracts the monster. Just when things seem under control, the Sea King launches acid at a little girl. Genos shields her and takes the full hit, which melts most of his body and nearly kills him. Before the monster can finish him off, Mumen Rider charges in. He’s completely outmatched but refuses to back down. The people cheer him on, moved by his determination, but it’s not enough — he collapses after a final blow. Just as he’s about to hit the ground, Saitama arrives, catching him. With the Association’s order for backup in hand, he steps forward and destroys the Deep Sea King with a single punch. The crowd erupts in praise. Back at their apartment, Genos and Saitama get fan mail. Genos receives praise, while Saitama gets a letter insulting him and calling him a cheat. The story then jumps back to right after the battle, when people were cheering Saitama’s victory. One man ruins the mood by criticizing the fallen heroes, claiming they must have been frauds if a C-Class hero finished the monster in one punch. To protect their reputations, Saitama laughs it off, saying he was just lucky and the others had weakened the Sea King before he got there. He tells the crowd to give credit to the fallen instead. The people call him a fraud and direct their thanks toward the defeated heroes. Back in the present, Saitama realizes he has more letters. One simply says “Thank You.” The next is from the Association, announcing his promotion to B-Class Rank 101\. He accepts. At headquarters, Amai Mask hears of Saitama’s promotion. At first he’s dismissive, blaming the higher-ranked heroes for their failures against the Sea King. But when he learns Saitama was the one who actually beat the monster, his attitude changes. As Saitama is walking home at night after his promotion, he runs into Mumen Rider at a roadside oden stand. Mumen Rider admits he was the one who sent the thank-you letter and later pays for Saitama’s meal.

7. **Alien Conquerors Arc** (Episodes 10 – 12\)

Meanwhile, the Ancient King wakes up after 300 million years of slumber, waiting for the other clan kings to fall before making his move. Earth’s military launches missiles at him, but none of them can pierce his skin. The Hero Association calls in Tatsumaki, and after a quick standoff, she pulls a meteor down from space and crushes him. At Bang’s dojo, Bang explains to Saitama and Genos how his top student Garou snapped and defeated all his other disciples. Before the conversation can continue, a staff member from the Hero Association arrives to announce an emergency. All S-Class heroes are being summoned. Bang invites Saitama to come along. At headquarters, Saitama meets the rest of the S-Class lineup: Atomic Samurai, Puri-Puri Prisoner, Metal Bat, Tanktop Master, Flashy Flash, Watchdog Man, Superalloy Darkshine, Pig God, Drive Knight, Zombieman, King, and Child Emperor. Association executive Sitch opens the meeting by warning that even with their strength, the situation they face could kill them. He then explains that Madam Shibabawa, the great seer whose predictions have always been accurate, recently died. She choked on a cough pill during a coughing fit brought on by a vision. Before she passed, she left behind her final prophecy: The Earth is in trouble. The heroes are told to prepare for a catastrophe within six months. Before the meeting can wrap up, HQ is attacked by the Skyfolk led by the Sky King. Melzargard, a member of the Dark Matter Thieves, appears and wipes them out. Above, an alien ship unleashes an attack that obliterates A-City, killing countless people. On the ground, Melzargard closes in on civilians, but Iaian of Atomic Samurai’s dojo intervenes. He loses his left arm before Atomic Samurai arrives to save him. Melzargard regenerates from their attacks without effort, and the fight drags on. Metal Bat, Bang, and Puri-Puri Prisoner join in. Puri-Puri recalls the Deep Sea King’s words and transforms into his Dark Angel form, hammering Melzargard with punches. Meanwhile, other S-Class heroes argue over how to handle the massive ship. Genos points out that Saitama has already boarded it and is tearing through the aliens on his own, confident that nothing evil can withstand him. Inside the ship, Saitama crushes Groribas with one punch and keeps moving forward, looking for the boss. On the throne, Boros waits. Geryuganshoop fumes over Groribas’ defeat, worrying that only he and Melzargard remain. Boros dismisses his concerns, trusting the ship’s protective systems. Back below, the heroes keep battering Melzargard despite his constant regeneration. Iaian urges them to retreat and find another strategy, but Atomic Samurai, Bang, Puri-Puri, and Metal Bat keep pressing forward anyway. While Melzargard’s attention is split with his telepathic link to Geryuganshoop, Metal Bat smashes a marble hidden inside his body, permanently destroying one of his forms. Elsewhere in the ship, Saitama stumbles upon Geryuganshoop. The alien has overwhelming telekinesis, bending everything around him, but Saitama casually takes him down with a pebble toss. At the same time, Dark Matter’s artillery crew prepares a bombardment. Child Emperor and Genos coordinate on the ground, while Sitch and Bushos stay linked to the heroes. Mumen Rider evacuates survivors. As Melzargard continues his rampage, the bombardment is fired. Tatsumaki arrives just in time, freezes the shells in midair, insults the other heroes for their recklessness, and hurls the projectiles back at the ship. Stinger and Lightning Max also show up to offer help. Bang manages to destroy another of Melzargard’s marbles before being struck and smashed into a building, knocked unconscious. Seeing Bang’s condition enrages Atomic Samurai, who renews his assault. Inside the ship, Saitama finally confronts Boros. The alien scans him, confirming he is Earth’s strongest warrior. He explains that a prophecy from his people’s seer foretold this encounter. Saitama interrupts by punching him. The strike shatters Boros’ armor, which was used to contain his overwhelming energy. Boros thanks Saitama for releasing him as his body shifts into his true form. Their battle rages across the throne room and spills onto the ship’s surface. Saitama effortlessly blocks Boros’ blows and counters with strikes of his own, blowing off Boros’ arm. Boros regenerates instantly, boasting of his abilities. He then unleashes his ultimate transformation, Meteoric Burst, and drives Saitama into an all-out high-speed assault. His attacks are so fast and destructive that he tears through his own ship, killing parts of his crew. Finally, Boros knees Saitama all the way to the moon. Unfazed, Saitama leaps back from the moon to Earth in seconds, crashing down onto the ship. Boros tries to overwhelm him again, but Saitama retaliates with Consecutive Normal Punches, blasting Boros’ body apart. Even then, Boros regenerates one final time and prepares his ultimate move, Collapsing Star Roaring Cannon, which could wipe out the planet. Saitama answers with his Serious Punch, completely neutralizing the blast and vaporizing Boros. The impact alone clears the clouds across the globe. Dying, Boros admits Saitama never fought him seriously and that the prophecy he believed in was false. The ship collapses onto the ruins of A-City. Afterward, Drive Knight warns Genos that Metal Knight is his enemy before leaving. Amai Mask appears, blaming the S-Class heroes for the devastation, nearly sparking a fight. Metal Knight interrupts to salvage alien tech from the wreck. Superalloy Darkshine discovers alien survivors, but Amai Mask immediately executes them, leaving Genos disturbed by how much he resembles his own past self. Saitama emerges from the ship to find Tatsumaki raging. Genos mocks her as a brat, and she slams him into a rock before Bang stops them from escalating further. Metal Knight’s salvaged parts are later used to rebuild the Hero Association HQ, now standing where A-City once was. A- and S-Class heroes are given places to live in the new headquarters. In this anime-exclusive scene, a Dragon-level threat named Pluton appears in Z-City and devastates everything in his path. As always, Saitama ends it with one punch, lamenting that his life is back to being filled with instant victories.

8. **King Arc** (Episode 13\)

A few days later, Saitama and Genos are heading back from the grocery store when they run into King, the S-Class Rank 7 hero. At the same time, Tongue Stretcher is causing chaos in the city. But the second he hears King’s Engine — that ominous heartbeat sound — he immediately surrenders without a fight. Not long after, a robot called G4 shows up and challenges King to a fight. King tries to dodge the situation by saying he needs to use the bathroom first, claiming that fighting at “full power” requires the right conditions. G4 accepts this excuse but warns that if King doesn’t return in ten minutes, it’ll start killing civilians nearby. King is overwhelmed with fear because the truth is, he isn’t the “strongest human alive” that everyone believes him to be. He’s just an ordinary otaku who always happened to be near battle sites where monsters were taken down. Thanks to those coincidences, he was wrongly credited for the victories, and eventually, he started going along with the reputation because of the fame and recognition it brought. While Genos jumps in and engages G4, King runs back to his apartment and boots up his favorite dating sim, Heartbeart Hottie Sisters 2\. Saitama follows him, catching him in the middle of his gaming session. Embarrassed, King quickly switches to another game, but Saitama doesn’t mind and even joins in. Their session is cut short when a monster, Giant Crow, attacks King’s apartment. Terrified, King panics, wets himself, and accidentally exposes his big secret — that he’s not a real hero. Saitama quickly kills the bird monster, and in that moment, King realizes something important. It was actually Saitama who had saved him years ago from Octopus Claw Man, the monster that left him with the scar on his eye. Afterwards, Saitama casually stays to play more video games with King, while Genos receives upgrades from Dr. Kuseno, using parts salvaged from G4 after defeating it. Meanwhile, the Hero Association takes drastic action. Sitch announces a controversial plan to recruit criminals to fight against the growing number of monsters and in preparation for the prophecy left behind by Shibabawa. With heroes spread too thin, the Association sees no other choice. During the meeting organized by Sitch, Sonic walks out after realizing Saitama isn’t there — since the only reason he attended was to challenge him.
