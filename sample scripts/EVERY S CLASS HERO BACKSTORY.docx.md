**EVERY S CLASS HERO BACKSTORY** 

**Puri-Puri Prisoner**

Puri-Puri Prisoner is a large, super-strong S-Class hero with a kind heart, but he is also known for his wild and uncontrollable behavior. He has a strong attraction toward handsome men and cannot stop himself from flirting or making advances, even during hero work. Because of this, he spends most of his time in a special prison for dangerous criminals, called Smelly Lid Prison, serving a 10,000-year sentence.

His real reason for being in prison is that he was arrested during his hero activities for harassing and possibly assaulting multiple men. Despite this, he remains a hero at heart. Whenever a villain threatens innocent people, Puri-Puri Prisoner escapes from jail to fight and protect others. His spirit never breaks, even in the face of strong enemies, and he always helps his fellow heroes when they need support. After completing his missions, he returns to prison, adding more time to his sentence. The jail staff usually ignore his escapes because they help deal with dangerous threats, but they still increase his time in prison.

Puri-Puri Prisoner refers to himself as "Ore," a masculine form of "I," but sometimes he speaks in a more girly or cute tone. He calls any man he finds attractive "boy" and makes no effort to hide his interest in them. Despite his reputation, he deeply cares about other heroes and treats them with kindness. For example, he calls Child Emperor “kyun,” which means "cute," adding a playful suffix. He even has a boyfriend and loves receiving gifts from him.

Among the other prisoners in Smelly Lid Prison, some are afraid of his actions, suspecting he might kiss them or worse, but many respect and like him, seeing him as their leader. He acts like a big brother, ensuring peace and order among inmates and making sure they get along. His position as the boss of the prison earns him both fear and respect. He is especially protective of innocent boys and becomes upset when they are harmed or when the prison is too noisy.

His personal interests include watching handsome men on TV, enjoying ballet performances, practicing flower arrangement, and eating stew. He also enjoys his boyfriend’s gifts and values his body highly. Puri-Puri Prisoner is often seen as a unique mix of strength and affection.

He ranks 17th among the S-Class heroes, and at one point, his hero name was different. The exact original name and the timing of the change remain unknown. However, it's likely that the “Prisoner” part of his name reflects his current situation, possibly chosen after his arrest. His hero name symbolizes his role as both a hero and an inmate.

Puri-Puri Prisoner first ended up in jail after capturing a wanted man who became his type. Instead of returning to hero society, he chose to remain in prison with that man. This decision led to his long sentence. His time in prison is not just punishment; it reflects his belief that punishing criminal men helps both himself and society.

Despite spending much of his time in prison, he is a powerful figure. The creator of One Punch Man mentioned that Puri-Puri Prisoner is strong enough to defeat the Giant Crow that once crashed into King’s home. His presence is so well-known that he appeared in a poster during Sneck's seminar.

He often skips prison labor, likely due to his responsibilities as a hero.

**Tanktop Master**

Tanktop Master is an S-Class hero ranked 12th and is the leader of the Tank Topper Army, a group of heroes who believe wearing tank tops symbolizes true strength. His real name is not well-known, but his hero name comes from his mastery of wearing and making tank tops that supposedly enhance his power.

He wears a dull blue tank top both during training and in battle. He claims the tightness of the tank top boosts his strength, though whether this is true remains unconfirmed. The tank top is so tight that during one battle, it acted like a defibrillator by restarting his heart when Bang’s grip temporarily stopped it.

Tanktop Master’s main goal is to be “the man who looks best in a tank top.” He takes pride in his appearance and abilities, and he makes his own tank tops, which are sold as official Hero Association merchandise. This reflects his confidence in his design and personal style.

He leads the Tank Toppers, a group of heroes who all wear tank tops and share his belief in their symbolic and practical power. Their uniform choice emphasizes physical strength and solidarity. Tanktop Master is strict about fitness and health. He enjoys chicken tenderloin and protein supplements but dislikes cholesterol, carbohydrates, psychic powers, and machines.

His backstory remains mostly unknown. The origin of his name stems from his expertise in maximizing the tank top’s performance. There is no clear record of what motivated him to become a hero or how he rose to the S-Class rank, but his appearance, self-made clothing, and strict diet are part of his identity.

In popular culture, the 2017 One Punch Man Halloween cover portrayed him wearing a hockey mask and wielding a chainsaw, a reference to slasher movie killers like Jason Voorhees and Leatherface. Some suggest it also plays on his appearance resembling rapper Eminem, who once showed up at a concert with a mask and chainsaw, though this is purely thematic.

**Metal Bat** 

Bad, known by his hero name Metal Bat, is a professional hero who reached S-Class Rank 15 in the Hero Association. He is now part of the Neo Heroes as a Neo Leader. He uses a metal bat as his primary weapon.

Metal Bat’s real name is Bad. His career started in the Hero Association as a C-Class hero, ranked 223rd. He fought many demon-level threats and consistently performed well in battles. One year before the current story, he was promoted from C-Class to S-Class due to his combat achievements.

When he became an S-Class hero, the Hero Association offered to provide any request he wished, including specialized weapons. Metal Bat requested a metal bat that would never break. His reason was that normal metal bats he used earlier would often break during fights. The Hero Association provided him with a custom-made unbreakable metal bat, which became his signature weapon.

Metal Bat has the lowest intelligence rating among all S-Class heroes, with a stat of 3\. His combat style is simple and relies mainly on brute force and physical strength. He does not use complex gadgets or hidden mechanisms in his weapon. The metal bat is special solely because it is unbreakable.

He is known for his delinquent appearance and behavior. His outfit is based on the stereotypical Japanese delinquent style. He is also known for cussing at people and being a problem student. Despite this, he shows consideration toward others, especially his younger sister. He enjoys attending her piano recitals. He dislikes inconsiderate people, loud mouths, and chores.

Metal Bat has a pet cat he cares about and shows a strong sense of responsibility toward people connected to the Hero Association. For example, when asked to babysit Narinki and his son, he followed restaurant rules carefully and acted responsibly, even during a threat from Senior Centipede.

During the battle against the Monster Association, Metal Bat “resigned” from the Hero Association and joined the Neo Heroes. In the Neo Heroes, he took the role of Sekingar’s bodyguard, as requested by Sitch. He accepted the offer because the Neo Heroes promised better support for his home, his cats, and his sister.

Murata confirmed that Bad’s name is a pun on "Bat," referring to his weapon. Murata also stated that Garou would have probably died from a surprise attack by Metal Bat if his sister had not intervened.

His fighting style is direct and aggressive. He focuses on attacking with full force using his metal bat. There are no hidden tricks in his weapon, no advanced technology, or gadgets. The bat is purely for hitting hard and is custom-built to be unbreakable.

His low intelligence stat indicates that his strength does not come from strategy but from raw power and determination. He does not plan complex maneuvers or use technical skills during fights.

**Genos**

Genos is a 19-year-old cyborg and an S-Class hero ranked 11th in the Hero Association. He is the disciple of Saitama and fights for justice. Genos is driven by a strong desire to become more powerful, especially to face the rogue cyborg who destroyed his home and family.

When Genos was 15, a runaway cyborg attacked his hometown, completely destroying it. His family was killed, and he was left severely injured. At that time, Dr. Kuseno, a genius inventor tracking the rampaging cyborg, arrived at the scene. Genos begged Dr. Kuseno to transform him into a cyborg so he could survive and seek revenge. The doctor agreed, and Genos became a powerful cyborg determined to fight evil and track down the destructive cyborg that ruined his life.

After becoming a cyborg, Genos spent four years hunting evil and fighting monsters. He often told Saitama about his battles, but Saitama, known for his impatience, asked him to summarize it in 20 words or less. The main point is that Genos has not yet defeated the rogue cyborg and continues to grow stronger for that future battle.

Genos is driven by a strong sense of justice mixed with youthfulness and responsibility. His traumatic past forced him to abandon his humanity and live as a cyborg. The kanji used in his name means “genocide” but can also mean “to fight fiercely to the end.” His name also references Ancient Greek, where “Genos” means a “clan” or “family,” highlighting his connection to Dr. Kuseno, who became a father figure.

His hero name, "Demon Cyborg," was given by the Hero Association because of his aggressive and fearless attitude when fighting powerful monsters and disasters. Genos quickly rose in popularity, reaching the sixth position in public polls just a week after joining the Hero Association. He is also ranked among the top five most handsome heroes. His popularity continues, and he holds the fourth rank in official character polls.

Genos lives with Saitama and considers him his master. He pays high rent and helps with household chores while training to become stronger. Genos also keeps extremely detailed "training diaries," in which he records everything Saitama teaches him, including both important advice and trivial details. These include how to hold and read a manga, hang laundry, and reports on Saitama’s diet. While King is the only person who sees how mostly useless these notes are, Genos continues to believe in their value.

Despite being mostly mechanized, Genos retains his human brain and the ability to eat. He explains that Dr. Kuseno designed him this way so he could still enjoy simple human pleasures. His face design is based on his appearance when he was human at 19 years old. He has a built-in sleep mode and requires a high-pressure shower, similar to a car wash, for body maintenance. His sphere-shaped arm components are used for daily life, and when not in battle, he can attach specialized arms for housework. These include a built-in vegetable peeler, ports that blow warm air for drying dishes, and a drink bottle opener built into his shoulder.

Genos cuts off his sleeves because his shoulders are too large to fit into them, and he finds sleeves ugly and inconvenient for using his built-in weaponry. He also wears piercings, which he got after becoming a cyborg. Due to his mechanical body, he is too heavy to swim under normal circumstances.

His signature attack is the "Machine Gun Blow," possibly inspired by similar moves in fighting games like Street Fighter. In the anime, his eye has a Russian sniper scope reticle design, modeled after the Dragunov rifle’s PSO-1 sight.

Genos dislikes complicated electronics, people who look down on Saitama, carbohydrates, cholesterol, psychic powers, and machines. His hobbies include surfing the internet, reading, and writing in his training diary. He enjoys Saitama’s simple teachings and oiled sardines.

His design went through several iterations. Originally, Murata, the illustrator, tried pairing Genos with Kaneda’s jacket from "Akira" and referenced Tetsuo’s appearance but later found these ideas too over-the-top. In a 2017 Halloween illustration, Genos was portrayed as a chainsaw-wielding slasher figure, a nod to horror movie killers like Jason Voorhees and Leatherface, and possibly Eminem, whose appearance resembles Genos in some aspects.

ONE stated that Genos is among the most powerful of the S-Class heroes. He also mentioned that a fight between Genos and Sonic would likely end in a draw.

Genos has at least 1098 notebooks filled with notes on Saitama's teachings. He keeps these meticulously, believing every detail matters. His dedication to self-improvement is absolute, and he records everything, no matter how small, in hopes it will help him get stronger.

**Flashy Flash**

Flashy Flash is the S-Class Rank 10 professional hero of the Hero Association, known for his incredible speed and swordsmanship. His real name is unknown, but he chose the hero name himself. He was originally part of the 44th Graduation Class from the Ninja Village, a place infamous for its brutal training of ninja assassins. The 44th Class carried the nickname “The End,” as it was considered a failure by the village due to the mistakes in training.

Flashy Flash was sold by his parents to the Ninja Village at the age of five. The village operated in secrecy, located in an unknown city, and was designed to create the strongest assassins. The training there was exceptionally cruel. Days were artificially extended to last 72 hours, giving trainees only 6 hours of sleep. Food was tasteless, and all contact with the outside world was forbidden. Emotions and friendships were suppressed. The rules were harsh: upon graduation, any two graduates who met each other were ordered to fight to the death.

Flashy Flash was placed in Class 5, known as the "failing class," where he met Speed-o’-Sound Sonic. Despite the strict prohibition, the two formed a friendship. Sonic consistently helped Flashy Flash without expecting anything in return, which allowed Flashy Flash to reconnect with lost emotions caused by the harsh training. Their bond became a critical part of Flashy Flash’s life.

Throughout his time in the village, Flashy Flash pretended to be weak, choosing the most difficult training options to make himself stronger. His goal was not to merely survive but to gain enough power to destroy the village administration itself. By the age of sixteen, he had surpassed everyone else in power. In a decisive event known as the "End of the 44th Class Incident," Flashy Flash slaughtered all his classmates, instructors, and village staff. Sonic was the sole survivor, spared by Flashy Flash himself. To save Sonic, Flashy Flash secretly poisoned his meal, knowing that Sonic's strong immune system would leave him with only diarrhea, not death.

After obliterating the Ninja Village, Flashy Flash left, rejecting the cruel system. He then joined the Hero Association, using his incredible abilities to fight for justice. His aggressive approach and fearless attitude earned him the hero name "Demon Cyborg," which suited his fighting style.

Flashy Flash rarely shares his past, preferring to keep his personal history to himself. However, his actions speak louder than words. After joining the Hero Association, he was hunted by many assassins and dangerous criminals sent by the underworld to eliminate him. He successfully defeated all of them, showcasing his unmatched combat skills. His known defeated foes include Aburabozu, the organ trade king; Harold, a drug kingpin; Heart Collector Burigura; Phantom Thief Chimagusa; Devil Storm, an invincible pirate captain; and the high-ranking members of the Coalition of Assassins.

He is the first S-Class hero to single-handedly defeat a categorized Dragon-level Mysterious Being, Melzargard, a feat that other Dragon-level monsters required multiple S-Class heroes to tackle.

Despite his immense power, Flashy Flash holds the lowest known "Justice" rating of all S-Class heroes, rated at 5\. His personality is largely shaped by his upbringing, giving him a distant and cold demeanor, though he respects Sonic deeply. In the 2017 One Punch Man Halloween cover, he was illustrated as a kitsune—a Japanese nine-tailed fox spirit—referencing his feminine appearance and swift movements.

Murata once revealed that Sonic and Flashy Flash are “somewhat” related, later confirmed by official chapters showing their shared origin in the Ninja Village. Flashy Flash was taught swordsmanship by Sonic, forming a unique dynamic between the two.

**Watchdog Man**

Watchdog Man is the S-Class Rank 12 hero of the Hero Association. He is known for his dog-like persona and his mysterious nature. Before becoming S-Class, he was ranked 301st in the C-Class. His true identity and background remain largely unknown, and his personality is enigmatic. He rarely speaks, and his facial expression does not change, making it hard to read him, even in conversation.

Watchdog Man adopts dog-like behavior in nearly every aspect of his life. He sits, sleeps, and even eats like a dog. Most strikingly, he refuses to use utensils, smashing his face directly into his plate to eat. This behavior is both unhygienic and unsettling for those who witness it, but it reflects his deep commitment to his chosen persona.

His combat abilities are exceptional, particularly against monsters. Watchdog Man approaches every monster fight with the same savagery and intensity. He aims for a swift killing blow, never leaving monsters alive. However, he behaves differently when fighting humans. When confronted by a human adversary trying to kill him, he refrains from delivering fatal blows, instead incapacitating them.

Watchdog Man is assigned exclusively to Q-City, a location known for frequent monster attacks and disasters. His pedestal is placed near the train station, a reference to Japan’s most famous loyal dog, Hachikō, who waited faithfully for his deceased owner at Shibuya station. Citizens in Q-City maintain and clean the pedestal regularly, removing blood stains from monster battles, reflecting their deep respect for him.

His presence makes Q-City safer than most other areas, even attracting people from neighboring towns seeking shelter. This strong protection allows the Hero Association to deploy other heroes to different regions. His consistent vigilance and effectiveness in battle also contribute to the city’s real estate value increase, as the area is seen as exceptionally secure.

Watchdog Man played a significant role in forming the S-Class hero group, alongside Metal Bat. His decision to remain in costume and on guard at all times suggests a strong dedication to his duty, possibly due to personal or mysterious reasons. He only operates within Q-City, and no official explanation has been provided for this limitation.

According to official sources, the origin of his hero name comes from both his dog costume and his fighting style, which mimics the behavior of a wild dog. His physical abilities and aggressive combat style reflect this persona perfectly.

Despite his terrifying appearance and brutal methods, Watchdog Man is viewed as a symbol of hope in Q-City. He stands motionless on his pedestal, always watching, a constant guardian. His indifference toward most human interactions, combined with his dog-like lifestyle, makes him one of the most mysterious S-Class heroes.

**Superalloy Darkshine**

Superalloy Darkshine is the S-Class professional hero of the Hero Association, ranked among the top heroes for his overwhelming physical power and indestructible body. His real name is Chougoukin Kurobikari. He is known for his nearly unbeatable body, which he developed through years of intense physical training starting at the age of 15\.

During his childhood, Chougoukin was physically weak and lacked confidence. He struggled in sports and was often bullied for his frail condition. His life changed when his parents gave him a set of 3-kilogram dumbbells for his 15th birthday. This simple gift became the turning point of his life. He dedicated himself to nonstop training, determined to transform his body. Over the years, his efforts paid off, and he became incredibly muscular and strong. His muscles developed a dark tone, which led to his hero name—Superalloy Darkshine. The name reflects the steel-like hardness of his body and the dark shine of his muscles, symbolizing his strength and appearance.

Chougoukin’s strength is remarkable. He is capable of lifting two tons with a single hand, and his body is considered one of the strongest in the Hero Association. His fighting style focuses on overwhelming power, using his bare fists to defeat monsters. Despite this physical prowess, his personality is complex. He initially had no confidence but became determined to use his strength for good. His intense training and physical growth made him stand out, allowing him to rise quickly through the Hero Association ranks. He started as a C-Class hero ranked 383—the lowest rank—and was later moved up when the S-Class was formed.

One of his notable experiences was sparring against Bang, the Water Stream Rock Smashing Fist master. Although Chougoukin lost that match, the encounter provided him valuable experience in fighting techniques and martial arts, which further shaped his combat style.

Despite his imposing appearance, Superalloy Darkshine struggles with deep-seated fear and self-doubt, particularly after a humiliating loss to Garou. This loss led to a crisis of confidence, causing him to briefly quit being a hero. However, even during this period, his extraordinary muscles remained among the most impressive in the hero world. He has a devoted fan base, whose contact details are collected by the Hero Association during fan events in case they are needed for backup in emergencies.

In early depictions in the manga, his appearance included exaggerated stereotypical facial features, which were later corrected. His journey shows a clear transition from a weak child to a powerful hero driven by the desire to protect others and prove his worth.

**Pig God**

Pig God is the S-Class Rank 8 professional hero of the Hero Association. His real name is unknown, and his origins remain a mystery. The Hero Association gave him the title "God" after hearing of his fearsome reputation and vindictive fighting style. An urban legend claims that Pig God once ate 50 Seafolk without leaving any trace, which only amplified his mysterious and intimidating reputation.

Pig God shares similar defensive capabilities with Food Battler Futoshi, where both rely on fat power. This means that during intense battles, they risk becoming emaciated if they expend all their calories. His physical bulk and endurance are his primary assets, allowing him to withstand heavy damage and overpower foes with brute force.

Despite his immense size and strange eating habits, nothing is officially known about his past. He rarely speaks about himself, and no verifiable records of his early life exist. His hero name reflects the idea of a god-like being, one so powerful and feared that he is considered a vindictive deity by those who know of him.

Pig God remains one of the most enigmatic S-Class heroes. His fighting style is focused purely on overpowering his enemies through sheer force and resilience. His large size allows him to devour or consume threats in a literal sense, making him effective against large groups of monsters and overwhelming foes. His presence in the Hero Association represents the strange and diverse nature of the organization, where heroes come in all forms, each with their unique methods of fighting evil.

**Drive Knight**

Drive Knight is the S-Class Rank 7 professional hero of the Hero Association. His real name is Zero, though he is almost always referred to by his hero name. Drive Knight is known for his tactical transformations and mysterious background. He wears a one-eyed mask and a black armored suit that conceals much of his identity. He was born human and lived in a peaceful village until it was destroyed by a prototype killer machine created by Dr. Bofoi, also known as Metal Knight.

After surviving the attack, Zero dedicated himself to becoming a cyborg to avenge his destroyed village. He began hunting down similar killer machines, studying their technology, and incorporating their parts into his body to become stronger. His unique ability lies in his capacity to transform parts of his body for different combat purposes, named after pieces in the traditional Japanese board game shogi. These Tactical Transformations allow him to adjust his combat style dynamically, depending on the situation.

Drive Knight’s personality is calm, cautious, and methodical. He never hesitates to sacrifice parts of himself if it means achieving victory. His ultimate goal is revenge against Dr. Bofoi for the destruction of his home. He aligns with Genos in this quest, as both share a painful past caused by rogue machines.

Despite his advanced mechanical body, there is ambiguity regarding whether Drive Knight is a remodeling cyborg or a fully autonomous robot. Official artwork shows him eating noodles, adding a layer of complexity to his character, suggesting retained human habits or a programmed trait.

Murata once drew Drive Knight’s Chariot form but later decided to use the Knight form, emphasizing the tactical aspect of his character. His design is somewhat inspired by Batman, particularly in his reliance on tactical gadgets and detailed intelligence gathering.

His combat approach focuses not on raw power but on outsmarting opponents, using carefully planned transformations and technology to his advantage. His mysterious past and ambiguous nature make him one of the most intriguing S-Class heroes, acting more like a tactician than a traditional superhero.

**Zombie Man**

Zombie Man is the S-Class Rank 6 professional hero of the Hero Association. His real origin traces back to the House of Evolution, an organization founded by Dr. Genus with the goal of achieving human perfection through artificial evolution. He was known as Subject No. 66 and is the only successful product of the immortal series the doctor worked on.

Ten years ago, Zombie Man escaped from the House of Evolution, leaving behind a destroyed laboratory. His survival and escape marked a turning point, making him a unique figure in the hero world. One of his most notable feats was his battle against a Mysterious Being that lasted for 140 continuous hours, where he emerged victorious. This intense encounter made him somewhat famous in hero circles.

Despite his overwhelming regenerative capabilities, there are rumors that Zombie Man is physically the weakest among the S-Class heroes. It is suggested that he would find it difficult to defeat the Deep Sea King in combat. His blood is known to taste terrible, so much that even Pureblood, a monster, found it almost impossible to swallow.

Zombie Man's hero name originates from his undying body. His regenerative abilities allow him to recover from 200 fatal injuries in just three minutes and to fully regenerate a severed arm in fifteen minutes, though in the manga, this process appears even faster. His smoking habit does not seem to affect him negatively, likely due to his regenerative powers.

He shares some similarities with the DC Comics anti-hero Rorschach. Both characters have a noir-like style, use brutal methods in interrogation and combat, wear similar leather jackets, and exhibit detective skills. The English dub of Zombie Man gives him a raspier voice akin to Rorschach's. Following his battle with Pureblood, Zombie Man famously locks himself in a room to kill all the monsters present, drawing a parallel to the iconic "You're locked in here with me" line from the Watchmen film adaptation.

His likes include killings that create rivers of blood. He prefers boxers over briefs and enjoys coffee with an excessive amount of sugar, typically more than seven cubes. Despite his abilities, he is not good at swimming. His lifestyle and habits reflect his detached and brutal personality, making him a complex and enigmatic S-Class hero.

**King**

King is the S-Class Rank 5 professional hero of the Hero Association. He is publicly known as "The Strongest Man on Earth" and "The Heroes' Hero." However, the truth is quite different. King is a normal civilian who unintentionally gained fame for heroic acts actually performed by Saitama. He is also part of the Saitama Group.

During his childhood, King excelled in video game tournaments, earning a reputation for skill in virtual competitions. His defining moment came when he was attacked by the Octopus Claw Man. He received three characteristic scars from this encounter but was saved by Saitama, who was training to become a hero at the time. Saitama would later save him five more times by eliminating nearby monsters, including Vaccine Man. Each time, King unintentionally took credit for these acts, often falling into the remains of defeated enemies. The Hero Association, impressed by his supposed strength, granted him S-Class status without him needing to take the hero exam.

King's title signifies "Champion of Heroes" because people believed he could defeat disaster-level monsters with a single blow. He is regarded as the greatest asset of the Hero Association. Despite this, King prefers to work alone, often refusing Hero Association missions. This choice prevents his rank from increasing further.

His silent personality and reputation earn him trust and respect from both heroes and the general public. Monsters are particularly terrified of him, though he has never actually fought anyone. He is even physically weaker than most regular people. The misunderstanding of his prowess has continued uncorrected, making King a figure of myth rather than reality.

King enjoys silence and peace. He dislikes crowds and noise, preferring to train in isolated and quiet places. His hobby is maintaining his body and spirit through training. The origin of his hero name comes from the belief that he effortlessly defeated a disaster-level "Demon" monster, establishing him as the king of heroes.

King recommended the book "How I Developed Indomitable Will," written by ONE and illustrated by Yusuke Murata, as an Easter egg. He is among the more popular heroes, with Sweet Mask being the most popular, but King still ranking high.

When Child Emperor developed a device to measure strength, both Saitama and King appeared undefined. The device either couldn’t detect their power because they were too weak or too strong. In fact, scanning Saitama cracked the device unnoticed.

Rumors claimed that King got his scars in a death match against a Disaster Level God monster, but flashbacks later disproved this. The scars were actually from the Octopus Claw Man.

The King Engine sound effect associated with him is actually the voice of his voice actor. Murata, in a stream, described King as having even more of a Western appearance than Genos.

King is notably the only hero so far who managed to kill a monster purely through sheer intimidation without striking a blow. He also created a martial art style called King Style Qigong. Despite the public’s belief in his strength, ONE confirmed that King will remain powerless and is not destined to suddenly gain abilities.

**Metal Knight**

Dr. Bofoi is the S-Class Rank 4 hero of the Hero Association, known by his hero alias Metal Knight. He is not just a frontline fighter but also provides significant technological support to the Hero Association. His services include weaponry sponsorships and constructing and upgrading association facilities.

Bofoi never attends urgent hero meetings and avoids group missions, preferring to act alone under his hero name. He considers this to be normal and conducts himself in an isolated manner. His reputation is built on his ability to destroy enemies and buildings alike using high-powered weapons and robots.

He possesses at least 100 fighting robots and an immense arsenal of weaponry. The Hero Association fears the potential of him going rogue. This fear is partly why Gyoro Gyoro considers him one of the few heroes capable of defeating Elder Centipede, a fact later confirmed by Drive Knight.

Dr. Bofoi is one of the few individuals aware of Saitama’s strength and has begun spying on him, considering Saitama a potential threat. His careful nature extends to advising his former assistant, Child Emperor, not to trust others easily.

Despite appearing selfish and cold, he contributes to the Hero Association by helping build and improve their infrastructure and secretly working to eliminate corrupt staff. He took possession of Boros' ship remains and moved them to a secret location to develop new technology.

His hero name, "Metal Knight," reflects his reasoning that a steel robot silently engages in peace activities like a knight. The irony lies in the medieval understanding of knights as paragons of chivalry, while Bofoi’s actions are pragmatic and cold.

His personal hobbies include collecting tokusatsu retro toys. His construction robot resembles the AT-AT from Star Wars, and his unmanned bombers are designed after real-world stealth aircraft like the Lockheed F-117 and Northrop B-2 Spirit.

**Child Emperor**

Isamu, better known as Child Emperor, is the S-Class Rank 5 professional hero of the Hero Association and later becomes the leader of Neo Heroes under the alias Wild Emperor. He is the youngest hero in S-Class and has his own laboratory in Y-City.

Originally, Isamu worked as an assistant to Dr. Bofoi, admiring him as a scientist. He also worked as a tutor. His early belief in adult leadership shifted after witnessing the prideful and uncooperative nature of the S-Class during the battle against the Monster Association. This led him to lose faith in adults and to quit the Hero Association.

Child Emperor joined Neo Heroes, a group that is more receptive to criticism and improvement. His true goal is to infiltrate Neo Heroes to uncover McCoy’s schemes and collect vital information about the organization.

He changed his hero name to Wild Emperor after realizing the double meaning of his original name. He is a well-liked figure among his classmates and frequently receives invitations to Christmas parties.

Phoenix Man warned that if Child Emperor combined with his Brave Giant robot, he would become a monster known as Brave Giant Man.

Child Emperor teaches after school and is committed to using intelligence and technology to contribute to hero activities, distancing himself from reliance on traditional adult leadership.

**Atomic Samurai**

Kamikaze, known by his hero alias Atomic Samurai, is the S-Class Rank 3 professional hero and a swordmaster in the Hero Association. He is a member of the Council of Swordmasters.

He owns a dojo that predates the formation of the Hero Association and only permits his three standout disciples—Iaian, Okama Itachi, and Bushidrill—to work as heroes alongside him. His recruitment process includes an apple-cutting test, and he has the unique ability to tell how a person lived their life by observing how they cut with a sword. Only those who pass his strict test are accepted.

Atomic Samurai appreciates his disciples, worthy opponents, people with great strength, and washed fundoshi (which is Japanese-style loincloth). He dislikes weaklings, bothersome things, and interviews. His hobbies are practicing swordsmanship and traveling with his disciples.

When enraged, he emits a powerful fighting spirit, transforming into a demon-like state. He ensures that only his best disciples are admitted into the Hero Association, and all his disciples rank high among the heroes. Their names are portmanteaus related to samurai or swordsmanship.

The artist Murata designed Atomic Samurai based on Kyuzo from "Seven Samurai." Initially, his sword's handguard resembled a swastika but was later changed to an atom symbol in the manga and anime.

His hero name has a double meaning: one refers to his atom-cutting swordsmanship, and the other to his nuclear weapons reminiscent strength.

“Kamikaze” translates literally as "divine wind" or "spirit wind," a reference to the legendary typhoon that protected Japan from Mongol invasion in 1281\.

### **Bang – Silver Fang**

Bang, known by his hero alias Silver Fang, is a martial arts master and the creator of the Water Stream Rock Smashing Fist. He was once the 3rd ranked S-Class hero in the Hero Association and stood among its most powerful heroes. Currently, he lives in Z-City and is part of the Saitama Group. In his youth, he was nicknamed Wind of Blood for his fierce demeanor.

Bang and his brother, Bomb, are considered the greatest masters in the martial arts world. Together, they practiced intensively, developing powerful combination techniques. Initially, Bang was a ruffian, recklessly challenging strong opponents and putting them in the hospital. His brother Bomb confronted him, noting that Bang had gained great strength but had not mastered himself. In their duel, Bang lost, and the experience humbled him. He resolved to create a defense-oriented martial art and established a dojo, following his brother's guidance. Bomb would visit Bang’s dojo over the years, proud of Bang’s transformed attitude.

The Water Stream Rock Smashing Fist was Bang's own invention, developed while training under Bomb. Bang's scars are proof of the effort he invested mastering this technique. At age 30, Bang was regarded as unstoppable. He participated in the Super Fight tournament to promote his dojo and emerged victorious.

Before the series began, Bang held the B-Class rank of 77\. He was later promoted when the S-Class was formed. His top student was Garou, until Garou went rogue, injuring many students, including Sour Face, and incapacitating several others. Bang confronted and expelled Garou, retaining only one student, Charanko.

Bang is left-handed or ambidextrous, shown using chopsticks in his left hand. His interests include Japanese spiny lobster, his dojo, promising youngsters, and his older brother. He dislikes Dojo Hunters, Rookie crushers, and Chinese cabbage. His hobbies consist of toughening his body, spiritual training, playing the board game Go, and continuously refining his martial art. He also enjoys eating tofu, often trying to take many pieces during hot pot meals.

The hero name "Silver Fang" reflects his age and the sharpness of his technique. He holds the second-highest hero rating in the Hero Association, ranking above Tatsumaki but below King. In the original storyboard, Bang fought Elder Centipede using the Water Stream Rock Smashing Fist. He aimed to push back the monster but ultimately only delayed it, suffering a setback himself.

### **Tatsumaki – Tornado of Terror**

Tatsumaki, the S-Class Rank 2 hero known as Tornado of Terror, is one of the strongest heroes in the Hero Association. She is an esper and the older sister and self-proclaimed teacher of Fubuki. Together, they are referred to as the Psychic Sisters.

Her psychic abilities led to her abduction at age seven by a scientific group named Tsukuyomi, interested in studying her powers. Her adopted parents received money for their cooperation. Scientists rewarded Tatsumaki’s compliance with candy and toys but denied her outdoor activities. She was locked in a cell when she refused to use her powers.

At age ten, during a monster outbreak, Tatsumaki was left in her cell to fend for herself. Blast intervened, rescuing her from a hybrid monster. He questioned why she didn’t use her psychic powers to save herself. Tatsumaki claimed they weren’t working, but Blast knew she lied. Her intent was to appear powerless so the scientists would release her. Blast encouraged her to use her abilities to protect her only family—her little sister, Fubuki—rather than expect rescue.

Tatsumaki likes her sister and places where she can fully utilize her psychic powers. She dislikes male heroes who think they are strong and being called a little girl. Despite her harsh demeanor, she respects King on several occasions.

Her hero name came from the Hero Association’s wish to highlight her terrifying abilities, hence "Terror" was added. Tatsumaki is actually five years older than Fubuki, though she is often mistaken for the younger sister.

Tatsumaki has a cameo appearance in Chapter 8 of Mob Psycho 100, describing espers. ONE stated that Tatsumaki is usually stronger than Mob from Mob Psycho 100, though her full power limit remains unknown.

She is obsessed with candy apples, visiting festivals to buy them, and has a fear of ghosts due to an incident involving Saitama.

According to Murata, Geryuganshoop surpasses Tatsumaki in controlling flying objects by eliminating air friction, allowing rocks to fly at sub-light speeds, whereas Tatsumaki has limits on how fast she can throw large objects.

In a livestream, Murata noted Tatsumaki and Fubuki appear French in style and are depicted in Paris, France in volume 33\. Murata admits he finds drawing Tatsumaki difficult, preferring to draw more mature figures.

An animation error misrepresented Tatsumaki’s abilities during the Ancient King fight. ONE originally intended only for her to drop a boulder, triggering the monster's flashback, not as a demonstration of power. Tatsumaki is not capable of pulling a meteor from space, contrary to some animated interpretations.

### **Blast**

Blast is the S-Class Rank 1 hero and the strongest known hero of the Hero Association, second only to Saitama. His primary mission appears to be collecting mysterious cubes, a hobby shared with Saitama. Only a few Hero Association agents have the means to contact him.

He is the father of Blue, the leader of the Neo Heroes. Blast, along with Empty Void and many collaborators, has spent the last twenty years searching for mysterious cubes that can turn humans into Mysterious Beings.

Eighteen years ago, Blast saved Tatsumaki from a hybrid monster and questioned why she didn’t use her psychic powers to protect herself. Tatsumaki claimed they didn’t work, but Blast understood she lied to deceive her captors. He advised her not to rely on others but to protect her sister, Fubuki.

Two years ago, Blast nearly killed Elder Centipede during a battle. He encountered "God" during this fight, who offered a transaction of power. Blast refused and engaged in combat with the mysterious entity, surviving the encounter. Only Sitch of the Hero Association knows about "God," as Blast keeps this secret from others.

Blast’s original design showed a long-haired figure with a cape, but in the tankōbon release, his appearance was changed to appear more mysterious and ambiguous.

His design and characteristics share similarities with Superman, such as his hairstyle, status as the world’s top hero, and secretive personal life. According to Fubuki, Blast can shoot laser beams from his eyes, referencing Superman’s heat vision.
