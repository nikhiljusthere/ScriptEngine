# **Every Nen Enhancer Explained**

***1.Isaac Netero:***

Isaac Netero stands as one of the most iconic figures in the series, having served as the 12th Chairman of the Hunter Association and widely acknowledged as one of the strongest Nen users in history. A warrior at his core, Netero devoted his entire existence to perfecting his craft, mastering Enhancement to an unparalleled degree. His signature Nen ability, ***"100-Type Guanyin Bodhisattva"***, manifests as a colossal, multi-armed deity capable of unleashing devastatingly fast and powerful strikes within seconds. Netero complements this with four distinct techniques: ***"First Hand"***, a straightforward yet devastating chopping strike; ***"Third Hand"***, a crushing clapping motion that traps targets between the Bodhisattva's hands; ***"Ninety-Ninth Hand"***, a relentless barrage of palm strikes from each of the deity's arms that creates a massively powerful attack capable of carving deep into the earth; and his ultimate technique, ***"Zero Hand"***, a last-resort attack that channels his entire aura reserves into one cataclysmic blast fired from the deity's mouth, unleashing an enormous flare of unmatched destructive force. Netero's finest moment comes during his final confrontation with Meruem, the Chimera Ant King. Despite being severely outmatched in raw strength and endurance, Netero demonstrates the tactical brilliance and indomitable spirit that define his character. Utilizing Guanyin Bodhisattva's incredible speed and precision, he launches an unrelenting assault against Meruem, maintaining tactical superiority throughout. Even when Meruem eventually adapts to his strategies, Netero forces the King into an exhausting battle that culminates in his ultimate gambit: the Miniature Rose. By sacrificing his life, piercing his own heart to detonate the hidden bomb, Netero demonstrates his unwavering determination to protect humanity at any cost.

***2.Gotoh:***

Gotoh serves as a butler of the Zoldyck family, entrusted with watching over Killua Zoldyck. As a longtime and trusted servant, Gotoh approaches his duties with utmost seriousness, displaying unwavering loyalty and a stern demeanor. His natural talent in Enhancement allows him to amplify his physical capabilities, augmenting both his strength and speed to remarkable levels. His signature Nen ability, ***"Coin Gatling"***, perfectly encapsulates his precision and lethality – he channels his aura to propel coins at incredible speeds, transforming them into high-velocity projectiles more lethal than conventional bullets, capable of penetrating flesh and overwhelming opponents with ease. When necessary, Gotoh can incorporate a rifling effect into his coins, sacrificing some velocity for increased penetrative power. The rifling is so intense that the coins maintain their spiral trajectory and continue to pierce targets even when momentarily impeded by strong elastic or adhesive materials. This technique exemplifies his masterful combination of raw strength with keen focus and innovative thinking. Gotoh's most memorable moment occurs during the 13th Hunter Chairman Election arc, where he engages in combat with Hisoka while protecting Killua and Alluka. Demonstrating remarkable accuracy with his Coin Bullets, Gotoh proves himself a formidable opponent, showcasing both his combat prowess and his unwavering dedication to duty. Despite his tactical acumen and powerful Nen abilities, Gotoh ultimately falls to Hisoka's cunning and lethal Nen ability, which combines elasticity with adhesive properties to deadly effect. In a cruel twist, Hisoka turns Gotoh's own coins against him, delivering a fatal blow in one fluid, efficient motion.

***3.Gido:***

In the realm of Heaven's Arena, Gido emerges as a formidable Enhancer and former participant who first crosses paths with Gon and Killua during their early journey into mastering Nen. His combat style revolves around the strategic use of mechanical weapons, which he seamlessly integrates with his three distinct Nen abilities. His primary technique, ***"Battle Waltz"***, involves launching Dancing Tops at tremendous velocities, creating a whirlwind of attacks that assail enemies from multiple angles simultaneously. Given the inherent simplicity of spinning tops, their programming remains basic yet effective – Gido circumvents this limitation by commanding them to attack indiscriminately, except for himself, which inadvertently makes their movements highly unpredictable and difficult to counter. His most devastating technique, ***"Tornado Top"***, enables him to transform himself into a human spinning top, utilizing his peg leg while shrouded in aura for both offensive strikes and defensive maneuvers, capable of repelling opponents with tremendous force upon contact. His final ability, ***"Shotgun Blues"***, complements his arsenal by allowing him to rapidly deploy multiple spinning tops in quick succession, launching direct attacks at enemies with exceptional speed. Unlike ***"Battle Waltz"***, these tops maintain a focused assault on their target, making evasion particularly challenging. During their initial encounter, Gido thoroughly dominates Gon due to the latter's inexperience with Nen. However, their second meeting tells a different story – Gon cleverly exploits Gido's overreliance on his spinning tops, enduring significant damage but ultimately turning the tide with his quick reflexes and natural fighting instincts, successfully breaking Gido's leg and claiming victory.

***4.Ikalgo:***

A unique figure in the Chimera Ant arc, Ikalgo transitions from adversary to invaluable ally of Killua and Gon. Unlike his fellow Chimera Ants, he possesses a contemplative nature and compassionate heart that ultimately lead him to question and reject the destructive path of his kind. As an Octopus Chimera Ant, he retains aquatic characteristics while favoring strategic combat over brute force. His primary Nen ability, ***"Living Dead Dolls"***, grants him the power to inhabit and control corpses, allowing him to manipulate their movements, voices, and even access their Nen abilities. While typically residing in the host's head, Ikalgo can exit from various points of the body, though he cannot prevent natural decomposition. His secondary Enhancement ability, ***"Fleadom"***, enables him to transform two tentacles into an air rifle. When controlling a corpse, Ikalgo loads this weapon with specialized giant fleas whose saliva contains potent anticoagulant properties – strong enough to affect even someone of Killua's caliber. These flea bullets demonstrate impressive range and velocity, capable of knocking unsuspecting targets back several meters. Initially struggling against Welfin's predatory abilities, Ikalgo proves his tactical prowess by exploiting the bunker's environment and employing psychological warfare to secure victory. Following the Chimera Ant arc's conclusion, Ikalgo survives to enjoy a peaceful existence after the Chimera Ant King's defeat.

***5.Bill:***

Within the intense drama of the Succession War arc, Bill emerges as a crucial supporting character whose expertise as a botanist complements his abilities as an Enhancer. Working alongside Kurapika as part of Prince Woble's security detail, Bill demonstrates exceptional composure and unwavering loyalty throughout their dangerous mission aboard the Black Whale. His Nen ability, ***"Erigeron"***, exemplifies his Enhancement affinity, allowing him to dramatically accelerate living organisms' growth by channeling aura through his palms. Though not primarily combat-oriented, Bill's resourcefulness and strength prove invaluable when collaborating with Kurapika to outmaneuver hostile forces, whether they be rival bodyguards or assassins targeting the royal family. His steadfast support and practical capabilities enable Kurapika to focus on broader strategic objectives, underlining Bill's essential role in their survival amid the chaos of the Succession War.

***6.Dogman:***

As the highest-ranking member of the Heil-ly Family, Dogman has established himself as their most effective killer, combining sadistic tendencies with a sophisticated understanding of Enhancement Nen. His approach to combat emphasizes raw power and resilience, typical of Enhancers who prioritize straightforward strength over complexity. Having achieved level 62 through Morena's Nen ability, Dogman's aura capacity and output have increased substantially, granting him ***"Supernatural Smell"*** – a specialized ability that enables him to detect enemy presence and identify both Nen types from 5 meters away and Nen proficiency from 2 meters distance. His full combat potential remains unexplored, as his introduction in the manga's later chapters preceded its hiatus, leaving his true capabilities shrouded in mystery.

***7.Uvogin:***

A formidable member of the Phantom Troupe, Uvogin exemplifies raw power as their \#11 member, harboring intense loyalty particularly toward his best friend, Nobunaga Hazama. His reputation stems from immense physical prowess and unwavering battle confidence, perfectly embodying an Enhancer's purest traits. His signature Nen ability ***"Big Bang Impact"*** channels concentrated aura into his fist through Ko, amplifying his natural strength to catastrophic levels, enabling him to obliterate enemies with single strikes and withstand massive damage without hesitation. Uvogin's most impressive display occurs during his confrontation with the Shadow Beasts, the Mafia's elite fighters, in Yorknew City. Surrounded and outnumbered, he demonstrates overwhelming power by effortlessly defeating all four opponents. His ***"Big Bang Impact"*** creates devastating shockwaves that not only destroy his enemies but leave massive craters in the ground, highlighting both his tremendous strength and absolute confidence in engaging without Troupe backup. Uvogin meets his end in an epic battle against Kurapika, who seeks vengeance for the Kurta Clan massacre. Despite his immense power, he falls to Kurapika's Chain Jail, specifically designed to neutralize Troupe members. Kurapika exploits Uvogin's overconfidence, defeating him with a judgment chain through the heart, though Uvogin maintains his loyalty to the Troupe even in death.

***8.Kastro:***

A prominent figure in Heaven's Arena, Kastro ascends to the prestigious position of Floor Master, earning widespread respect for his exceptional might, endurance, and uniquely crafted battle style that ingeniously merges Enhancement with creative Nen applications. His signature Nen ability, ***"Tiger Bite Fist"***, channels enhanced strength into his hands, precisely mimicking the devastating power of tiger claws and fangs, enabling him to execute attacks with such ferocity that they can effortlessly mutilate opponents. Although primarily an Enhancer, Kastro ambitiously extends into Manipulation and Conjuration to develop ***"Double"***, creating a fully functional clone that perfectly mirrors his movements and attacks, allowing him to overwhelm opponents with synchronized assaults of unprecedented precision. However, the complexity required to maintain Double demands extraordinary mental concentration and depletes significant aura reserves, ultimately leaving him vulnerable to more strategically minded adversaries. Having previously suffered defeat at Hisoka's hands, Kastro channels every ounce of his being into their rematch, initially gaining the upper hand through masterful application of his Double technique and landing several decisive blows. Yet, Hisoka's superior Nen mastery and psychological warfare ultimately expose the fundamental weakness in Kastro's strategy \- the severe aura drain caused by Double. This strategic oversight leads to Kastro's downfall as Hisoka systematically dismantles his technique before ending his life in a particularly brutal and humiliating fashion.

***9.Komugi:***

Emerging as a pivotal figure in the Chimera Ant arc, Komugi represents humanity's resilience and complexity through her role as a blind Gungi prodigy from humble origins. Her Enhancement Nen manifests uniquely, rapidly evolving her gaming abilities after each match. Her significance grows when summoned to challenge Meruem, the Chimera Ant King, in Gungi. Initially dismissed, her brilliant strategies, humble nature, and unwavering dedication earn Meruem's deep respect and admiration, fostering an unprecedented bond. Through countless matches, she consistently outmaneuvers the King, demonstrating extraordinary mental acuity and focus. Beyond the game, her influence profoundly transforms Meruem, revealing his capacity for compassion and self-reflection, fundamentally altering his view of humanity. Their story concludes tragically as both succumb to radiation poisoning from the Poor Man's Rose.

***10.Maha Zoldyck:***

Within the legendary Zoldyck assassin family, Maha Zoldyck stands as the eldest known member, a figure whose reputation precedes his mysterious presence. As family patriarch, he embodies generations of unparalleled assassination expertise and lethal precision. Though not explicitly confirmed, his Enhancement affinity aligns perfectly with his renowned physical prowess as an assassin. While his direct appearances remain limited, Maha's historical significance resonates throughout the series, particularly through his legendary encounter with Isaac Netero, the era's strongest Nen user. The fact that Netero emerged as the sole survivor of combat with Maha cements the latter's position among the series' most formidable figures. Speculation suggests his true identity as Zigg Zoldyck, one of two companions who ventured with Netero to the Dark Continent in years past.

***11.Nobunaga Hazama:***

A founding member of the Phantom Troupe, Nobunaga Hazama distinguishes himself through exceptional swordsmanship and unwavering loyalty, particularly to his close friend Uvogin. His boisterous personality and quick temper belie masterful combat abilities, especially in close-quarter sword fighting. His Enhancement affinity perfectly complements his katana expertise and ***"En"*** mastery, allowing him to amplify his speed, precision, and striking power during combat, though his specific Nen ability remains undisclosed. Nobunaga's most compelling moment occurs during his confrontation with Gon and Killua in Yorknew City. Though brief, the encounter showcases his extraordinary reflexes and swordsmanship when he intercepts Killua's escape attempt, positioning himself with blade ready before Killua can react, demonstrating his mastery of close combat and establishing himself as one of the Troupe's most dangerous members in close-range encounters.

***12.Orarge:***

As a member of the Heil-Ly Family, Orarge embodies the group's sadistic tendencies while maintaining steadfast obedience to their cause. Having reached level 21 through Morena's Nen ability, his enhanced aura capacity and output hint at significant combat potential, though his specific Nen ability remains unrevealed. While his combat appearances remain limited, Orarge demonstrates impressive reflexes when intercepting a thrown knife with a book to protect an ally, suggesting his Enhancement abilities significantly boost his physical capabilities.

***13.Palm Siberia:***

Within the complex narrative of the Chimera Ant arc, Palm Siberia emerges as an emotionally intricate and unpredictable character whose volatile nature masks her exceptional capabilities as an Enhancer. Initially training under Knov's guidance, Palm develops an intense dedication to her mission as a Hunter, while simultaneously forming a deeply protective and eventually romantic attachment to Gon during their time together. Her primary Nen ability, ***"Wink Blue"***, demonstrates remarkable sophistication, allowing her to register anyone she observes with her right eye in her crystal, maintaining constant surveillance through her left eye regardless of distance or obstacles. Her defensive technique, ***"Black Widow"***, showcases creative application of her abilities, utilizing her hair to form an elegant yet protective dress and wide-brimmed hat that shields her from enemy attacks, enabling her to focus all her aura on offensive ***"Ko"*** strikes rather than dividing it between attack and defense. The most significant transformation in Palm's journey occurs during her capture by the Chimera Ants, where she undergoes both physical metamorphosis into a hybrid and initial mental conditioning. Yet, despite these profound changes, the core of Palm's humanity perseveres; her unwavering spirit and loyalty to her comrades shine through as she overcomes her conditioning, becoming a crucial ally in the fight against the Ants. One of her most powerful moments unfolds during her emotional confrontation with Gon, where she plays a vital role in preserving his humanity as he descends into despair over Kite's fate. This intervention proves crucial in maintaining Gon's connection to his human side during one of his darkest periods. Palm ultimately survives the intense conflicts of the Chimera Ant arc, continuing to stand steadfastly by her companions' side as they move forward from those harrowing events.

***14.Phinks Magcub:***

As the \#5 member of the Phantom Troupe, Phinks Magcub commands respect through his reliability and fierce loyalty, particularly evident in his close partnership with Feitan. Though his impulsive nature and hot-headed demeanor might suggest otherwise, he demonstrates refined fighting skills and deep camaraderie with fellow Troupe members. His Enhancement affinity perfectly suits his direct combat approach, manifesting in his devastating Nen ability ***"Ripper Cyclotron"***. This technique allows him to multiply his punch's power by winding up his arm like a spring – the more rotations, the greater the destructive force, making it an exceptionally versatile close-combat ability. Phinks demonstrates this devastating power during the Troupe's infiltration of the Nostrade family stronghold. While not fully unleashed in this encounter, his technique's reputation suggests catastrophic potential against both opponents and environment.

***15.Rabid Dog:***

As a formidable member of the Shadow Beasts, the elite Nen-using guard force serving the Ten Dons within the Mafia Community, Rabid Dog embodies a perfect fusion of sadistic tendencies and lethal combat efficiency. His personality aligns seamlessly with his Enhancement-type Nen ability ***"Poison Fangs"***, a technique that dramatically amplifies his speed while increasing both his jaw strength and the lethal sharpness of his poisonous teeth, allowing him to inject powerful neurotoxins capable of paralyzing even the most formidable opponents with a single bite. During the Shadow Beasts' coordinated assault against Uvogin, Rabid Dog demonstrates the true extent of his capabilities in a display of both power and tactical precision. Working in perfect synchronization with his fellow Shadow Beasts' diversionary tactics, Rabid Dog seizes a crucial opening to tear away a significant portion of flesh from Uvogin's shoulder, achieving the remarkable feat of penetrating the Spider's seemingly impervious skin using nothing but enhanced teeth. Pressing his advantage, Rabid Dog executes a second strategic strike, sinking his fangs into Uvogin's thigh while confidently taunting his increasingly unsteady opponent. His tactical prowess shines as he expertly evades Uvogin's retaliatory strikes, patiently waiting for his neurotoxins to take full effect until the mighty Spider finally succumbs to paralysis and collapses to the ground. However, Rabid Dog's triumph proves short-lived as Uvogin eventually overcomes the paralysis through sheer force of will, leading to Rabid Dog's ultimate demise at the hands of the enraged Phantom Troupe member.

***16.Rammot:***

Emerging as an Officer within Colt's Squad during the Chimera Ant invasion, Rammot stands out as an aggressive and violence-loving character among the early Chimera Ant soldiers. His significance grows as one of the first Ants to awaken Nen abilities after exposure to Gon and Killua's aura. As an Enhancer, his physical capabilities reach impressive heights, complemented by his signature ability ***"Feather Blades"*** – transforming his arm feathers into lethal cutting implements. His Nen mastery extends to fundamental techniques including ***"Zetsu"***, ***"Ten"***, ***"Ren"***, and ***"Gyo"***. Following his Nen awakening, Rammot seeks revenge against Killua, but gravely underestimates his opponent's growth. Killua's enhanced speed and refined assassination techniques prove overwhelming, resulting in Rammot's swift decapitation.

***17.Tevelares:***

Within the Heil-Ly family's ranks, Tevelares emerges as a tense, easily provoked individual whose Enhancement abilities are amplified by reaching Level 51 through Morena's Contagion. While this advancement suggests significant increases in both aura quantity and output, his specific Nen ability remains undefined. His lack of combat appearances maintains an air of mystery around his true capabilities, leaving his potential unexplored in the series.

***18.Wing:***

As a pivotal mentor figure introduced in the Heavens Arena arc, Wing brings his experience as Biscuit Krueger's disciple to guide the next generation of Nen users. Though his personal Nen ability remains unknown, his comprehensive understanding of Nen principles makes him an invaluable teacher, particularly in instructing the four fundamental techniques: Ten, Zetsu, Ren, and Hatsu. His measured yet firm teaching approach proves crucial in developing Gon and Killua's potential. Recognizing their exceptional promise, Wing takes the calculated risk of teaching them Nen despite its dangers. This foundational training enables them to awaken their aura nodes and develop crucial defensive capabilities, setting the stage for their remarkable growth throughout the series. His guidance leaves an indelible mark on both students, contributing significantly to their future successes.

***19.Zeho:***

A member of both Team Hanse and Kazsule's alliance in Greed Island, Zeho represents an example of untapped Enhancement potential. Despite his confidence in physical capabilities, his weakness as an Enhancer becomes apparent through his inability to even produce ***"Ren"*** or develop a unique Nen ability. His limitations are so evident that Killua assesses he could defeat Zeho without employing Nen. This assessment proves accurate when Zeho fails to move Bopobo even slightly during their confrontation, resulting in his swift defeat.

***20.Gon Freecss:***

As the series' central protagonist and son of the legendary Hunter Ging Freecss, Gon embarks on an extraordinary journey to follow in his father's footsteps while demonstrating remarkable natural talent for Nen and an unshakeable spirit. His affinity for Enhancement manifests in his signature ability ***"Jajanken"***, an ingeniously straightforward yet effective adaptation of rock-paper-scissors transformed into powerful combat techniques. ***"Rock"***, his primary and most devastating attack, channels his natural Enhancement affinity into a close-range punch of extraordinary power, surpassing the destructive capability of a standard ***"Ko"*** strike by a significant margin. His secondary technique, ***"Scissors"***, demonstrates his ability to transmute aura into a extended blade projected from his fingers, providing effective mid-range combat options, while ***"Paper"*** employs Emission to launch concentrated spheres of aura as ranged attacks, though at reduced power due to its distance from his natural Enhancement affinity. The discovery of Kite's tragic fate triggers a profound transformation in Gon, as overwhelming grief and vengeful rage drive him to make the ultimate sacrifice, trading all his future Nen potential to achieve an immediate adult transformation, instantly gaining physical capabilities equivalent to those he would have possessed in his prime. In this transformed state, his Enhancement abilities, aura output, and reserves reach unprecedented levels, enabling him to completely overwhelm Pitou with devastating force. The true extent of this power manifests in the most catastrophic version of ***"Jajanken: Rock"*** ever witnessed, absolutely crushing Pitou with a single, overwhelming strike before delivering a final, merciless assault. Killua later observes that achieving such power would normally require decades of intensive training, highlighting the magnitude of Gon's sacrifice. However, this immense power extracts a terrible price: Gon's body suffers critical damage, permanently losing all Nen abilities and sacrificing a portion of his natural lifespan. Only through Killua's desperate intervention and Nanika's power does Gon survive this near-fatal transformation, though this moment marks a decisive turning point in his journey as he must now confront life without the Nen abilities that once defined his path forward.
