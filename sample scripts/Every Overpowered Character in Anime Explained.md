**Every Overpowered Character in Anime Explained**

1. **Saitama (One Punch Man)**

Saitama is the most powerful character in *One Punch Man* and he’s basically the definition of overpowered in anime. His entire reputation is built on the fact that he can end almost any fight with a single punch. We're talking about facing off against massive kaiju monsters and alien invaders that would give other heroes nightmares, and he just casually throws a punch and calls it a day. But unlike the typical superhero origin full of destiny and cosmic powers, his backstory is probably the most anticlimactic origin story ever. No radioactive spider bites, no alien heritage, no tragic backstory involving dead parents. Nope. He was just your average kid getting picked on at school, forgetting homework, and sleeping through classes. His uneventful life carried into adulthood, where he ended up unemployed and frustrated while job hunting. That was until one day, three years before the start of the story, when he impulsively saved a boy from a crab monster after failing yet another job interview. That single decision changed everything — he set his sights on becoming a true hero. Determined to transform himself, he started what would become the most infamous workout routine in anime history: 100 push-ups, 100 sit-ups, 100 squats, and a 10 km run every day. His diet was simple — three meals a day with only a banana for breakfast — and he forced himself to tough it out without heating or air conditioning to build mental resilience. By day 300, his body was already feeling the strain, but he also started seeing results. He casually crushed a car while saving a boy from an accident and one-shotted a Tiger-level monster. About a year and a half in, his hair started falling out, and by the time he finished the full three years of training, he had gained power far beyond human limits. Once he began his hero career, it didn’t take long for him to prove his strength by easily annihilating Dragon-level threats like Vaccine Man, Marugori, and Carnage Kabuto  \- these are threats that would normally require teams of top-tier heroes to handle. At first, he was only doing it for fun, but eventually he decided to join the Hero Association alongside his self-proclaimed disciple, Genos. Despite scoring perfectly in every physical exam and breaking every record, he barely passed overall because of the written portion, landing him in C-Class and assigned the hero name Caped Baldy. From the bottom of C-Class, he started climbing the ranks by defending Z-City and defeating increasingly dangerous enemies. Although other heroes often got the credit for his feats, his battles against Deep Sea King, Boros, and later Garou pushed him higher up the hero ladder. Following the Monster Association incident and Garou’s downfall, he now sits at A-Class Rank 39\. The source of his strength is still a mystery. He insists his training routine is the cause, but no one believes him. Regardless, his power has no real equal. His punches are so ridiculously powerful that monsters don't just get defeated \- they explode on contact. He once launched himself off a building, punched straight through a massive meteor heading for the city, landed perfectly fine, and just decided to head home like nothing happened. When the alien Boros kicked him to the moon, he simply jumped back to Earth in seconds, leaving a huge crater on the moon and creating massive shockwaves when he landed. During his fight with Garou, things got even more ridiculous. He lifted the entire surface of Io, one of Jupiter's moons, with one hand and launched chunks of it into space. Then he moved so fast through the debris field that his movement pattern created what looked like a constellation from sheer speed alone. His durability is just as broken as his strength. Temperature means nothing to him \- he can chill in lava like it's a hot bath, casually break out of massive glaciers, and tank multiple nuclear explosions without even flinching. Radiation doesn't bother him either. Garou hit him with a Gamma Ray Burst, which is basically the same energy output as the cosmic phenomenon that can sterilize entire solar systems, and he walked it off like it was nothing. Even psychic powers barely work on him. While he's not completely immune, his willpower is so strong that telekinetic attacks just bounce right off most of the time. His signature attacks fall under what he calls the Serious Series. Unlike his casual punches, these moves actually show a glimpse of his real strength. His Serious Punch creates shockwaves powerful enough to part clouds across the entire planet. He used this to completely obliterate Elder Centipede, a Dragon-level monster that was one of the biggest threats in the series. The most insane display happened when he and Cosmic Fear Garou, who had copied his abilities, threw Serious Punches at each other. The collision created what they called a Serious Punch², which released enough energy to potentially destroy Earth. Luckily, Blast and his team redirected the blast into space, where it wiped out countless star systems and left a massive void in the cosmos. Even with that insane display, people always wonder if he could actually destroy a planet since most overpowered characters can do that. While we haven't seen him intentionally destroy one, we got a pretty good hint during his fight with Garou. He sneezed. Just a regular, involuntary sneeze. It blew away half of Jupiter's gas layers and exposed the planet's core. If a casual sneeze can do that kind of damage, imagine what would happen if he actually wanted to destroy something with a full-power Serious Punch.

2. **Ainz Ooal Gown (Overlord)**

Ainz Ooal Gown is the guildmaster of the group that shares his name, the Overlord of the Great Tomb of Nazarick, and worshiped as the highest of the Supreme Beings by the NPCs who live there. But before he ever took on that legendary title in the New World, he was simply Momonga, a player in the DMMO-RPG YGGDRASIL. And behind the undead skeleton avatar, his true self was Suzuki Satoru, a lonely man in the real world. Satoru’s early life was anything but bright. He lost his mother after she worked herself to death. He never finished beyond elementary school and ended up working as a salaryman while living in a rundown apartment. With no family or friends to lean on, he threw himself entirely into YGGDRASIL, a game that became his second home. What he lacked in reality, he made up for in the virtual world, where the guild Ainz Ooal Gown became the center of his life. The memories he built with his guildmates inside the game meant far more to him than anything outside of it. His devotion ran deep. He poured nearly a third of his monthly salary into YGGDRASIL through cash shop purchases, just to strengthen and sustain the guild. After the creation of the guild, Momonga and his friends managed to track down the dungeon known as the Great Tomb of Nazarick. With his leadership and the cooperation of his guildmates, they conquered Nazarick and claimed one of the rarest World Items, the Throne of Kings. From then on, the Tomb became their guild base, and together they fought off intruders who dared to challenge them. Much of the guild’s efforts during this period went into creating their most prized treasure, the Staff of Ainz Ooal Gown. They also spent time designing custom NPCs, with Momonga personally creating Pandora’s Actor. For a time, everything was thriving. But reality eventually caught up with his friends. One by one, almost all of the guild members quit the game, deleting their avatars and leaving behind their equipment for Momonga to inherit. In the end, only four of the original forty-one remained, and even they left. Out of loyalty and sentiment, Momonga stayed behind, running the guild base by himself despite the financial and emotional toll. Even as the game was nearing its official shutdown, he refused to abandon Nazarick. His persistence paid off — no outside players ever managed to conquer the Tomb, even in its final moments. But instead of fading away as expected, everything changed at the stroke of midnight. The server never shut down, and Momonga suddenly noticed the NPCs moving and speaking on their own. What should have been the end of the game was instead the start of something completely new: Nazarick had been transported to another world, a place that would come to be known as the New World. His powers and abilities are the foundation of his overwhelming presence in the New World, making him both feared and revered as he takes on the name of the guild itself. At his core, he is a level 100 magic caster who specializes in necromancy and death-related magic, but his abilities extend far beyond the standard limits of a typical caster. Normally, players in YGGDRASIL were capped at learning 300 spells, yet through his unique racial skill “Dark Wisdom,” he managed to memorize and store an incredible 718 different spells. This enormous repertoire gives him a massive tactical advantage, letting him adapt to almost any opponent or battlefield condition. Even spells he hasn’t mastered are useful to him, since simply knowing them allows him to gauge the strengths and weaknesses of enemies who attempt to use similar magic against him. In the New World, this careful and methodical approach makes him far more dangerous. He always prepares beforehand, collecting items, arranging equipment, and buffing himself before engagements. Although his primary role is that of an arcane caster, he has developed a wide range of combat options that let him stand against warriors and melee fighters as well. With spells like “Perfect Warrior,” he can temporarily transform his skeletal body into one that allows him to wield physical weapons without restrictions, bypassing his usual class penalties. This flexibility lets him use powerful melee weapons like the Frost Pain sword with deadly effect. He has even demonstrated skill with weapons like halberds, surprising foes by combining martial tactics with magical support. In direct combat, his undead body also gives him unusual durability — his immunity to many forms of magic, resistance to status effects, and lack of vital organs make him much harder to kill than any ordinary human opponent. Beyond combat spells, Ainz has a vast collection of utility and defensive magic. He can nullify divination with his anti-scrying barriers, ensuring enemies cannot spy on him, and he can even turn attempts to locate him into attacks by linking explosive counterspells to divination efforts. His resistance to low- and mid-tier magic is absolute, meaning that even powerful casters in the New World cannot touch him with conventional spells. When it comes to summoning, his undead legions give him control of powerful creatures, and through mental bonds, he can direct his summons seamlessly in battle. This allows him to overwhelm opponents with numbers while still focusing his own attention on higher-level strategy. His signature large-scale magic reflects his status as an Overlord-class caster. He has cast devastating spells like “Fallen Down,” a super-tier attack that obliterates enemies with celestial light, and “Ia Shub-Niggurath,” which consumes thousands of lives as a sacrifice to summon Dark Young, eldritch horrors capable of leveling armies by themselves. His Serious-tier spells are so destructive that even large-scale armies stand no chance against them. In one display, he casually annihilated an entire Scripture unit of the Slane Theocracy using a chain of high-tier spells including “Black Hole,” effortlessly wiping out forces that would have terrified kingdoms. He founded and rules the Sorcerer Kingdom with E-Rantel as its capital, conquered the Re-Estize Kingdom through overwhelming military might, established diplomatic relations with the Baharuth Empire and other nations, and successfully infiltrated and manipulated multiple kingdoms under his adventurer alias "Momon."

3. **Zeno (Dragon Ball Super)**

Grand Zeno is basically the ultimate boss of everything in *Dragon Ball Super*. This little guy rules over the entire multiverse and nobody even comes close to his level of power. He's got this crazy ability called Erase that only he and his future version can use, making them absolutely untouchable in terms of raw strength. When he wants to delete something from existence, he charges up these twin blue energy spheres in his hands and just wipes whatever he's targeting completely out of reality. We're talking about erasing immortal beings too \- this technique doesn't mess around. He actually used this power to eliminate six entire universes just because he was having a bad day. Though in the manga, it seems like he thought there were just too many universes cluttering up the place, so he decided to clean house. This Erase ability is hands down the most devastating power in the entire *Dragon Ball* franchise. Future Zeno proved just how insane it is when he completely wiped out Infinite Zamasu, who was supposed to be immortal and everything. He also took out either just Universe 7 from Future Trunks' timeline in the anime, or went completely nuclear and erased the whole timeline in the manga version. Interestingly enough, people who were originally from that timeline but happened to be somewhere else didn't get erased. In the manga's Universal Conflict Saga, Fused Zamasu actually hints that his immortality let him survive Future Zeno's erasure technique, which is pretty wild to think about. During the Tournament of Power, both versions of him erased seven universes along with all their fighters and gods. The only ones who survived were the angels like Mohito, Kusu, Sour, Vados, Cognac, Camparri, and Marcarita. Luckily, Android 17 wished all those universes back when he won the tournament. The scary part is that Zeno had a backup plan \- if whoever won had made a selfish wish, he would have erased absolutely everything. This technique is so thorough that even if someone from a doomed universe managed to escape to another universe, they'd still vanish if he wanted them gone. The manga showed us that he can completely destroy entire realities \- the Time Ring representing Future Trunks' world literally shattered when he eliminated that parallel dimension. During the Tournament of Power, it seemed like he could erase all timeline versions of a universe if he felt like it. This means everyone in that universe across all timelines would disappear, but as long as one timeline stays safe, alternate versions of people would survive. That explains why Future Trunks and Future Mai didn't get erased \- their present timeline version of Universe 7 was never touched. Even after Zamasu killed all the other gods in Future Trunks' timeline, Shin was completely confident that Zeno would still be alive. He made it clear that nothing can kill the Omni-King, which turned out to be true when Future Zeno survived wiping out his own timeline. Both versions of him were completely unaffected by Rumsshi's Battle Roar, which knocked out the Supreme Kais and paralyzed the Gods of Destruction. Despite having this terrifying level of power, he doesn't actually fight anyone. His fighting instincts are pretty much nonexistent compared to other deities in the series. But he's still got some decent awareness \- in the anime, he managed to erase Frost right before the guy could fire a Death Beam at Frieza as payback for getting knocked out of the ring.

4. **Anos Voldigoad (The Misfit of Demon King Academy)**

Anos Voldigoad is basically the most ridiculously overpowered demon you'll ever hear about. He was literally known as the Demon King of Tyranny during the Mythical Age. He was born from his mother's corpse after humans killed her while she was pregnant. This brutal beginning shaped him into someone with unmatched destructive power. He ruled over Dilhade when it was chaos central, with the Four Evil Kings, Sorcerer King Bomiras, and other demons constantly fighting each other. He built his empire by welcoming anyone who wanted to join him while absolutely crushing those who opposed him. The more people he protected, the more ruthless he became toward his enemies, earning his terrifying reputation throughout the entire realm. During the Great War between humans, spirits, gods, and demons, he kept clashing with this hero named Kanon. These two developed this weird mutual respect thing despite being enemies, constantly testing each other's limits on the battlefield. Eventually, he got completely sick of all the fighting and came up with this wild plan to create walls between the four races, hoping they'd chill out after a thousand years or so. He made this deal with Kanon, Great Spirit Reno, and the Goddess of Creation Militia, then sacrificed himself to provide the magic needed for this massive undertaking. Fast forward 2000 years and he reincarnates into the Magical Age, only to find that his descendants have gotten soft from all the peace and magic has seriously declined. He enrolls in the Demon King Academy, which is supposed to identify and train the reincarnated Demon King, but these idiots completely fail to recognize his power and label him as a Misfit. Pretty ironic considering he's literally the original Demon King they're all looking for. His power comes from being a Voldigoad, which means his blood is basically the essence of destruction itself. The Voldigoad bloodline has such intense destructive power that they can barely have children \- his mother's death during his birth actually contributed to his insane destructive abilities. His magic power is so ridiculous that measurement crystals literally shatter when trying to gauge it. During his fight with Ivis Necron, who was fused with the Keeper of Time and had his power increased tenfold, he completely dominated while his own magic was reduced to less than a third and he was simultaneously protecting other people. Later, it was revealed that everything he'd been doing up to that point was with less than a tenth of his actual power because he wasn't even used to his new body yet. When he fought Graham, we learned that he actively holds back his power by suppressing and offsetting it with itself, controlling just the tiny remaining amount so he doesn't accidentally destroy the world. His magical arsenal is just as insane as everything else about him. He's got the Magic Eyes of Destruction that literally destroy everything he looks at, though they're actually the ultimate form of anti-magic and the destruction is just a side effect of their true power. Then there's Rivide, this origin spell that lets him mess with time itself \- he can reverse objects and spells back to their starting point, freeze them in time, or even send them into the future, though he needs to know their history to pull off the reversal trick. One of his nastiest attacks is Jio Graze, which is supposedly the highest grade fire magic in the demon magic system. This thing creates a miniature jet-black sun that he can fire at enemies. Normally, this spell requires multiple magic users working together with a Hellfire Mortar, but he can just cast it solo without even needing the mortar. And that's just scratching the surface of his magical abilities. His physical abilities are completely absurd too. He once lifted an entire castle with one hand and spun it around his finger like it was a toy. He moved the actual moon with his bare hands to create a solar eclipse and held up a collapsing sky dome with raw strength alone. Speed-wise, he casually outpaced a Holy Knight who had literally turned into light and achieved lightspeed. Intellectually, he's just as broken. He memorized and learned a complicated magic formula in one second that took someone else a month to master. He can decipher hundreds of thousands of magic characters instantly and read nearly 1,800 books in moments while perfectly memorizing everything. Despite being a demon, he can use holy magic because he understands it's just on a different wavelength. When he creates objects with magic, he doesn't just make the object \- he creates the actual atoms that compose it. He wields Venuzdonoa, the Abolisher of Reason, which can destroy literally anything that exists, including the fundamental order of the world itself. He also has access to other weapons like the Adamantine Sword and the Selection Pledge Jewel that lets him summon gods. Even with the legendary holy sword Evansmana specifically forged to destroy him, he remains virtually unstoppable. His Demon King's Blood is so corrosive that it affects even divine powers, and if enough accumulates, it could slowly destroy the entire world.

5. **Kageyama Shigeo (Mob Psycho 100\)**

Shigeo Kageyama, better known as "Mob," looks like your typical unremarkable middle school kid. He's physically weak, has zero coordination, terrible stamina, and basically can't do anything athletic to save his life. But underneath that plain exterior, he's packing some of the most ridiculous psychic powers you'll ever see in anime. His whole emotional suppression thing started after an accident where his younger brother Ritsu got hurt because of his powers. Ever since then, he's been terrified of what he might do if he loses control, so he keeps everything bottled up inside. He was desperately looking for guidance when he stumbled across Reigen, this con artist who calls himself the "Greatest Psychic of the 21st Century." Reigen runs this fake consultation office called Spirits and Such, and he immediately saw an opportunity to exploit this incredibly powerful but naive kid. The funny thing is, even though Reigen is a total fraud who's using him, he actually teaches Mob some important life lessons. He convinces him that having psychic powers doesn't make him better than anyone else and that he shouldn't use his abilities to hurt people. So Mob becomes his assistant, going around exorcising actual spirits like the ones haunting Honeido Tunnel and dealing with a perverted ghost at a girls' school. Reigen benefits from Mob's work, but at least Mob learns to use his powers to help people. The real insanity happens when his emotions finally hit their breaking point. He's got this internal meter that builds up to 100%, and when it maxes out, all hell breaks loose. The crazy part is that how he uses his exploded power depends entirely on what emotion pushed him over the edge. When he hit 100% Rage after some cultists made fun of him, he got dead serious about fighting and stopping problems. 100% Sadness happened after he accidentally destroyed a whole school, and he literally cried while automatically rebuilding the entire thing with his powers. 100% Hostility came out when he saw some psychic thug brutally attacking his brother, leading to Mob delivering an absolutely savage beatdown. 100% Rejection fried someone's brain when they forced him to see an illusion of his brother bleeding out. He almost reached 99% Murderous Intent and was ready to kill people until Reigen snapped him out of it, which flipped his emotions to 1000% Gratitude. That was wild because he actually transferred all his powers to Reigen temporarily, making his fake mentor incredibly powerful for a brief moment. 100% Courage might be his most combat-effective state because it makes him look like some kind of anime pretty boy and lets him fight on equal terms with opponents who were previously dominating him. 100% Tenacity turned him into a single-minded hunter tracking down enemies who supposedly kidnapped his family. 100% Friendship let him absorb and redirect massive energy attacks while sharing his life story. 100% Ecstasy had him laughing maniacally while flying around enjoying battle like some kind of psychotic berserker. 100% Compassion made him cry while trying to crush his opponent with buildings because he felt sorry for them. 100% Resignation led to him brutally twisting someone's body to physically stop them from fighting. 100% Kindness had him creating protective barriers and trying to save his enemy's life. The weirdest one was 100% Trust, where he completely shut off his psychic powers and became totally vulnerable because he chose to trust someone like Dimple instead of fighting him. But the absolute most terrifying state is when he goes into what we're gonna call the question mark mode (???%). This happens when he gets knocked unconscious under extreme stress, and his body basically goes into autopilot with unlimited psychic power. When he got hit by a car trying to save a kid, this mode activated and continued his journey to see his crush Tsubomi. Every step he took crushed the road beneath him, buildings collapsed just from him walking by, and he completely destroyed anyone who tried to stop him, including his friend Teru and his brother Ritsu. The only person who is able to reach him was Reigen, who finally came clean about being a fraud and taking advantage of him all this time. Reigen poured his heart out with a genuine apology and convinced Mob to accept that this destructive side of him was also part of who he is. Once Mob stopped trying to suppress that part of himself and embraced it instead, he regained control of his body and could finally express emotions normally without his powers going berserk.

6. **Frieren (Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End)**

Frieren might look like your average pointed-eared magic user, but she's basically a walking disaster wrapped in elf form. This thousand-year-old mage has been perfecting the art of magical mayhem since before most kingdoms even existed, and her track record speaks for itself. Born in a peaceful elven village over a millennium ago, she quickly became the local magical prodigy with a mana pool so massive it made everyone else look like they were running on AA batteries. Unfortunately, her childhood took a dark turn when the demon general Basalt the Throne decided to redecorate her village with destruction. Most people would call it quits after barely surviving genocide, but not our girl. She actually hunted down Basalt and took him out personally. Enter Flamme, the legendary human mage who saw potential in this traumatized elf and took her under her wing. Flamme didn't just teach her spells, she gave her the ultimate cheat code that lets her conceal her mana level, making demons think she's just another mediocre mage until she unleashes hell on them. After Flamme's death, she went full hermit mode, wandering the world and collecting magic like some immortal hoarder. During this time, she had a random encounter with a lost kid named Himmel, showed him a flower spell, and completely forgot about it. That kid grew up to become a legendary hero who never forgot their meeting and eventually convinced her to join his party to take down the Demon King. The Hero Party's ten-year journey was basically a magical road trip with incredibly high stakes. Alongside Himmel the Hero, Eisen the Warrior, and Heiter the Priest, she helped defeat the ultimate evil threatening the world. After their victory, they made a promise to reunite in fifty years to watch some special meteors together. When that reunion finally happened, reality hit her like a truck. Himmel was now an old man while she hadn't aged a day, and shortly after their meteor viewing, he passed away peacefully. This moment broke something in her. For the first time in centuries, she realized she'd wasted decades without truly understanding the people who called her friend. That emotional awakening started her new journey with Fern, a young human mage adopted by Heiter, and later Stark, a cowardly warrior with hidden potential who was mentored by Eisen. The trio is on their way to Aureole where the souls of the dead are believed to reside. Frieren hopes to meet the soul of Himmel there. But let's talk about why she's absolutely broken as a character. Her mana output is so ridiculous that it rivals Serie, the head of the Magic Association who's been around for who knows how long. When she faced Aura the Guillotine, one of the Seven Sages of Destruction, the demon tried using the Scales of Obedience to force her into submission. This spell compels weaker beings to obey based on mana comparison. The problem was, she had been hiding her true power for so long that when she finally dropped her concealment, the scales immediately flipped and forced Aura to obey her instead. She literally commanded a five-hundred-year-old demon general to unalive herself. Her magical arsenal reads like a greatest hits collection of destruction. She's mastered Zoltraak, turning what used to be demon-killing magic into standard offensive spells through sheer innovation. She can summon lightning storms with Judradjim, create hellfire blasts with Vollzanbel, and even cast spells that create black holes. When pushed to her absolute limit, she can use something called "the Height of Magic," an attack so advanced that targets can't even recognize it as a spell until it's crushing them. Her defensive capabilities are equally insane. She's immune to most restraint and hypnosis magic thanks to some ancient technique that only specialists might crack. Her sealing magic is so potent that she once turned the demon Qual into stone for eighty years, and the spell was still holding strong when she finally decided to release him for a rematch. The Golden Land arc really showcased her abilities when she went up against Macht, one of the few beings who had actually defeated her in the past. This demon had turned an entire region into indestructible gold, and everyone thought the curse was permanent. But she pulled off something that was supposedly impossible, reversing the transmutation and restoring thousands of people from their golden prison. What makes her truly overpowered isn't just her raw magical strength, though that's certainly terrifying. It's her combination of experience, technique, and the ability to constantly adapt and learn new magic. She's been collecting spells for over a thousand years, has survived multiple demon wars, and has enough mana to power a small country. When you combine millennium-long experience with magical innovation and enough raw power to intimidate ancient demons, you get a character who's basically playing the game on cheat mode while everyone else is still reading the tutorial.

7. **Griffith (Berserk)**

Griffith was a brilliant military genius who basically speedruns his way to the top of the medieval food chain. He's got the Band of the Hawk, a scrappy mercenary group that he transforms into an unstoppable war machine through pure tactical brilliance and a magnetic personality that makes everyone want to follow him. He literally helps end a century-long war, brings honor to the kingdom of Midland and gets handed a noble title while he patiently awaits to attain his ultimate dream of ruling his own kingdom. As he gradually climbs the ranks due to his numerous victories and feats, his dream is within his reach. It also helps that the princess of Midland is in love with him. Now all he has to do is keep his victories coming and wait until the right time comes. But then his best friend Guts decides to leave the Band to find his own path, and that's when everything takes a chaotic turn. This departure hits him so hard that he completely loses his mind and makes the worst possible decision \- sneaking into the castle to sleep with the princess. Predictably, he gets caught, thrown in a dungeon, and spends the next year getting tortured until he's basically a broken shell of a human being. When Guts returns and finally rescues him, he's mute, crippled, and can barely function. His grand dream of ruling a kingdom seems completely dead in the water. After his rescue, he tries to escape in a carriage, crashes it into a river, and while staring at his mangled reflection, decides to end his life by cutting his throat on a sharp tree root. But he is too weak to even inflict a minor gash. His Crimson Beherit, a creepy supernatural artifact he'd been carrying around, suddenly shows up out of nowhere and activates. The whole Band of the Hawk gets yanked into this nightmare dimension where the God Hand \- four absolutely terrifying demon lords \- make him an offer he apparently can't refuse. They tell him he can have ultimate power and achieve his dream, but there's a catch. He has to sacrifice the people closest to him \- his entire mercenary band who just risked everything to save him. Without hesitation, he accepts. He literally whispers "I sacrifice" and dooms every single person who believed in him to a horrific death by demon monsters. The transformation turns him into Femto, a member of the God Hand, and this is where he becomes completely broken as a character. He can manipulate gravity and space like they're toys, creating crushing force fields and redirecting attacks through dimensional warping. Physical weapons can't touch him because he can phase through them, and he can fly around with these massive black wings. The most insane part is his near-omniscience when it comes to causality \- he can basically see how events will unfold and manipulate them to his advantage.
