Saitama, the bald-headed juggernaut of Z-City, wasn’t always the world’s strongest hero. In fact, his beginnings were painfully ordinary. He lived alone in a rundown apartment in the Ghost Town district of Z-City. As a kid, he obsessed over superhero shows and dreamed of saving people from monsters. 

In middle school, third-year bullies cornered him and demanded money. Saitama didn’t back down, but he wasn’t strong enough to fight them either. They took his 200 yen. That’s when things took a weird turn. A pig-like monster called Piggy Bancon slammed into the bullies, demanding their money too.

Saitama overheard that one bully was just trying to buy lunch for his younger brother. Feeling fired up, Saitama chased after the monster to recover the money. He got flattened instead. The authorities eventually took Piggy Bancon down. But the incident left Saitama questioning his place in the world.

Three years before the main story begins, Saitama was just another unemployed guy on the job hunt. Before that, he worked part-time at a convenience store. 

After another failed interview, he encountered Crablante, a human-crab hybrid monster. Crablante noticed Saitama’s deadpan stare and decided to let him live, saying he was hunting a kid with a cleft chin. Saitama spotted the child and, despite initially walking away, jumped in when Crablante attacked.

This spontaneous act of heroism reignited his childhood dream.

He trained every single day:

* 100 push-ups

* 100 sit-ups

* 100 squats

* 10 km run

No AC, no heat, just raw grit. His body was constantly in agony, he vomited blood, ached all over, and felt like quitting daily. But he stuck to it, no matter what. Slowly, painfully, his body began to change.

Around Day 300 of training, Saitama had improved but wasn’t unstoppable yet. When the monster “Personification of a Light Pull Cord” started smashing up the city, Saitama stepped in. The creature punched him through a wall. But Saitama got up, annoyed more by a cavity than the monster’s strength. He ended the fight in one punch, and went back to training.

As his training continued, Saitama ran into more monsters. During an early fight with Octopus Claw Man, he sustained injuries but managed to save a bystander, King. 

By the end of a year and a half of relentless training, Saitama had lost all his hair, and gained immeasurable strength. He kept training for another year and a half before officially starting his career as a hero. What began as a childhood fantasy turned into a bizarre, lonely, and legendary journey.

And it all started with a tie, a crab, and a kid with a weird chin.
