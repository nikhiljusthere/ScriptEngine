**The Most Complex Arc in Hunter x Hunter Explained**

The Succession Contest arc is famously known as the part of *Hunter x Hunter* where you need a full-on conspiracy board and detective-level focus just to keep track of who’s currently breathing. It’s a chaotic mess of fourteen princes, dozens of secret assassins, and parasitic spirit beasts all trapped on a giant boat where a single conversation can feel like a high-stakes chess match. So today we're breaking down the galaxy-brain strategy and the complicated web of alliances into eight simple steps so you can finally stop scratching your head and actually enjoy the genius of this arc.

* **The Dark Continent Expedition**

Everything kicks off with the Zodiacs — the Hunter Association's top twelve hunters — getting called into an emergency meeting by the current chairman Cheadle. Before the meeting even gets started, Beans barges in and shows everyone a video of King Nasubi Hui Guo Rou, the ruler of the Kakin Empire, announcing that his country is going to the Dark Continent. If you don't know what the Dark Continent is, think of it as the terrifying unknown world beyond the borders of the known world, a place so deadly that every previous attempt to explore it has ended in catastrophe. Humanity has tried before and come back with calamities, plagues, and other horrors that they've been desperately trying to hide from the public ever since. And the kicker in that video? Nasubi's expedition leader is Beyond Netero — the son of Isaac Netero, the former Hunter Association chairman who went down fighting Meruem, the King of an invasive species that originated from the Dark Continent. So yeah, that alone tells you the kind of horrors waiting out there. Beyond is essentially using Nasubi's political muscle and the prestige of the Kakin Empire to force this whole expedition to happen, bypassing every international regulation that would normally stop someone from doing something this reckless. The V5 — the five most powerful nations in the world — technically have authority over who gets to travel to the Dark Continent, but Beyond has maneuvered them into a corner. The Zodiacs, now under a direct assignment from the V5, are told to hunt Beyond down. But Beyond, being the incredibly smug chess player that he is, just turns himself in. He walks up, lets them restrain him, and casually predicts that they'll end up releasing him and going along with his plan anyway. And he's right. The V5 ultimately decide that the safest move is to let Kakin join them as the new V6, officially support the voyage, and just supervise Beyond rather than fight him. Beyond gets his trip, and the Zodiacs get dragged along to babysit him for the entire journey. Meanwhile, Ging Freecss — the world's biggest chaos agent and Gon's dad — shows up at Beyond's base and just decides he's going too, weaseling his way in as the team's deputy leader through a mix of intimidation and raw talent. And Cheadle recruits Leorio into the Zodiacs to fill the seats left by Ging and Pariston's departures. Leorio then immediately asks if Kurapika can come along, which is how we get to the actual meat of the story. Mizaistom, one of the Zodiacs, actually tracks Kurapika down at a Nostrade Family base and basically blackmails him into joining — in a friendly way — by offering him the identity of the person who owns a large collection of Scarlet Eyes. That person turns out to be the 4th Prince of Kakin, Tserriednich Hui Guo Rou. That's all Kurapika needs to hear.

* **The Royal Deathmatch**

Here's where things get wild. King Nasubi didn't just use this expedition as a political move — he's been planning to use the entire voyage as the stage for Kakin's succession war. The Kakin Empire has fourteen princes born from eight different queens, and in the Kakin tradition, they determine the next king through a ritual deathmatch where only one prince survives. The last one standing takes the throne. There are a few rules that make this whole thing extra cursed. First, only children of the king's legal wives can participate. Second, the contest can only be canceled if someone dies before the voyage begins. Once the ship sets sail, it's game on. Third — and this is the part that really messes with everyone — participants technically can't just quit. There's no formal way to drop out. The only way to leave the game is to die. Before the voyage, every participating prince goes through something called the Seed Urn Ceremony. The details of the ritual are kept vague, but what it does is implant a parasitic Nen creature inside each prince — something that hatches and manifests as their Guardian Spirit Beast. Each Guardian Spirit Beast is influenced by the personality and subconscious desires of its host, which means no two beasts are the same. Some are aggressive, some are defensive, some are completely bizarre, and none of them can be seen by their own hosts. Only other people can see them, and only people with Nen can do anything about them. There are two major rules governing the Guardian Spirit Beasts. First, they cannot kill each other. Second, they cannot directly attack other princes who already have their own beasts. This is actually a deliberate design choice by Nasubi to level the playing field by preventing brute-force personalities from just steamrolling everyone else. A prince whose Guardian Spirit Beast is just rocking fangs and claws is at a disadvantage compared to a prince whose personality spawned a more low-key, sneaky ability. In this game, patience and clever plays win way more than raw aggression. And these beasts feed on the aura of their hosts, which means princes slowly feel an unexplained fatigue throughout the voyage without knowing why. Now the ship carrying these 200,000 people is called the Black Whale 1, and it’s basically a floating city split across multiple tiers. The royals and VIPs are up on Tiers 1 and 2 while general passengers are in the lower tiers. 

* **The 14 Kakin Princes**

Let's run through every single prince from oldest to youngest because each one of them is a whole situation. 1st Prince Benjamin is the eldest and the most militarily inclined. As the Deputy Military Adviser of the Kakin Empire, he commands a private army of elite soldiers who were already skilled Nen users long before the voyage began. Benjamin is a rare double threat — he was an experienced Nen user even before the ceremony, and he possesses a physical strength so immense he can crush a lion with his bare hands. His personal Nen ability is called "Benjamin Baton," and it’s one of the most dangerous powers in the series. It allows him to inherit the Nen abilities of his loyal private soldiers after they die, provided they were graduates of the Kakin Royal Military Academy and sworn to him. This effectively gives Benjamin a growing arsenal of diverse powers with every loyal soldier he loses in battle. Meanwhile, his Guardian Spirit Beast remains a total mystery; while we’ve seen its intimidating, bug-like appearance, its actual ability hasn't been revealed yet. 2nd Prince Camilla is Benjamin's younger sister and is arguably more unhinged than he is. She commands a private army of "Have-Nots" — Kakin’s lowest social caste — who have developed a "Curse" type of Nen. These assassins build up a death wish over time that triggers a lethal curse on their target only after the assassin takes their own life. Camilla herself possesses a terrifying counteractive Nen ability called "Cat’s Name". When she is killed, a giant cat-like Nen beast appears, crushes her killer to distill their life force, and feeds it to her to bring her back to life. She is so confident in this "immortality" that she’ll purposefully walk into gunfire just to trigger the revival and eliminate her enemies. Additionally, she has a separate Guardian Spirit Beast which is a Manipulator with a coercive-type ability, capable of taking complete control of anyone who fulfills its specific secret conditions. 3rd Prince Zhang Lei is the candidate who feels most like a traditional king — he’s patient, diplomatically savvy, and avoids unnecessary bloodbath. He maintains a strategic alliance with the Xi-Yu Mafia family, using them as his eyes and ears on the lower decks. His Guardian Spirit Beast is a Conjuration-type that produces a gold coin from its mouth every day. The mechanic is unique: when a person holds the coin, their Nen potential increases, but there’s a catch — the "value" of the coin changes based on who is holding it and how long they keep it. Zhang Lei distributes these coins to his associates to build a literal and figurative network of value, though he is still frustrated by the fact that he doesn't fully understand the coins' ultimate power yet. He’s essentially playing the long game, betting that his wealth and influence will eventually manifest into a power that can overwhelm the more aggressive princes. 4th Prince Tserriednich is the true "monster" of this arc — a sociopath who views himself as an artist and everyone else as sub-human. He owns a massive collection of Scarlet Eyes, including what appears to be the head of Kurapika's childhood friend, Pairo — which is exactly why Kurapika joined this mission. The most terrifying thing about him is his growth: he is a "one-in-a-billion" Nen genius who surpassed Gon and Killua’s learning speed in just a few days. His Guardian Spirit Beast acts as a lethal lie detector — if someone lies to him once, it marks them; twice, it warns them; and a third lie causes them to undergo a gruesome, non-human transformation. On top of this, Tserriednich has developed his own personal Specialist ability, "Parallel Future." By entering a state of Zetsu and closing his eyes, he can see 10 seconds into the future and then act within a "parallel reality" where others only see the original future he foresaw, effectively making him  untouchable in a one-on-one fight. He served as the benefactor for the Heil-Ly mafia family. But when the Heil-Ly family began creating chaos on the ship, he cut ties with them and is now seeking to eliminate Morena, the Heil-Ly family's new leader. 5th Prince Tubeppa is a sharp political player who immediately starts working angles. She approaches Tserriednich early on with a proposal for an alliance, and then separately begins reaching out to the lower princes to build a coalition. Her Guardian Spirit Beast is a collaborative Transmuter that works with a partner to create various substances inside its body — useful for intelligence gathering and subtle manipulation. Tubeppa sends her bodyguard Longhi to Kurapika's Nen lessons specifically as an informant, which leads to a major plot twist later. 6th Prince Tyson is the arc's most bizarre wildcard. She’s obsessed with "world peace" and her own brand of universal love, which she’s documented in a massive manifesto called the Book of Tyson. Her Guardian Spirit Beast is an Emitter-type that takes the form of a giant heart with a single eye, and it produces tiny, dragonfly-like sub-beasts that hover over anyone who reads her book or spends time with her. These little creatures feed off the person's aura to grant them a feeling of overwhelming happiness and peace — but it's a trap. The catch is that this "happiness" is tied to how well they follow Tyson's "Ten Commandments." If they break one of her rules, they are hit with a divine punishment. Unlike the other princes, Tyson isn't necessarily out for blood; she truly believes that if everyone just lived by her book, the world would be perfect — which is a terrifyingly naive kind of danger in a death match. 7th Prince Luzurus is essentially the "stoner prince" of the family. He’s the benefactor of the Cha-R Mafia family and is primarily focused on the illegal drug trade, but surprisingly, he’s one of the few princes who seems to have a genuine moral compass, even showing concern for the younger siblings. His Guardian Spirit Beast is a Conjuration-type that looks like a giant centipede with a humanoid head. It’s a pseudo-coercive manipulator that works by setting a trap: it materializes a physical object that its target strongly desires. Once the target interacts with that object or gives in to their desire, the beast's manipulation kicks in, making them vulnerable to Luzurus's influence. While Luzurus has been relatively laid back during the voyage so far, his beast is constantly working in the background to convert the people around him into loyal subjects without him even needing to lift a finger. 8th Prince Salé-salé is the ultimate party prince who spends the voyage surrounded by women and loud music, seemingly ignoring the death match entirely. Despite his lazy attitude, he’s surprisingly confident, telling his mother that he has a grand plan to win. His Guardian Spirit Beast is a Manipulator-type that looks like a giant, multi-faced ball of fat. It emits a specialized smoke that, when inhaled, places a "mark" over the victim's head. As the victim spends more time in the smoke, their affection for Salé-salé grows until they become a mindless, loyal puppet. However, because his beast is so loud and obvious, it made him an easy target. He becomes the second prince to die in the contest when Prince Benjamin’s private soldier, Rihan, uses a specialized Nen ability called "Predator" to materialize a creature that literally eats Salé-salé’s Spirit Beast. Once he was defenseless, another of Benjamin’s men simply walked in and finished him off. 9th Prince Halkenburg is perhaps the most morally interesting prince in the entire arc. A brilliant scholar and pacifist, he originally tried to withdraw from the war entirely, but King Nasubi told him there is no way out: either he wins, or he and everyone he loves perishes. Forced into a corner, he awakens to a terrifying Symbiotic Party-type power. His Guardian Spirit Beast marks his loyal guards with feathers; when they all gather and unify their will, they create a collective aura so overwhelming it feels like an entirely different level of power. He channels this collective power into a specialized Nen ability called "Grimmel the Dissonance". This ability is essentially an unblockable soul-cannon: when he fires an arrow, the soul of one of his loyal followers is launched into the body of an enemy, overwriting the target's consciousness. He used this power to take over the body of Balsamilco, Prince Benjamin’s top advisor and a skilled military strategist. Now, Halkenburg’s mind is inside Balsamilco’s body, allowing him to manipulate the war from the highest level of Benjamin’s inner circle — a move that has turned the former pacifist into the most dangerous threat on the ship. 10th Prince Kacho is loud, brash, and uses her terrible personality as a survival strategy. She acts like a spoiled brat to make herself unlikeable, but it’s all a facade to protect her twin sister, Fugetsu. Together, they are the only princes who truly love each other, and they hatched a plan to escape the Black Whale entirely using a magic tunnel created by Fugetsu's Guardian Spirit Beast. However, they quickly learned the hard way that the Succession Contest is a cursed ritual you can’t just walk away from — the moment they tried to flee in a lifeboat, a swarm of mysterious Nen hands emerged from the ocean to stop them. In a heartbreaking twist, Kacho sacrificed herself to ensure Fugetsu made it back to the ship. This is where her Guardian Spirit Beast, called "Without You," finally revealed its true form. It’s a post-mortem ability that only activates upon the death of one of the twins; it took on Kacho’s exact appearance, memories, and personality to stay by Fugetsu’s side. Now, Fugetsu is back on the ship, unaware that the "Kacho" next to her is actually a Nen ghost dedicated to protecting her until the very end. 11th Prince Fugetsu is gentle and sweet and completely in over her head — except that her Guardian Spirit Beast has an ability that nobody expected. Her Guardian Spirit Beast is a Mutual-type that manifests as a Magical Worm. It creates a teleportation tunnel with two doors: Fugetsu can create the inbound door to travel to a destination, while Kacho creates the outbound door to travel back. This allowed the twins to secretly meet and plan their escape despite being in separate rooms. After their failed escape attempt, Fugetsu is being haunted by malevolent evil spirits. She is currently frail, sickly, and being kept alive only by the protection of "Ghost Kacho" and a rotating team of bodyguards including Melody. 12th Prince Momoze was the first tragic casualty of the Succession Contest. Despite being a talented girl who loved to knit, she was essentially abandoned by her own mother, Queen Sevanti, who prioritized the safety of Momoze's younger brother, Marayam, by stripping Momoze of almost all her loyal bodyguards. This left her vulnerable and exhausted, as her own Guardian Spirit Beast — a giant, multi-armed mouse — was secretly draining her energy to function. Because she was physically depleted from her beast’s high aura cost, Momoze was fast asleep when she was attacked. She was ultimately suffocated in her bed by Tuffdy, one of the guards who had been assigned to her but was actually working for Queen Swinko-swinko. Her death served as a brutal wake-up call for the readers, proving that being innocent or uninterested in the throne wouldn't protect anyone from the cold-blooded reality of the Black Whale. 13th Prince Marayam is a young child whose Guardian Spirit Beast has been quietly growing since the voyage began, especially after Momoze's death. While Marayam remains an oblivious kid playing with his pet hamster, his Guardian Spirit Beast has reacted to the surrounding danger by becoming a massive, multi-faced dragon-like creature. The beast’s true power is a "Space-Shifting" ability: it has essentially moved Marayam’s entire living quarters into a parallel dimension. To anyone walking down the hallway, Room 1013 looks normal, but anyone who enters is actually trapped in a "nen space" where they can’t leave or communicate with the outside world. This makes Marayam almost impossible to assassinate because his physical body isn't technically even on the ship anymore. 14th Prince Woble is a literal baby and the youngest of all the princes. She's the one Kurapika ends up guarding after his attempt to get close to Tserriednich goes sideways. Woble can't fight, can't strategize, and can't even talk — but her Guardian Spirit Beast is something nobody has managed to fully analyze yet. Even Benjamin's spy Babimyna, who is specifically stationed to figure it out, can't get a read on it. That mystery alone makes everyone nervous.

* **The Royal Bodyguards**

Now this is where Kurapika really starts to take center stage in the arc. He applies to become a bodyguard for what he thinks is Prince Halkenburg's listing as part of his plan to get closer to Prince Tserriednich. But he ends up with the 8th Queen Oito, mother of baby Prince Woble, instead. Oito is one of the lower queens, with no resources and a baby prince who has almost no chance in the succession war. She fills Kurapika in on the full reality of what the succession war is — an actual deathmatch with one survivor. Once the voyage starts and bodies start appearing in Woble's quarters, Kurapika realizes nobody else among Woble's guards knows anything about Nen. He springs into action and makes a bold move that changes the dynamic of the entire succession war: he broadcasts a message across every channel on Tier 1, informing all the princes that Nen exists, that their Guardian Spirit Beasts are real, and that they need to understand this. He then opens a two-week Nen training course in Room 1014 and invites the bodyguards of all the princes to attend. This is a massive strategic play. Kurapika's goal is to create a stalemate — by giving the weaker, lower-ranked princes' guards the ability to use Nen, he raises the cost of targeting them. No prince or their bodyguards can pick off the lower princes for free anymore. It's not just charity; it's Kurapika buying time and information while trying to get close to Tserriednich. He also uses Emperor Time — his terrifying Nen ability that forces him into a mode where all his chains become fully powered but at the cost of shortening his lifespan for every second he uses it. He uses Steal Chain and Stealth Dolphin to forcibly awaken Nen in others, which is how he runs his training course. Before the voyage, he recruits a team of hunters and sends them to apply as bodyguards for different princes. Hanzo ends up guarding 12th Prince Momoze. Biscuit ends up with 13th Prince Marayam. Basho lands with 7th Prince Luzurus. Melody attaches herself to 10th Prince Kacho. And Izunavi takes on 6th Prince Tyson. Each of them becomes a crucial intelligence node in Kurapika's network, feeding him information about the princes they're watching while he works the Nen lessons. The Nen Information War — as it essentially becomes — is a constant battle of observation and counterintelligence. Benjamin plants his soldier Babimyna in Room 1014 as a spy. Tubeppa sends Longhi and Maor. Zhang Lei sends Sakata and Hashito to help Kurapika in exchange for information. Every single participant in those lessons has a hidden agenda, and Kurapika knows it since he manages the whole thing anyway.

* **Phantom Troupe and Hisoka**

Before the voyage even begins, the Phantom Troupe and Hisoka have their long-awaited rematch at Heavens Arena. Chrollo, having finally gotten Skill Hunter back, shows up as a Floor Master and proceeds to fight Hisoka in one of the most anticipated fights in the series. Using a combination of borrowed abilities — Shalnark's Black Voice, Kortopi's Gallery Fake, Order Stamp, The Sun and Moon, Convert Hands, and Double Face — Chrollo basically turns the entire arena into a weapon. He fills the crowd with puppets marked with explosive seals and uses them to overwhelm Hisoka. Hisoka loses a hand and a leg before a massive explosion brings the fight to an end. He's declared dead at the scene. Except Hisoka doesn't actually die. He uses post-mortem Bungee Gum to restart his own heart, coming back to life while Machi is literally standing there about to treat his corpse. He wakes up, restrains her with Bungee Gum, tells her he's going to kill every single Spider, and walks out. He tracks down Kortopi and Shalnark almost immediately after, killing them both before they have any idea what's happening. He leaves Shalnark's body tied to a swing with Kortopi's head at his feet. It's genuinely one of the most disturbing scenes in the series. Chrollo's plan in the meantime is to board the Black Whale with the Phantom Troupe to steal Kakin's treasures. So the entire Troupe gets on the ship, and now you have Hisoka hunting the Troupe and the Troupe hunting Hisoka, both of them tearing across the lower decks of a ship with two hundred thousand civilians on it. On board, the Troupe establishes a base of operations in the Cha-R family's territory on Tier 5 after Chrollo makes it clear that finding Hisoka is their first priority. Chrollo, Shizuku, and Bonolenov work together, though Chrollo insists he'll be the one to kill Hisoka when they find him. Bonolenov walks around in disguise as Hisoka, trying to mislead the mafia into thinking they've already found him so the Troupe can search freely. Chrollo himself wanders the lower tiers using an ability called Love Dial 6700, trying to locate a specific Nen user with an ability that can take Hisoka down for good — but keeps coming up empty. 

* **The Mafia War**

The lower decks of the Black Whale are essentially mafia territory. Three families have carved up the ship's lower tiers between them, and each of them is tied to a different royal family member. The Xi-Yu family is run by Onior Longbao, who is actually one of King Nasubi's half-brothers. They back 3rd Prince Zhang Lei. Their top enforcer on the ship is Hinrigh Biganduffno, a young Xi-Yu lieutenant with a Nen ability called Biohazard that lets him transform objects into living things and living things into objects — incredibly versatile. The Cha-R family is led by Brocco Li, another of Nasubi's half-brothers. They're aligned with 7th Prince Luzurus. Their leadership on the ship falls to Ken'i Wang, a skilled fighter, and their consultant Ittoku. The Heil-Ly family is the wildcard that breaks everything. Backed by 4th Prince Tserriednich — at least officially — the Heil-Ly is actually being run by a young woman named Morena Prudo, who is Nasubi's illegitimate daughter. And Morena's agenda has nothing to do with Tserriednich's goals. Her Nen ability, Contagion, lets her grant other people Nen abilities by infecting them through a kiss, with abilities growing stronger the more people they kill. She's essentially building an army of newly powered killers on the lower decks, and she actively wants chaos. The Heil-Ly starts murdering Cha-R and Xi-Yu members indiscriminately, breaking every code of conduct the mafia operates on. The mafia's code is strict — you can't hit another family without notifying your boss first, your boss contacts the other boss within 24 hours, and compensation is arranged. Morena just ignores all of that entirely since she's building a revolution. Her real identity and goals become one of the biggest revelations of the arc. Morena was actually born as a “Carnival Orphan” — basically kids born from non-consensual encounters during Kakin’s royal festivals. About three years before the story, she killed the original Morena Prudo — an illegitimate royal with a bit more status — and took her name and position, becoming the new boss of the Heil-Ly. From there, her goal isn’t just power — it’s total chaos. She wants to tear down the entire Kakin Empire from the inside, wipe out the royal bloodline, and on a bigger scale… just end humanity altogether. So while the princes are out here fighting over the throne, Morena’s playing a different game entirely — she’s trying to burn the whole room down with everyone still inside. This chaos brought by the Mafia war spills into the Phantom Troupe's mission almost immediately. The three families end up in a situation where the Xi-Yu and Cha-R are officially asking the Phantom Troupe to crush the Heil-Ly for them, in exchange for access to the upper tiers to search for Hisoka. Nobunaga, Phinks, and Feitan eventually track the Heil-Ly to a phantom room in Tier 3 using a tracker created by Hinrigh. There, they discover a teleportation trap designed to keep the hideout unreachable, setting the stage for a deadly game of hide-and-seek between the world's greatest thieves and a cult of Nen-using serial killers.

* **Beyond’s True Goal**

This is the twist that reframes everything. During the Nen lessons in Room 1014, Tubeppa's guard Longhi reveals that she can already use Nen but she's been hiding it the entire time. She isn't just some informant for Tubeppa. Longhi is actually one of Beyond Netero's children. She explains that about 30 years ago, Beyond convinced some young Kakin officers to get into fake marriages and were tasked with raising his children as their own. These kids were Nen users from birth and were later sent to the Royal Military Academy where most of them excelled. She suspects that Beyond did this every time a new Prince was born, and the plan was for his children to become Royal Guards for the princes. Beyond’s endgame was to get his kids into important positions around the next king. But here’s a dark twist. Beyond puts a curse on his children in the form of an eye-like seal under their tongue which can be seen with Gyo. Longhi’s purpose, along with Beyond’s other children, was to be a living weapon and sacrificial pawn for Beyond’s goals, likely meant to assassinate a certain prince. Longhi believes Beyond’s bigger plan is to wrap up the Succession War quickly and put a prince of his choosing on the throne. Kurapika thinks that prince might be Prince Benjamin, but Longhi disagrees. She suggests that one of the Kakin Princes is actually Beyond’s child, and that’s the one he wants to see crowned. With his child as the king, he can use the entire Kakin Empire’s money and resources to fund his real obsession — conquering the Dark Continent. He's been influencing the battle from outside the ship, from inside a jail cell, the entire time. Cheadle told the Zodiacs they needed to get to the Dark Continent before Beyond — and he's been playing his real game on the chessboard they never even saw. Longhi then makes her own agenda clear: if Beyond's child is among the princes, she intends to kill them herself — both to stop Beyond's plan and to free herself from the curse that would otherwise claim her life. She asks Kurapika for help identifying the child, proposing a formal contract between Tubeppa and Woble's sides that carries real Nen-enforced consequences for betrayal. Kurapika, after verifying she isn't lying using his Dowsing Chain, accepts the alliance.

* **The Current State of Play**

Let's catch up on the most explosive things that have happened as of where the manga currently stands. Benjamin vs. Halkenburg becomes one of the defining conflicts of the arc's second half. Benjamin had originally tasked Balsamilco with a mission to assassinate Halkenburg using a very specific, untraceable poison. Balsamilco kept this poison in his pocket, waiting for the perfect window to use it. During the confrontation, Halkenburg fired his unblockable arrow at Balsamilco. The ability successfully swapped a soul from Halkenburg's side into Balsamilco’s body. By sheer chance or fate, it was Halkenburg’s own consciousness that was injected into Balsamilco. When Halkenburg successfully took over Balsamilco’s body, he found the poison. He realized that for the ruse to work, Benjamin had to believe Balsamilco actually did his job. So, while inhabiting Balsamilco's body, Halkenburg poisoned his own original physical body. Benjamin was officially notified that Halkenburg died of a "sudden illness". He even authorized a funeral procession for his fallen brother, completely unaware that his most trusted advisor is now his greatest enemy in disguise. Chrollo's plan to steal Kakin's sacred treasures is a massive wrinkle nobody saw coming. Chrollo figures out during the voyage that the royal family is already aware of the Phantom Troupe's presence on board but isn't doing much about it — which tells him the things he wants are protected somewhere on the upper tiers. Kakin has three sacred treasures that are tied to the succession ritual itself. He wants to steal them to upgrade Skill Hunter — to add abilities of such profound power that they'd give him the edge he needs to finally kill Hisoka for good. Meanwhile, Benjamin declaring Martial Law is the arc's most recent political earthquake. After Halkenburg's death is announced, Benjamin begins laying the groundwork for martial law. In his internal monologue, he acknowledges that he has been poisoned and has approximately 10 hours left to live. It is heavily implied that Halkenburg, in Balsamilco's body, is the one responsible. Benjamin has declared Martial Law and is attempting to eliminate the remaining princes quickly so he can secure the throne for his illegitimate child before he dies. With multiple mafia families at war, with Hisoka and the Phantom Troupe tearing through the lower decks, and with Benjamin declaring martial law — things are only getting messier.
