**Every Forbidden Jutsu (Kinjutsu) in Naruto Explained**

* **Iwagakure**

This forbidden jutsu of Iwagakure, or the Village Hidden in the Stones, was a jutsu used by Deidara of the Akatsuki. This technique lets the user mold their chakra into materials, giving them the power to shape, control, and even change the size of objects \- from super tiny to absolutely massive. Once the chakra is infused, the object can be used for different jutsu. Deidara stole this technique while searching for the ultimate art, and he mixed it with his Explosion Release kekkei genkai to create his signature Explosive Clay. Deidara also used this kinjutsu in his battle against Sasuke with his ultimate C0 technique, which basically uses ones own body as a bomb and meant as a last-ditch effort. Deidara shows off this stitched-up mouth on his chest, which he unseals to feed a big chunk of clay. Since the mouth is linked directly to his heart, it starts building up a ton of chakra while continuously pulling energy straight from his heart’s meridians right up until detonation. His chakra then spreads through his veins which then compress into a sphere that cracks open and eventually blows up, producing a deadly explosion covering a ten-kilometer blast radius. Sekiei, a Fabrication created from the genetic material of a White Zetsu, also got the nature transformation that grants him access to Explosion Release after being implanted with Explosive Clay, also allowing him to use this jutsu in a similar way. The mouths on Deidara’s hands and chest are likely a result of using this forbidden jutsu, though Sekiei didn’t have those.

* **Earth Grudge Fear**

Earth Grudge Fear, is a kinjutsu from Takigakure, or the Village Hideen in the Waterfalls, and was stolen by Kakuzu of the Akatsuki before leaving the village. Earth Grudge Fear basically turns the user's body into something like a rag doll held together by thick, black threads. With it, Kakuzu could patch up any injuries on himself or others, and even reattach severed limbs with it. He could also separate parts of his own body, control them remotely, and put them back together when needed. But these threads weren’t just for healing \- they could also tear open his target’s body, steal their organs, and merge them into his own to extend his life. Kakuzu usually used this technique to steal hearts, keeping up to four extra ones stored in the masks on his back, which gave him access to different elemental jutsu including fire, lightning, water, and wind. He could even release these hearts from his body, letting them form massive, thread-covered limbs or monstrous creatures with the masks as their faces. These masks could move and fight on their own, launching powerful elemental chakra attacks. If Kakuzu was killed, one of his spare hearts could jump back into his body and revive him, so you’d have to kill him at least five times in a fight to really take him down. One of his hearts was pierced by Kakashi’s Lightning Cutter, the second one was unintentionally destroyed by Hidan, and his three remaining hearts destroyed by Naruto. With no usable heart left, Kakashi was able to finish him off.

* **Reverse Lotus**

Techniques that can seriously harm the user are often considered forbidden, and Reverse Lotus is one of those. Rock Lee learned it from his mentor, Might Guy, but it’s meant to be used only as a last resort because of how dangerous it is. This move requires opening at least three of the Eight Gates, which takes off the body's natural limits and pushes the user to insane speeds. But it comes with a big risk \- the technique exhausts the user to the point that they can hardly move or even stay conscious afterward. Once the gates are opened, the user becomes so fast it’s hard to even see their movements, attacking the target from all sides and launching them into the air. When Rock Lee used this against Gaara, even Gaara's automatic Sand Shield couldn’t keep up. Lee’s attacks wrapped Gaara in his bandages which allowedRock Lee to position him for a final blow that sent him crashing into the ground. The sheer speed and strength of the Reverse Lotus make it incredibly powerful, but if the enemy survives, the user is left completely vulnerable.

* **Cursed Seals of Heaven and Earth**

Orochimaru’s cursed seals of heaven and Earth, is a more twisted version of an existing jutsu. What makes his cursed seal special is that it has Jugo’s cells in it. Jugo has the ability to absorb natural energy and transform his body for battle. Orochimaru experimented with this ability to create different versions of the cursed seal. The most complete versions were the Cursed Seal of Heaven and the Cursed Seal of Earth. The Cursed Seal of Heaven gives its user a massive chakra boost, likely powered by Senjutsu, and enhances their physical abilities. Orochimaru gave Sasuke this seal during the second phase of the Chunin Exams, and when Sasuke activated its second level, he was as strong as Naruto’s one-tailed form. Anko also had this seal when she was Orochimaru’s student, and he briefly reactivated it when they fought in the Forest of Death before Hiruzen sealed it again. The Cursed Seal of Earth, which was given to Kimimaro, boosted his chakra, physical strength and ninjutsu. At the second level, he could even merge with his bone blades, letting him launch surprise attacks from his enemy’s blind spots, and his healing improved, with torn flesh instantly regenerating when he transformed.

* **Multi Shadow Clone Jutsu**

Multi Shadow Clone Jutsu, is a powerful jutsu created by Tobirama Senju from the Hidden Leaf Village. It’s basically a supercharged version of the regular Shadow Clone Jutsu. Instead of making just a few clones, the user can pump out hundreds or even thousands. While it’s really strong, it also drains a ton of chakra, so it’s not safe for most shinobi. Because of that, the First Hokage made it forbidden and hid it away in the Scroll of Seals. Naruto, however, is one of the few who can handle it because of his insane chakra reserves. Most people can only make up to five clones with the regular jutsu, but Naruto can make dozens, hundreds, or even a thousand at once\! While smaller groups of clones are useful for all kinds of tasks, Naruto’s huge clone army is mostly used for overwhelming his enemies in battle. He also uses it to speed up his training, like when he mastered the Wind Release: Rasengan way faster than normal. This is possible because each clone is capable of independently learning, so all the combined experience of the clones expedited his training. By the time Naruto became Hokage, he was using this technique on a massive scale, helping him handle a bunch of tasks around the village at the same time. 

* **Mutually Multiplying Explosive Tags**

Mutually Multiplying Explosive Tags, is another deadly jutsu invented by Tobirama Senju. It’s a terrifying technique that’s usually paired with Edo Tensei, the Reanimation Jutsu. This jutsu is designed for those brought back to life with Edo Tensei, using special explosive tags to create an endless chain of explosions. The reincarnated individual’s body acts as a medium for the paper, which explodes and creates even more tags in the process. So it basically turns their bodies into paper bombs that keep on detonating nearly infinitely due to the regenerative property of the Edo Tensei bodies. This leads to a continuous loop of explosions which can be nearly impossible to escape. Although live individuals can use this technique, it’s incredibly dangerous and usually results in sacrificing their own life, which is why it’s classified as forbidden. 

* **Rasenshuriken**

The Rasenshuriken is one of Naruto’s signature moves, which he developed with the help of Kakashi. It’s an upgrade of the Rasengan, adding Wind Release to make it even more powerful. When Naruto first started using it, the Rasenshuriken caused serious damage to both his enemy and his own arm, leading Tsunade to warn him not to use it again. But Naruto eventually found a way around this by adding sage chakra that allows him to throw it as a projectile, which kept him from hurting himself. Once thrown, the Rasenshuriken can even expand to hit dodging targets. When Naruto uses his Nine-Tails Chakra Mode, he can guide it with his chakra arms from a safe distance. He’s also able to modify the Rasenshuriken in different ways, like making multiple ones at once, or changing its size. Instead of a Rasengan at the core, he can sometimes use Tailed Beast Balls or Truth-Seeking Balls for an even more devastating explosion. Plus, he can apply other nature transformations to it, like he does with the Super Tailed Beast Rasenshuriken.

* **Twin Snake Mutual Death Jutsu**

The Twin Snake Mutual Death Jutsu, is a technique taught to Anko by Orochimaru back when she was his student. It’s basically a double-suicide move. To use it, the user grabs the target's hand and forces them to help perform the hand seals. Once that’s done, both the user and the target are supposed to die. When Anko tries to use it on Orochimaru, she stabs his hand to hers so he can’t escape while she starts the hand seals. But it turns out Orochimaru was using a shadow clone and Anko stops the jutsu just in time before she ends up killing herself. We never actually see the jutsu work in the series, but in the official guidebook, it’s shown as two snakes wrapping around the user and target, biting each other’s necks before detonating.

* **One's Own Life Reincarnation**

One's Own Life Reincarnation, is a Jutsu created by the Puppet Brigade in Sunagakure and allows the user to give life to others by using the user’s life force. Originally, it was meant to bring puppets to life to make them better in battle. To perform this, the user channels all their chakra as a medium and transfers their life force to the target. It can be used on both the living and the dead, but with different outcomes. If used on someone who's still alive, it can heal them from a fatal injury, although it leaves the user completely drained. If used on the dead, the user will die in exchange for reviving the other person. Chiyo from the Hidden Sand Village is the only one we've seen use this technique. She brought Gaara back to life after the Akatsuki killed him by extracting the One-Tail Shukaku from him. Feeling guilty for sealing Shukaku inside Gaara in the first place, Chiyo used this technique to atone. However, she didn’t have enough life force to fully revive him on her own. Naruto stepped in and lent her his chakra, allowing her to save Gaara, though it cost Chiyo her life. Since Chiyo was the only known user of this technique, it’s believed to have been lost when she died.

* **Strength of a Hundred Seal**

Strength of a Hundred Seal, is a powerful jutsu that dates back to Hagoromo's era, and both Tsunade and Sakura are well-known for using it. The technique allows medical ninjas to store chakra in one spot over a long period of time, and then unleash it all at once when needed. But it’s tough to pull off because it requires super-precise chakra control to build up and store that chakra. Typically, the chakra is stored in a specific point on the body,usually the forehead, forming a rhombus-shaped seal. Sakura took about three years to store enough chakra to create her seal, which was impressive considering how hard it is to master. Once released, the seal spreads across the user's face or wraps around their body, pumping the stored chakra into them and boosting their power significantly. The seal stays permanently on the user’s forehead, allowing them to use its effects whenever they need. It’s also linked to Lady Katsuyu, the giant slug summon, allowing the user to perform mass healing or communicate with the slug over long distances. The strength of Katsuyu’s abilities directly depends on how much chakra is stored in the seal. Tsunade and Sakura use this stored chakra with the Byakugou no Jutsu (Creation Rebirth), which heals their injuries automatically with no hand signs needed. The seal also amps up their physical abilities, as seen when Sakura took on the Ten-Tails' clones during the war. Tsunade also taps into the seal’s chakra to maintain her youthful appearance. 

* **Fury**

Fury is an anime-exclusive technique so powerful it can create a massive explosion big enough to wipe out an entire village. It was created by En no Gyoja, and his granddaughter Hotaru had the technique implanted on her back. To activate the technique, the user first has to gather a ton of chakra using the Tsuchigumo Style: Forbidden Life Technique Release: Creation of Heaven and Earth. Because of how destructive it could be, the Third Hokage labeled it as a forbidden technique to protect the village. Even though Fury is insanely powerful, Utakata managed to absorb all its power and destroy it by using his Tailed Beast Mode. To keep everyone safe after Fury was declared a kinjutsu, the Third Hokage and En no Gyoja agreed on a treaty that Konoha would help the Tsuchigumo Clan in times of need.

* **Living Corpse Reincarnation**

Living Corpse Reincarnation, is a kinjutsu created by Orochimaru to transfer his soul into someone else's body, making him essentially immortal. The idea is that he moves into a new, stronger body before his current one starts to break down. By doing this every few years, he can keep living as long as he keeps finding suitable hosts. Orochimaru can only use this technique once every three years, though. As his time in a host body nears the end of the three years, the body starts rejecting him, and he has to switch to a new one. But if he found the perfect host, he could theoretically stay in that body for a lifetime. This explains why he was so focused on preparing Sasuke and Kimimaro as potential hosts. When Orochimaru is ready to perform the transfer, he sheds his current body to reveal his true form \- a huge white snake made up of smaller snakes. He then swallows the new host and takes them to a mental plane where he essentially overtakes their mind. The snake body dies and Orochimaru's soul takes over the new body. The souls of his previous hosts remain inside him, still "alive" in a way, but suppressed. These souls can potentially resurface if Orochimaru’s chakra gets too low, as seen when Sasuke used up most of his chakra and Orochimaru almost reappeared using the Eight Branches Technique. Once he’s in a new body, Orochimaru modifies it to align with his snake-like abilities, letting him stretch, heal quickly, and even reconnect severed body parts. This lets him survive longer if he can’t find a new host right away. After Sasuke revived him using the Evil Releasing Method, which involved Kabuto’s modified flesh, Orochimaru bypassed the usual process and transferred himself into a White Zetsu clone.

* **Izanami**

Izanami is one of the most powerful and forbidden jutsu in Uchiha history, and using it comes with a huge price \- you lose the sight in one of your eyes forever. It's a genjutsu that traps the target in a loop of events, making them relive the same moments over and over. The trick is that this loop is based on shared physical sensations between the user and the target. Itachi Uchiha used Izanami during his fight with Kabuto in the Fourth Great Ninja War. Once the jutsu is activated, the target is stuck in the loop, replaying the same events in their mind. Even if they realize what's happening, they can't escape it by trying to act differently because the illusion adapts to their changes and keeps them stuck. The only way out of this infinite loop is for the target to accept the reality of their actions and come to terms with their fate. It's a harsh jutsu that forces the target to face their choices and learn from them \- otherwise, they're trapped forever.

* **Izanagi**

Izanagi, another dojutsu from the Uchiha clan, is the counterpart to Izanami, but with a totally different purpose. Instead of trapping the target in a loop, Izanagi lets the user rewrite reality itself, allowing them to turn an illusion into reality. Most Uchiha use this technique to survive fatal injuries or even death, basically “undoing” the injury by turning it into an illusion and replacing it with a non-injured version of themselves. But like Izanami, using Izanagi comes with the cost of permanently losing sight in the Sharingan eye used to perform it. Madara Uchiha used Izanagi to undo his death after his fight with Hashirama, having sealed a time-activated Izanagi in his right eye. This revived him and swapped his real body with a shadow clone. Since Uchiha only have half of the Sage’s chakra, they can usually only keep Izanagi going for a few seconds. Danzo, who had both Uchiha and Senju DNA implanted in him, could use Izanagi for one minute. He also had ten Sharingan implanted in his right arm, allowing him to stretch this technique for up to ten minutes. However, since he wasn’t an Uchiha, each use of Izanagi drained a lot of his chakra. Obito Uchiha, with better control over his Senju cells, could maintain Izanagi for much longer. Izanami was actually created as a way to counter and punish those who would abuse Izanagi.

* **Reaper Death Seal**

The Reaper Death Seal is a powerful but deadly jutsu created by the Uzumaki clan that calls upon the Shinigami (Death God) to seal away a target. The catch is, The caster also loses their soul in the process, making it a serious last resort. Minato Namikaze first used it when Obito Uchiha attacked the Leaf Village, sealing half of the Nine-Tails inside himself. Hiruzen Sarutobi also used it during Orochimaru's invasion to seal away Orochimaru’s arms and stop him from using any more jutsu. Once the summoner finishes the hand seals, the Shinigami shows up and creates a cursed seal that gives the summoner the power to trap a target's soul. The Shinigami's hand comes out of the summoner’s body and grabs the target's soul, freezing them in place and canceling any jutsu they’re using. With the target immobilized, the summoner can pull out and seal their soul, but the target's soul ends up locked inside the summoner’s body. Once it’s done, the Shinigami slowly devours the summoner’s soul, leading to their death. Neither the summoner nor the target’s soul moves on to the afterlife; instead, they're trapped in the Shinigami's stomach forever, battling each other for eternity. The only way to free a soul from the Shinigami is by using the Reaper Death Seal: Release. For this, someone has to wear the Shinigami Mask, which causes them to be possessed by the Shinigami. If they cut their stomach open, the Shinigami does the same, freeing any souls trapped inside. However, there's a downside: the mask doesn’t protect the user from dying from their own injury. When Orochimaru used this to restore his arms, he survived by switching bodies again. After releasing the other souls, Orochimaru was even able to perform the Reanimation Jutsu.

* **Eight Inner Gates**

The Eight Inner Gates are chakra points along the body’s chakra pathway system that act as limiters. These gates are there to prevent people from using too much chakra and damaging their own bodies. But with the right training, a person can learn to unlock \- or "open" \- the gates. Each gate they open allows more chakra to flow through them, boosting their strength and speed. However, opening the gates comes with a price. It takes a toll on the body and even the first few gates can cause fatigue or injury. In the worst case, like opening the final gate, the user dies because their body can’t handle the immense chakra flowing through them. Only a few people in the series have been able to open all Eight Gates. Might Duy spent 20 years training to master them and he passed on a strict rule to his son, Guy, to only open the gates when protecting someone important enough to risk your life for. Guy then passed this rule on to his own student, Rock Lee. Duy sacrificed himself by opening the Eighth Gate \- known as the Gate of Death \- to save Guy from the Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Mist. This gate is located in the heart, and to open it, users puncture their chest with their thumb to force chakra there. Once opened, their heart pumps at maximum capacity to give them power a hundred times greater than their usual strength, which is even stronger than the Five Kage combined. But it’s a deadly power \- eventually, the user's body heats up so much that they burn from the inside out and turn to ash. Guy used the Eighth Gate during his fight against Madara, who had just gained the Six Paths powers and became seemingly unbeatable. But Guy nearly killed him by unlocking this final gate and accessing high-level taijutsu like the Evening Elephant and Night Guy. Luckily for Madara, Guy’s body succumbs to the overwhelming power of the Eight Gates before he could finish him off. Naruto later saves Guy from death with Yin-Yang Release, but the loss of his leg and the damage to his body leave him in a wheelchair for the rest of his life. As for Rock Lee, he also mastered all Eight Gates by adulthood.

* **Impure World Reincarnation**

The Impure World Reincarnation aka Edo Tensei is a forbidden technique that brings the dead back to life. It was originally created by Tobirama Senju but he never fully completed it. Orochimaru later found the jutsu and improved it, while Kabuto nearly perfected it. To perform this jutsu, the user needs some DNA from the person they want to bring back, which they smear on a special scroll. The soul of the dead person also needs to be in the Pure Land and a living sacrifice is required as the vessel for the reincarnated soul. Once the jutsu is activated, the sacrifice’s body gets covered in dust and ash, transforming them into the person being revived. The process is painful for the sacrifice, and although they’re still technically alive, their body is no longer their own. Once the reincarnated soul is released, the sacrifice dies. Reincarnated individuals can be summoned in two ways: they can either travel to the location on foot or be brought in via coffins that appear wherever needed. When first summoned, they look a little decayed, but the summoner can restore their appearance and plant a control talisman to give them full command over the reincarnated soul. The revived person keeps all their powers, including kekkei genkai, but some limitations exist \- like with Madara who couldn’t use his full abilities after being reincarnated. The summoner can control how much free will the reincarnated person has. At the highest level of control, their personality is wiped out completely and they act like puppets. Orochimaru likes to use this full control, while Kabuto sometimes switches to it when needed. One of the biggest advantages of this jutsu is that it makes the revived person immortal. They can regenerate from any damage and keep fighting even while they’re healing. Tobirama originally designed the technique for kamikaze-like attacks, and he even made the Mutually Multiplying Explosive Tags for this purpose. Killing the summoner won’t stop the jutsu, but there are three ways to end it: removing the reincarnated soul using the Reaper Death Seal, having the summoner stop it, or allowing the reincarnated person to gain closure and freeing themselves from the jutsu’s control. 

* **Rinne Rebirth**

Rinne Rebirth, is a super powerful and forbidden Jutsu that can only be used by someone with the Rinnegan. It’s accessed through the Outer Path, which is the seventh and final path of Pain. What makes this jutsu so formidable is that it lets the user bring the dead back to life, not like Edo Tensei where they’re revived as controlled reanimations, but as actual living humans with full control of themselves. But there’s a catch. The user has to give up their own life in exchange for bringing someone else back. When the jutsu is activated, the King of Hell appears and releases the souls of the dead from its mouth, even pulling them back from the brink of the afterlife. These souls are then returned to their bodies, which are fully healed. We don’t know all the limits of this technique, but Pain (Nagato) was able to use it to bring back hundreds of people at once during his attack on Konoha, including Kakashi. The revived people seem to come back with full energy, as Kakashi was back in action right after being revived and even helped carry Naruto. Later, during the Fourth Great Ninja War, Obito used Rinne Rebirth to completely revive Madara who was previously reincarnated through Edo Tensei. Obito survived the technique temporarily thanks to Black Zetsu attaching to him. Naruto also helped stabilize Obito’s life force for a short time using his Six Paths Yang Power, just like he did for Might Guy after the Eight Gates. Obito hung on long enough to bring Sasuke back and fight against Kaguya, but eventually sacrificed himself to save Kakashi, Naruto, and Sasuke.
