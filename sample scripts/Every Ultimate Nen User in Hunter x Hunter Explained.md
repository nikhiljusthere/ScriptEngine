**Every Ultimate Nen User in Hunter x Hunter Explained**

1. **Isaac Netero**

In the world of Nen, there are four levels of mastery for each type: Great, Excellent, Genius, and Ultimate. This ranking system was officially revealed by series creator Yoshihiro Togashi during a 2022 exhibition through a memo. When someone reaches Ultimate level, they've basically maxed out their potential \- they're the absolute peak of what their Nen type can achieve. Out of all the incredibly powerful Nen users in the series, only seven have earned this Ultimate rating. For Enhancers, the top spot goes to none other than Isaac Netero, widely recognized as the most powerful Nen user in the world. This veteran Hunter served as the 12th Chairman of the Hunter Association and led the Exam Commission for the 287th Hunter Exam. He'd been training in martial arts his whole life, and at 46, he figured he'd reached his physical peak. So during one winter, he headed into the mountains for some serious soul-searching. While up there, something clicked. He realized how grateful he was for martial arts and everything it had given him. That gratitude hit him so hard that he made a wild vow \- he'd do ten thousand punches and prayers every single day. His routine was brutal. Focus his chi, put his hands together, say a prayer, then throw a punch. Rinse and repeat ten thousand times. On day one, each sequence took about five to six seconds, meaning he was punching for over eighteen hours straight. After finishing, he'd collapse from exhaustion, sleep it off, then do it all over again. After two years, something incredible happened. He finished all ten thousand punches and realized the sun was still up. Two more years passed, and by age 50, he could knock out his entire ritual in under an hour. Eventually, he returned to his original dojo. His training had transformed him \- he could now strike faster than sound itself. The dojo's grandmaster was so moved that he begged to be trained and offered to give up his title as Grand Master. Netero accepted, but only after asking for some food first. From that point on, the dojo started teaching Shingen-ryu kung fu. After becoming a Hunter, he joined the Pure Paladin Squad and went on two expeditions to the Dark Continent \- one official, one not so much. He made it back alive both times, which is saying something considering how dangerous that place is. Two of his friends, Zigg Zoldyck and Linne Horsdoeuvre, joined him on the unofficial trip. When his son Beyond (whose existence he kept secret) tried to do a similar expedition, Netero shut that down real quick. He ordered Beyond to never go to the Dark Continent again and convinced the V5 to make it completely off-limits, even for the Hunter Association. Eventually, his reputation earned him the position of 12th Chairman of the Hunter Association. He first shows up in the series during the Second Phase of the 287th Hunter Exam when the examiner decides to fail all the participants because she didn’t like the meal they made. He criticizes her attitude as an examiner and makes her create a new test that she has to participate in herself. After the issue is settled and all the passers board the airship to the next site, he introduces himself as Chairman of the Hunter Exam Selection Committee. While Gon and Killua are hanging out, he gets intrigued by the boys and pulls a little trick to make them think they're being watched from one direction, then appears from the opposite side. Killua immediately gets suspicious. He then invites both boys to play a game \- if they can take a ball from him, they'll become Hunters instantly. The kids give it everything they've got, but it's completely hopeless. Killua figures out just how skilled Netero is, realizes the old man hasn't even used his right arm or left leg the entire time, and quits. Gon keeps trying until Netero finally uses his right arm, which amuses the Chairman greatly. After the Third Phase wraps up, Netero announces to conduct an interview with the nine remaining applicants. When they reach a hotel owned by the Hunter Exam Selection Committee, Netero shows them a tournament bracket that leaves everyone scratching their heads. He explains that their placement is based on their exam performance, goes over the battle rules, and kicks off the Final Phase. He and the other examiners watch silently through every single match until the very end. A year after the 287th Hunter Exam, Netero gets word about a massive Chimera Ant colony threatening NGL. He puts together an Extermination Team with Knov and Morel. The three of them meet a seriously stressed-out Killua at the NGL border. Netero tells the kid they need to act fast with just a small group of elite Hunters to prevent assimilation. Before heading out, he gives Killua two halves of shogi tiles and invites him to fight two assassins in the nearest town for the other pieces if he wants to join them. The Extermination Team takes out at least four Chimera Ant squadrons using the same strategy every time. Morel creates a smoke screen, Knov transports the ants to his pocket dimension, and Netero picks them off one by one, starting with the Squadron Leaders. This setup also lets Netero recover some of his strength between fights. After the Chimera Ant King is born and the Queen dies, Netero leaves things in Morel's hands and goes to see an old friend. He hires this person to help separate the King from his Royal Guards when the time comes. Netero infiltrates East Gorteau solo and then texts the rest of the Extermination Team, which now includes Gon and Killua. His orders are simple \- split into pairs, with each duo luring away one Royal Guard so he can face the King one-on-one. He knows he can't win if even one of those incredibly powerful Royal Guards stays with the King. The plan goes into effect at midnight sharp the night before the rally. Until then, he meditates. On attack night, Netero and Zeno get flown above the palace by Silva Zoldyck. They jump off the dragon's back, with Zeno creating a Nen dragon to carry them. Neferpitou detects them, but as soon as they enter the Royal Guard's En range, Zeno activates Dragon Dive. Pitou manages to locate Netero and jumps toward him, activating Terpsichora. But in just 0.1 seconds, before the attack can land, they hear Netero mocking their bad move as he elegantly joins his hands together. Then Pitou gets blasted away by his 100-Type Guanyin Bodhisattva. After landing, either Zeno or Netero uses En to find the King and they start heading toward him. Since Komugi got injured during Dragon Dive, the King agrees to be taken away. Zeno activates Dragon Head and both the King and Netero ride it to another location. This is when we finally see the full power of Netero's ultimate Nen ability. His 100-Type Guanyin Bodhisattva summons a massive multi-armed Nen construct that looks like a gigantic Guanyin statue. To attack, Netero clasps his hands together like he's praying, then one of the statue's arms strikes down in sync with his hand motion. He does this before every single attack. Thanks to his incredible speed, the whole process takes way less than 0.1 seconds and can be repeated thousands of times per minute. Just one palm strike from the bodhisattva was enough to send Pitou flying without the Chimera Ant even understanding what hit them. The statue has so many arms that the possible attack combinations are basically infinite. Nobody knows if the construct is conjured or emitted, but it's probably made using Emission Nen and then strengthened with Enhancement. Manipulation is also involved through the hand gestures and stances that control and channel his attacks. When fighting Meruem, Netero starts with First Hand \- a chopping motion that makes the bodhisattva strike with a chop. It made the King spit blood and comment that he couldn't see the attack coming, plus it created a deep crack in the ground. When Meruem invites him to sit and talk, Netero retaliates with Third Hand \- a clapping motion that crushes the target between two palms. But Meruem comes out unscathed, pushing the palms apart instantly. Netero suddenly remembers that the King doesn't know his own name and suggests they fight \- if Meruem can make him admit defeat, he'll reveal it, since one of his underlings heard it from the Queen before she died. The King accepts, much to Netero's delight. Excited by the formal challenge, Netero takes to the air and activates Ninety-Ninth Hand. He clasps his hands together, rests his right ankle on his left calf, and joins his thumbs and index fingers to make a sign that looks like the number ninety-nine. The bodhisattva unleashes a barrage of palm strikes with all its arms \- a massively powerful attack that digs deep into the ground. He doesn't need to pray between blows and just maintains the starting pose. The ground caves in, and both fighters end up in an underground tomb. They start exchanging blows, with both fighters actually enjoying themselves. Meruem manages to slip through and cuts off Netero's right leg. The Chairman tightens his leg muscles to close the wound. Next, the King chops off his left arm. Despite the pain, Netero smirks and asks if Meruem thinks he needs two arms to pray. Then comes his ultimate attack \- Zero Hand. This is his absolute last resort, only used when none of Guanyin's abilities will work. Netero prays with everything he has, raising just one hand. The bodhisattva appears behind Meruem and gently clasps him within its palms. Then it focuses every last bit of Netero's aura in its mouth before firing it as an enormous blast of unmatched destructive power. This single attack hurts Meruem more than the thousands of palm strikes before it, though the damage is ultimately just superficial. After using it, Netero ages rapidly from his aura being completely drained. But even Zero Hand doesn't significantly injure the King. With his strength and aura completely gone, Netero chuckles and suggests Meruem shouldn't underestimate humans, finally revealing his name. Despite being completely withered and vulnerable, he manages to strike fear into the King's heart, claiming Meruem knows nothing about humanity's endless potential for evolution and malice. In his final act, Netero pierces his own heart with a finger and detonates the Poor Man's Rose bomb in one last attempt to end Meruem's reign. Though the explosion leaves the King gruesomely injured and near death, Shaiapouf and Menthuthuyoupi soon revive him. What the Ants don't know is that the bomb contained lethal poison that eventually kills not just Meruem, but Shaiapouf and Menthuthuyoupi too. Before heading into NGL, the V5 ordered Netero to eliminate the Chimera Ants by any means necessary, with the Hunter Association taking all responsibility. He recorded a DVD announcing his resignation as Chairman and giving instructions for the next election, then had the bomb implanted in his chest, programmed to detonate when his heart stopped. Prior to the Black Whale's Dark Continent expedition, his personal secretary shows another video recording Netero made for the Zodiacs. In it, he talks about his Dark Continent ventures and how he forbade his son Beyond from returning until after his death. He explains that there were no battles to win or lose there \- just a survival match against nature itself. He wants a group capable of a more successful venture than his son's planned expedition, but warns that great risk will follow since he rates this task as "A" level difficulty. Though he phrases it as a request, his repeated emphasis makes it clear it's really an order.

2. **Meruem**

When someone can survive the ultimate attack from the world's strongest Enhancer, you know they've earned their spot among the seven Ultimate Nen users in the entire series. Meruem, officially rated as an Ultimate Emitter, holds the crown as King of the Chimera Ants and represents the most powerful member of his species to reach the Known World from the Dark Continent. Unlike Netero, who achieved Ultimate status through decades of brutal training and combat experience, Meruem was simply born with immense talents and unfathomable strength. Even before birth, he demanded to emerge prematurely, tearing through the Queen's abdomen and forcing his way into the world while causing the Queen to bleed toward death. Within moments of being born, he killed an Ant with a casual tail flick when the creature prioritized the Queen's condition over his demand for food. His three Royal Guards appeared and escorted him to his first meal, leading them to a nearby village where the King ruthlessly slaughtered an entire family and consumed a child's brain. The four then abandoned NGL and invaded the Republic of East Gorteau, targeting the Royal Palace. Meruem swiftly eliminated every soldier stationed there and executed the Supreme Leader of East Gorteau. Pitou then reassembled the corpse to create a puppet, allowing them to control the nation's citizens. Meruem commanded all East Gorteau citizens to gather in the capital within ten days for a three-day national rally. His goal was to implement the Selection \- a process of brutally awakening the population to Nen to transform survivors into high-quality food and Nen-capable soldiers before proceeding with Unification: complete world conquest. When Killua began disrupting the Selection, they had the Supreme Leader puppet appear on television to declare martial law. While the Royal Guards hunted down enemies surrounding the capital and disrupting the remote Sorting operations, the King entertained himself by learning strategy board games to sharpen his tactical abilities. They brought in national champions as opponents. He easily crushed the shogi champion, killing him when he attempted to escape. His next opponent, a Go champion, also fell quickly and later hanged himself out of pure terror. Everything changed when they brought him a Gungi champion \- a blind girl who radiated zero fear and constantly put herself down. Despite looking clumsy and unremarkable, this girl named Komugi turned out to be an absolute master at Gungi. She kept winning against the King as her strategies grew sharper and more unpredictable with each match. Frustrated by his constant defeats, he decided to mess with her head through a high-stakes wager. His psychological warfare involved granting anything she desired if she wins versus her left arm if she loses. She thought about swapping out her usual stakes, and he was completely caught off guard when she revealed that she prefers to offer her own life instead of just an arm – if she loses. This made him burst into laughter while admitting he'd been the one treating their games too lightly. He ripped off his own left arm as a gesture of apology. Shaiapouf suggested Pitou reattach his arm while he played, stating that if the King refused, he’s willing to be killed by him. Stubbornly, Meruem told him to come closer so he could finish him off. He ordered Komugi to continue playing, but she refused even when he threatened to kill her. Faced with her determination, he finally agreed to summon Pitou. This marked the first time the King bowed to someone else's demands, as he usually got his way in everything. They continued playing for three days until even Komugi reached her mental and physical limits. Claiming he wouldn't enjoy beating her while she was weakened, Meruem broke his word about not giving her breaks and allowed her to rest. These subtle changes in him worried the Royal Guards as the King became more focused on his Gungi matches than the Sorting and Unification processes. During one match, Komugi asked about the King's name, throwing him into complete confusion since he didn't have one. He discussed this with his Royal Guards, and after they reminded him that his status as "king" held more power than any name, he reverted to his tyrannical nature and headed to Komugi's room, determined to kill her. As he barged in, he saw her being attacked by a bird and immediately struck it down without hesitation. He scolded her for not calling for help while remarking on her fragility. At that moment, Meruem himself felt something had changed within him. During the Palace Raid, Zeno’s Dragon Dive tore into the palace at midnight. Meruem rushed straight to Komugi’s room, only to find her mortally wounded. Pitou, Netero, and Zeno arrived just as he gently placed her down and ordered Pitou to heal her. He asked the intruders to fight elsewhere, realizing their plan was to separate him from his guards while sparing as many civilians as possible. Accepting this, he allowed himself to be taken away when Zeno summoned his Dragon Head. He and Netero rode it away from the palace to begin their battle. Before clashing, he tried to convince Netero to support his plan for uniting the world under his rule, promising to spare him if he surrendered. The Chairman refused. Even after surviving Netero’s First Hand and Third Hand attacks, Meruem continued to push for negotiation. Netero then offered a different prize: if Meruem defeated him, he would reveal the name the Queen spoke before dying. That was when the King decided to take the fight seriously. Netero’s Ninety-Ninth Hand ripped the ground apart and sent them crashing into a vast underground chamber. Thousands of blows landed on Meruem, who began to feel faint pain but still managed to sever one of Netero’s legs. In the next exchange, after blocking countless attacks, he launched off an arm of the construct and cut off one of the Chairman’s arms. Sitting down, he demanded to hear his name. Netero responded with his ultimate move, the Zero Hand, but Meruem walked out of the crater with only minor injuries. He again ordered Netero to reveal his name. The exhausted Chairman laughed, called him by name, and warned him never to underestimate humanity. Faced with this defiance, Meruem felt fear for the first time. When Netero drove his own finger into his chest, the King realized too late that the fight had been decided from the start. Before he could escape, the Poor Man’s Rose hidden inside the Chairman’s body detonated, engulfing him in a massive explosion. The blast left him unrecognizable, his limbs mangled and his body scorched. Shaiapouf and Youpi retrieved him from the site, sobbing when they found he was still alive. Pouf gave six-sevenths of his body to restore him, and Meruem praised the taste of his sacrifice. Youpi followed by liquefying his own body for him to consume, and the King declared both of their offerings supreme. Within minutes, he had completely healed, stronger than before, though memory loss clouded his mind. After absorbing so much of his guards, he gained Youpi’s transformation powers, including wings that allowed him to fly at incredible speed — distances his guards needed fifteen minutes to cross now took him only five. He could also fire explosive aura blasts from his arm, powerful enough to obliterate rock formations. From Pouf, he inherited traits that, when combined with his En, allowed him to develop his own version of Spiritual Message. By breaking his aura into countless photon-like particles, he could instantly spread them across an area, analyze everything they touched, and even move across that field so quickly it appeared as if he had teleported. Returning to the palace, he had no memory of Komugi. To keep him from remembering, Pouf proposed a game: he and Youpi would search for Pitou while Meruem hunted the remaining intruders. If they won, he would grant them each a wish, but if they lost, they had to reveal what they were hiding from him. Cutting through crowds of hypnotized civilians, he found Welfin and interrogated him. Cornered, Welfin blurted out one word: “Komugi.” The name shattered the fog in his mind, and his memories returned. He then ordered Pouf to continue searching for Pitou and interrogate the captives before releasing them. Through deduction, he learned Komugi was hidden in the underground warehouse with Palm and Ikalgo. By then, he knew the poison from the Poor Man’s Rose was in his body, just as it had claimed Youpi and Pouf. After persuading Palm to reveal her location, he reunited with Komugi to play one final game of Gungi. For the first time, he properly introduced himself, asking her to call him by his name. When he seemed to gain the upper hand by countering her signature move, he confessed he had been poisoned and would die shortly. He revealed his last wish was playing with her but warned that the poison was contagious. She simply countered his move and chose to stay with him, leading him to believe he was born to experience that moment. In his final breath, he admitted he never managed to beat her, and asked her to say his name once more. He passed away soon after, with Komugi following him moments later, succumbing to the same poison.

3. **Zeno Zoldyck**

Meruem doesn't hold the Ultimate Emitter title alone. He shares this elite ranking with the world's deadliest assassin \- Zeno Zoldyck. The Zoldyck family has dominated the assassination game across generations in Hunter X Hunter's world, and Zeno's relentless pursuit of maintaining this legendary reputation pushed him to rival Isaac Netero's strength while achieving Ultimate-level Emitter status. Even after stepping down as head of the Zoldyck family and passing leadership to his son Silva, assassination remains his entire existence. Born in the Republic of Padokea, he underwent assassin training from birth as the previous Zoldyck patriarch. This brutal conditioning developed incredible resistance to all poison types plus high electrical tolerance. Throughout his extensive career, Zeno calculates he never eliminated anyone without proper payment \- maintaining professional standards even in murder. His relationship with Hunter Association Chairman Netero spans decades, with the two knowing each other since Zeno was just a baby. This long friendship would prove crucial during later world-threatening events. We first witness his skills when the Ten Dons hire both Zeno and Silva to eliminate the Phantom Troupe. After tracking down Chrollo using his 100-meter radius En, the father-son duo confronts the Troupe leader in a spacious room. Silva immediately warns his father about their opponent's ability-stealing powers as they enter. The battle erupts instantly, with both Zoldycks forcing Chrollo into pure defensive mode. The veteran assassin doesn't just attack mindlessly though \- he's simultaneously analyzing every single movement Chrollo makes. During this brief but intense encounter, he deduces that their opponent operates as a Specialist who must satisfy four or five specific conditions before successfully stealing abilities. Realizing he can't trick them into lowering their defenses for ability theft, Chrollo abandons strategy and commits to all-out combat. Zeno activates his Gyo, unleashing power so convincing that Chrollo immediately resorts to Skill Hunter. He selects and conjures Owl's Fun Fun Cloth for defense. Zeno recognizes this could become problematic, especially if he ignores its effects, but notices Chrollo backing away as he advances. This retreat pattern suggests their opponent is simply stalling for time. He combines Dragon Head and Dragon Lance to launch remote attacks while maintaining pursuit when Chrollo dodges. Dragon Head allows him to emit his aura as a tangible dragon extension that gets hurled at opponents like a projectile. This technique serves dual purposes \- direct attacks and transportation. The dragon can also foundation other Nen abilities like Dragon Lance. Dragon Lance lets him control the Dragon Head creation through precise hand motions without actually emitting it. The power and flexibility make it perfect for maintaining safe distance while attacking. Since this technique involves extending and reshaping his aura without separating it from his body, Dragon Lance qualifies as a Transmutation ability. His dragon moves fast enough to prevent Chrollo from closing distance while gripping him the instant Silva creates a distraction. He rapidly closes in, restraining the Troupe leader's legs and unleashing a devastating punch barrage. Shouting for Silva to attack, his son responds with two massive aura orbs that trigger a huge explosion at the auction building's ground level. Just before the Zoldycks can finish off the Phantom Troupe leader, Silva's transmitter rings, causing him to deliberately misfire. Illumi's message reveals the Ten Dons have been killed. Turns out Chrollo hired Illumi beforehand to assassinate the Ten Dons. Zeno dusts himself off and walks away, declaring Chrollo no longer qualifies as their target since their clients are dead. During the Chimera Ant crisis, Chairman Netero hires Zeno to assist the Extermination Team in separating the King from his Royal Guards, enabling mission completion with minimal human casualties. About three minutes before midnight on Selection day, both Zeno and Netero leap from a flying dragon directly above East Gorteau's Royal Palace. High above the clouds, Zeno shaped his aura into a massive dragon to cushion their descent. When his Dragon Lance clashed with Pitou’s En, both sides got a clear read on each other’s abilities. Once Pitou withdrew their En to prepare for combat, he shattered the dragon into countless fragments, unleashing Dragon Dive. The technique blanketed the entire palace grounds and everything within the outer walls. Each shard of aura carried enough force to punch through multiple layers of concrete before dispersing. But against high-level Nen users, especially Royal Guards or seasoned Hunters, it wasn’t as effective unless they were caught completely off-guard. For skilled opponents, dodging was still very possible. Even so, the storm of aura shards tore through the palace, sending the entire structure into chaos. Using the confusion to their advantage, Zeno and Netero spread their En across the battlefield to track down the King, eventually locating him in the west tower’s second floor. When they entered the room, they froze at the sight before them — the King holding a wounded girl in his arms. The King takes initiative and decides to relocate their confrontation. Aware of their plan, he agreed to be transported away. Zeno, keeping his aura free of malice, employs Dragon Head to transport both Meruem and Netero to a military weapons testing site. Once they touched down, the dragon dissolved into nothing.

4. **Bisky Krueger**

Bisky Krueger is a Double-Star Stone Hunter and the only Ultimate-level Transmuter in the entire series. Being in the same breath as Hisoka and Youpi and rated even higher than them says plenty about the kind of threat she can be. She started learning Nen at 17, mastered Shingen-ryu kung fu, and eventually became Wing's master. After passing the Hunter Exam, she even worked as an examiner herself. Her introduction comes during Tsezguerra’s selection test for Battera’s Greed Island challenge. As a Stone Hunter, she’s obsessed with gemstones, collecting rare ones and even naming them like they’re trophies. The whole reason she joined Greed Island was to chase after a rare blue jewel that only exists in the game. She clears the test alongside Gon, Killua, and about twenty others, then heads to Battera’s mansion to start the game. By that point, Greed Island is already under threat thanks to a serial killer known as the “Bomber.” That’s when she crosses paths with Gon and Killua and offers to join them, only to get shut down by Killua. Irritated, she decides to mess with their friendship from the shadows while keeping up her innocent “little girl” act. She tails them all the way to Masadora, testing their skills while pretending not to be involved. After the boys lose almost everything to bandits and then struggle against dangerous monsters in the badlands, she watches their repeated failures until she can’t stand it anymore. She secretly takes out monsters herself and eventually steps in, giving them the tip to use Gyo to trace the creatures’ power source. Once they win, she declares herself their teacher. Killua isn’t convinced, pointing out they already have Wing. That’s when she reveals she’s actually Wing’s teacher and she’s actually a 57-year-old Nen master. Their conversation is halted when she senses a killer’s bloodlust nearby. She pretends to part ways with them while instructing them to follow her later, setting herself up as bait. The killer, Binolt, takes it and chases after what he thinks is easy prey. After letting him cut her hair with his scissors, Binolt realizes her true strength and formally challenges her. She effortlessly destroys him without even using Nen. This finally convinces Gon and Killua that she’s the real deal, and they ask her to train them. Training starts immediately, and Binolt becomes part of it. She locks the three of them in a rock-walled area with strict rules: if Binolt dodges every attack for two weeks, he walks free. If he gets taken down and can’t recover, she kills him. Same punishment if he tries to escape. The boys also get punished if they fail or leave early. Over the next ten days, she notices rapid progress from both Gon and Killua. When they eventually beat him, she lets Binolt go on his promise to turn himself in. For the next month, she continues training them while they move to and from Masadora. She eventually introduces them to Ryu, watching their sparring match closely. Their slow but steady grasp of it impresses her, so instead of the planned two months, she speeds things up and moves them to Nen type training after just two weeks. Once they’ve chosen their abilities, she allows them to actually start playing the game. Meanwhile, the Bomber finally reveals himself as Genthru and, with his two allies, begins tearing through the game, killing players and stealing cards. After Gon's group obtains the rare "Plot of Beach" card from Razor, the Bombers contact them, announcing they're coming for them next. While Tsezguerra buys time, Bisky trains Gon and Killua for the upcoming battle. When it’s time for the showdown, she faces Bara, one of Genthru’s allies. Using “Accompany,” she teleports with him to a beach outside Soufrabi to split up the bombers. She dominates early but then takes a hit on purpose, letting Bara think he has the upper hand. She laughs it off and warns him about what’s coming next. That’s when she transforms, dropping her cute little girl disguise and revealing her real body: a towering, muscular form. Her intense training caused her body to become far larger and more masculine than she ever wanted. It was the price for achieving nearly unmatched strength. It’s not a form she likes but it’s the price of the strength she achieved. She doesn't even understand how she got this transformation ability. All she did was wish for a cute form for years, and the changes appeared gradually. She can alter her appearance to look younger and less muscular, but this severely reduces her physical abilities. While the transformation started as vanity, it became a perfect way to hide her true power and keep a trump card ready. She loves using her younger appearance to deceive others and gain advantages, like asking for favors or information by acting fragile. She can revert to her original form anytime when she needs to restore her incredible height and strength. After beating the bombers and clearing the game, she gets her precious gemstone and temporarily parts ways with the boys. She reunites with them when Palm contacts her to train them for battles against Knuckle and Shoot in their attempt to return to NGL. She makes them maintain their Ren for three hours, and the boys struggle hard with this training. Besides her body transformation, she can summon a Nen Beast masseuse called Cookie. This thing uses various massaging techniques and Nen-transmuted lotion to relieve fatigue and restore vitality. It can burn excess fat and cure arthritis, muscle tension, and locked joints. For training purposes, it's incredibly useful \- Gon and Killua compressed over a year's worth of aura training into just one month thanks to these massages. Cookie has many different massage types, though only one has been shown. After ten grueling days of maintaining Ren, she sends them to fight Knuckle and Shoot while still exhausted. Eventually, she notices them casually keeping Ren for an hour while talking. Before the deadline, she lets them rest, then sends them to face Knuckle again. When Gon collapses after the fight, she has Cookie treat him while sparring with Killua herself in her true form. She sees them off afterward, but unsure of the outcome and still wary of Palm, she leaves the city. During the Succession Contest arc, Killua recommends her to Kurapika, who needs trusted Hunters to monitor Kakin Empire princes by working as bodyguards. She ends up working as Prince Marayam’s bodyguard.

5. **Abengane**

Abengane is a Nen Exorcist and the only Ultimate-level Conjurer in the series, despite barely showing up in the entire series. Because of his brief appearance, we don't know much about him or what drives him, but his specialty speaks volumes: Nen Exorcism. This is the art of removing Nen Curses from people who've been afflicted, and he's the absolute best at it. He first appears as one of the potential applicants in Tsezguerra's trial for a chance to play Greed Island. After passing the test with 20 other applicants, he enters the game through a console in Battera's mansion. Once inside, he encounters a player alliance discussing the threat of a serial killer called "Bomber." The group wants to recruit skilled players to help clear the game by collecting all cards, promising to split Battera's reward money equally among alliance members. He joins up after learning that acquiring an SS-rank card would net him a bigger share of 500 million. Later, he's seen handing over his cards to an alliance member. He makes it to the group's secret base, recognizing how carefully they've hidden its location. Along with everyone else, he gets instructed to memorize a list of all spell cards and their functions within an hour. When the alliance gets close to completing the game, all members gather at the base to celebrate their success. That's when everything goes sideways. A member named Genthru makes an announcement revealing he's the Bomber and has placed bombs on every alliance member. After demonstrating his ability on someone, Genthru explains how his Countdown ability works. Abengane suddenly remembers when Genthru touched him and planted the bomb. Genthru calmly reveals the only way to disarm it is to touch him and say "I caught the Bomber." In exchange for their lives, he wants them to hand over all their spell cards and 90 specified slot cards. He then uses a "Leave" card to escape, leaving everyone panicking. Despite his life being on the line, Abengane stays calm and logical. He tells the alliance they need to stick together and asks whether they should fight Genthru or surrender their cards. Later, he uses "Magnetic Force" to travel to Gon and Bisky, telling them everything that happened at the alliance base and revealing the Bomber's identity. He begs them to warn others about the Bomber and avenge him and the other alliance members. After using "Return" to get back to Bunzen, he enters a nearby forest and uses his Exorcism ability to remove the bomb. The ritual begins with a campfire. He prepares a wooden effigy, chants a prayer to forest spirits, then weaves a netted decoration from a leafy branch before burning it. The flames swell as the ritual gathers aura from the forest and his own energy, summoning a Nen beast. The form and size of this creature depend on the curse it’s removing, and for the Countdown bomb, a massive snake-like beast appears to swallow it. The beast coils around him as some kind of a burden, and to keep it hidden, he drapes a large cloak over himself and the creature. He confidently notes that the Bomber will assume everyone from the alliance is dead, planning to use this assumption to his advantage. When Genthru and his allies betray the alliance and detonate the bombs before the countdown ends, only he remains unharmed. At some point while laying low, Hisoka tracks him down thanks to Kalluto's ability and offers him an exorcism job for Chrollo. After Gon defeats Genthru, he discreetly approaches the restrained bomber and touches his shoulder, uttering the necessary phrase to dispel Genthru's lingering Nen and dissolving the Nen beast attached to him. Once it’s over, he removes his cloak and reunites with Hisoka to make a fortune from his exorcism services in the outside world. Since Chrollo ends up fighting Hisoka later in the series, it's pretty safe to say that Abengane actually pulled off removing Kurapika's chain from him \- though we never got to see what kind of burden he had to deal with for pulling off that exorcism.

6. **Alluka Zoldcyk**

Alluka Zoldyck is the second youngest child of Silva and Kikyo Zoldyck, and she’s also the second member of her family to reach the Ultimate rating in Nen proficiency. In fact, she’s the only Specialist in the series who has achieved that level, which is a big deal considering how Specialists work. According to the memo, Specialists don’t always show clear progress with Nen like other types. Sometimes their awakening to “Ultimate” level depends on training or practicing the types of Nen they’re supposed to struggle with. Specialists are complicated, and there’s no single rule that explains them. Even with that, only one Specialist is rated Ultimate, while Manipulation type doesn't even have one. Unlike her brothers, Alluka has no combat ability or physical endurance. She’s basically like a regular kid in terms of fighting. But under mysterious circumstances, she became possessed by a strange being her family named Nanika, and now the two share the same body. Alluka is technically the Specialist, but she doesn’t use Nen skills or abilities herself. Instead, she acts as the medium for Nanika’s powers. Nanika’s real form is unknown. When it manifests, Alluka’s sclerae, irises, and mouth turn pitch black, with her mouth also stretching wider than normal. What makes it frightening is its ability to grant wishes. The process starts when someone fulfills three of her requests. After that, Nanika takes control and grants one wish. That wish doesn’t even have to come from the person who completed the requests — anyone nearby can declare their wish while Nanika is active and it will be granted. Killua has explained that the scale of wishes Nanika can handle seems limitless. She can do small things like levitating someone, or reality-bending feats like altering probability. She’s teleported someone across countries with ease, and she even lifted Gon’s deadly vow and restored his ruined body. The catch is that the bigger the wish, the harder the next three requests become. And the person who bears the burden isn’t the one whose wish was granted, but the next person she makes requests to. If that next person refuses or fails four times, the penalty is severe. At least two people will die, and the number grows depending on how large the previous wish was. The deaths happen instantly and simultaneously. First, the person who failed and their most loved one are crushed by an invisible force. After that, additional victims are chosen based on who spent the most time with the failed recipient, regardless of their relationship. Unlike the first two, they are wrung to death instead of crushed. One example was Kasuga, a Zoldyck servant. She failed to complete Alluka’s requests four times, carrying the backlash from a previous billion Jenny wish made by another servant. The result was brutal: she, her lover, and sixty-five people closest to her were killed. Through experiments with their butlers, the Zoldyck family figured out five main rules about Nanika’s ability. First, if someone fails and dies, the request difficulty resets to the lowest level. That makes it the safest time to make a wish. Second, once Nanika starts giving requests to someone, she can’t switch to another person midway. If the chosen person hides or disappears, she can’t make requests to anyone else. Third, if the chosen person dies before completing the requests, it counts as failure, and at least one more person will also die. Fourth, she can’t make requests of someone whose name she doesn’t know. And fifth, the same person can’t ask for consecutive wishes. There are also rules that Killua kept to himself until Illumi forced them out of him or figured them out. The first is that Nanika can only grant one wish at a time, but that wish can include multiple conditions, making it much more versatile. The second is that if the wish involves healing, Nanika has to touch the target. In these cases, there’s no cruel backlash — though this might be something Killua made up to protect his advantage. Healing does take a toll on her, causing Alluka to fall asleep afterward. The third is that Killua has a unique privilege because of his bond with both Alluka and Nanika. He can issue a “command,” which bypasses all repercussions. If anyone else makes a wish, they’re still bound by the normal penalties, but Killua’s requests go through safely. Lastly, he can even issue a command when Nanika is already making requests of someone else, and he’s allowed to do it back-to-back without penalty. It’s still not clear if Nanika’s wish-granting counts as Nen. There’s heavy speculation that she came from the Dark Continent, mainly because of similarities with one of its Five Threats called Ai. Both share traits like deep black eyes and the way they kill their victims. There’s also a connection to the Zoldyck family’s past — Zigg Zoldyck was part of Netero’s Dark Continent expedition, so it’s possible he brought Ai back with him, which later possessed one of his descendants. There’s no official confirmation in the main story about Nanika being Ai, but one extra panel in Volume 33 shows her with the note “I am from the Dark Continent.” In that image, her skin is shaded black using the same technique Togashi used to draw Ai, making the link even stronger. Nanika’s possession of Alluka, her drive to please others, and her devotion to Killua also fit Ai’s description as the “co-dependence of desire.”

7. **Adult Gon**

Gon Freecss, the main protagonist for most of the series, is a Rookie Hunter who just learned how to use Nen for roughly one year. Before you think he's getting “main character privilege” for landing a spot on this list despite his limited experience and training, he's actually rated "Great" which is basically the lowest tier. This makes perfect sense since he's just starting his journey to develop his Nen ability Jajanken. Under Bisky's instruction, he develops three different attack modes for his signature ability. Rock is his close-range strike and his strongest move thanks to his natural affinity for Enhancement. He channels his aura into his fist with Ko and, if given time to build up Ren, delivers an even heavier punch. Scissors is his mid-range option, where he transmutes his aura into a blade extending from his middle and pointer fingers. Then there’s Paper, his long-range attack, which turns aura into a projectile using Emission and fires it from his palm. Considering all the battles he's won using these abilities, giving him a "Great" rating seems totally fair at this point. But there's no denying the hidden potential lurking inside him. Hisoka, Wing, Bisky, Razor, and Chairman Netero all see him becoming a force to be reckoned with in the future. This suggests that when he reaches adulthood, he'll become an Ultimate Enhancer-type just like Netero. His fate seems sealed in that regard, he just needs to keep training for years to make it reality. That path, however, was cut short by one of the most extreme vows ever made in the series. Despite his friendly, upbeat personality, he had a self-destructive streak when consumed by anger. When his emotions get ignited, he becomes completely irrational and oblivious to everything else. His breaking point came when the Chimera Ant Royal Guard Pitou kills Kite, the closest thing he had to a father figure, and later discovers they used his corpse as a mindless training puppet. When he finally accepts that Kite can no longer be revived, his rage takes complete control and he channels it toward his target who's also ready to kill him. While we've seen him develop and strengthen his Jajanken ability throughout his training, he knows all too well his current form won't be enough to take down Pitou. That's when he risks absolutely everything. In that moment of despair, he made a vow that forced his body into its ultimate form — summoning every ounce of strength he would ever have in his lifetime. He aged his body forward, reaching the level of power it would take decades of training to achieve. His physical strength, aura output, and Nen capacity skyrocketed, terrifying Pitou to the point of thinking he could even rival Meruem. His speed and reflexes sharpened to the point he could easily dodge Pitou’s Terpsichora who had already pushed their limits. Even a single Ko-enhanced kick sent the Royal Guard flying and coughing blood, something that Netero himself hadn’t managed to inflict. His first adult Jajanken launched Pitou across a hill and knocked them unconscious. A few more attacks later, and he finished one of the most brutally one-sided fights in anime. When Killua saw him in this state, he immediately realized it would normally take decades of training to reach that level of power. The price he paid was as massive as the boost he received. Pitou guessed the ability had only been possible because an extraordinarily gifted individual willingly threw away his natural potential and compressed his entire life force. They predicted he would never be able to use Nen again in the future. After the Royal Guard's death, his body shrinks back his normal body and fell into a coma. His body began decaying rapidly that he requires constant life support. Without Nanika’s intervention, he wouldn’t have survived. Despite making a full recovery thanks to Nanika’s wish-granting abilities, he remained completely unable to see or use aura afterward. Ging later explained that he had simply returned to “normal,” and that surviving at all was something he should be thankful for.
