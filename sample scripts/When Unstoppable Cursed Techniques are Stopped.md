**When Unstoppable Cursed Techniques are Stopped**

In *Jujutsu Kaisen*, certain cursed techniques get hyped up so hard that even the characters themselves start believing nothing in the universe can touch them. The funny thing is, the series loves nothing more than taking these supposedly untouchable abilities and finding the one specific, humiliating way to shut them down for good. Today we're going through every major cursed technique that got sold to us as unbeatable and breaking down exactly how each one got cooked.

* **Limitless**

Limitless is conceptually the most "unstoppable" defense in the series belonging to Satoru Gojo, and honestly? The technique backing that up is absolutely filthy. Limitless is an inherited technique passed down through the Gojo Clan that brings the concept of Infinity into reality, letting the user manipulate and distort space at will. Gojo also happens to be the only one who can truly go all-out with it because he's the first person in 400 years to be born with both the Limitless and the Six Eyes. Now, the main defensive application of all this is called Infinity, and it's exactly as broken as it sounds. The technique is founded on the Achilles Paradox, where the distance between two objects can be divided infinitely. Basically, Gojo slaps an invisible layer of infinite space between himself and anything trying to touch him, so attacks keep subdividing that distance forever and never actually arrive. Infinity also automatically detects threats and lets harmless things like oxygen pass through while stopping attacks (*Episode 7*). The man is literally breathing fine inside a mathematically infinite wall. And this isn't just sitting there tanking hits either. Gojo is also throwing out Blue (*Episode 20*), which collapses space to pull things in, and Red (*Episode 28*), which blasts everything away with violent repulsive force. Combine them both and you get Hollow Purple (*Episodes 20, 28*), an expulsion of imaginary mass that travels so fast it's almost impossible to dodge. The guy has a perfect defense and nukes for hands. So how do you stop something like this? Of course, being a literal god gets old for the plot, so the universe had to find ways to actually hit the man. The first time Gojo really gets humbled is by Toji Fushiguro, who comes in with the Inverted Spear of Heaven — a cursed tool that immediately cancels any technique it makes contact with. Toji uses it to shut off the Infinity mid-fight and drives it straight into Gojo's neck (*Episode 27*). Then comes Sukuna, who doesn't even bother trying to pierce through the infinite distance.  Instead of trying to cut Gojo, Sukuna uses a "World Slash" that cuts the actual space Gojo is standing in (*Chapter 236*). He basically deletes the coordinates of the universe where Gojo exists. The Infinity protects Gojo from things aimed at him but it has no answer for an attack aimed at the world he's standing in. Gojo goes from being the most invincible man on the planet to being split in half because Sukuna decides to rewrite how geometry works. It is a ridiculous way to die, but it proves that even a barrier made of infinity fails when the opponent simply deletes the ground you are standing on.

* **Shrine**

Ryomen Sukuna owns the most terrifying kitchen set in existence with his innate technique called Shrine. This ability basically turns the entire world into a cutting board where Sukuna is the only one holding the knife. He uses two main types of invisible slashes called Cleave and Dismantle to shred everything in his path without even moving a muscle. Dismantle is his standard long-range projectile that chops up inanimate objects or unsuspecting targets (*Episode 4*), while Cleave adjusts itself based on the toughness of the target to ensure it cuts through them in one go (*Episodes 13, 39*). On top of that, Shrine has a hidden feature that kicks in with the Open command, letting him pull out a fire-based attack that straight-up burns his target to ash (*Episode 40*). And when Sukuna wants to go full villain mode, he opens Malevolent Shrine. His barrierless domain basically turns the entire area into a massive guaranteed-hit zone, where his constant slashes — Cleave, Dismantle, and Furnace — will hit anything within range not. It is a completely unfair power that makes the concept of armor or defense feel totally useless (*Episode 41, Chapter 225*). The invincible streak of Shrine finally starts to wobble when Sukuna runs into the relentless tagging of Yuji Itadori and a bunch of desperate sorcerers at the end of the Shinjuku showdown. Yuji realizes that Sukuna’s technique is tied to his control over his physical vessel, so he starts landing punches that specifically strike the boundary between Sukuna’s soul and Megumi’s body. Every time Yuji connects, Sukuna’s cursed energy output drops and his grip on Shrine gets shakier (*Chapter 258*). After suffering seven consecutive Black Flashes from Yuji, Sukuna's reverse cursed technique output drops and his Hollow Wicker Basket starts to fall apart. Sukuna tries to reset and fire off Malevolent Shrine one last time, but Nobara Kugisaki's Resonance hits him before he can activate it, leaving him fully exposed to the sure-hit effect of Yuji's domain, which drives him out of Megumi's body for good and leads to his ultimate defeat (*Chapter 268*). The King of Curses, stopped not by someone out-cutting him, but by getting absolutely battered until he physically couldn't hold it together anymore.

* **Idle Transfiguration**

Mahito is a cursed spirit born from humanity's hate and fear of other humans. His technique is called Idle Transfiguration, and it's easily one of the most conceptually disturbing abilities in the entire series. It allows him to reshape the souls of anyone he touches, which automatically changes their physical form to match. This means he can turn a person into a tiny ball, a giant monster, or a puddle of goo with a single tap. The truly broken part is that Mahito also applies this to himself, making him basically immortal against normal attacks. Since he can always maintain the shape of his own soul, blowing his head off or punching a hole through his chest does absolutely nothing. He just regenerates instantly because his body follows whatever shape his soul dictates. The technique requires no cursed energy from Mahito's reserves, meaning he can use it repeatedly without any drain to his power. Then there's his Domain Expansion, Self-Embodiment of Perfection. Inside the domain, Mahito is automatically connected to the soul of anyone caught within it and can transfigure them without even needing to make physical contact. Inside his domain, he's untouchable and everyone else isn't. The invincible wall of Idle Transfiguration finally crumbles during the Shibuya Incident when Nobara Kugisaki enters the chat. She uses her Resonance technique on a severed piece of Mahito’s double, which sends a spike of cursed energy straight into his main soul. This attack rattles Mahito because he can’t defend against his soul getting hammered from the inside. She doesn't need to touch him at all, which blows a massive hole in his entire defensive strategy — something Mahito himself calls her a "natural enemy" for. Then Yuji and Todo team up and just refuse to stop hitting him. Mahito is forced out of his Instant Spirit Body of Distorted Killing true form and ultimately defeated by a Black Flash imbued with Yuji's maximum cursed energy. Critically injured and terrified, Mahito attempts to flee, only to be absorbed by Kenjaku, who uses Cursed Spirit Manipulation to consume him entirely. 

* **Ten Shadows Technique: Mahoraga**

Mahoraga is the strongest shikigami of the Ten Shadows Technique, an inherited cursed technique of the Zenin Clan that lets the user summon up to ten shikigami through their shadows. It is the most uncontrollable shikigami in the entire history of the Ten Shadows Technique, and not a single Ten Shadows user has ever managed to tame it. It is essentially a walking middle finger to the entire concept of combat because of its ability to adapt to any and all phenomena which all comes down to its Wheel of Adaptation. The wheel spins every time Mahoraga is attacked, and once it completes a rotation, Mahoraga adapts to that attack completely — hit it with fire, it becomes immune to fire, hit it with physical attacks, its regeneration adapts to heal instantly. Basically, every attack you throw at it is just free tuition for this thing. Mahoraga eventually meets its match when Sukuna realizes he needs to delete the general before the wheel spins too many times (*Episode 41*). During their first encounter, Sukuna opens his Domain Expansion and shreds Mahoraga with a constant barrage of thousands of slashes. Since Mahoraga already started adapting to the cutting attacks, Sukuna switches things up and blasts it with a massive fire arrow. This works because Mahoraga only adapted to the "cutting" part of Sukuna’s kit and had zero resistance to getting vaporized by a giant nuke of heat. Sukuna tamed Mahoraga after taking over Megumi’s body and unleashes it in his ultimate battle against Gojo. During the fight, it adapts its way through Gojo's Infinity across five consecutive domain clashes (*Chapters 229 \- 230*). Gojo realizes that Mahoraga is slowly learning how to bypass his Infinity, so he prepares a move that is too big to adapt to. He combines his Red and Blue abilities to create Hollow Purple, which is basically an orb of imaginary mass that deletes everything it touches. Gojo sets off a massive Purple explosion that covers the entire battlefield and disintegrates Mahoraga instantly (*Chapter 235*). The divine general doesn't get a chance to spin its wheel or find a loophole because there is nothing left of its body to even process the damage. 

* **Deadly Sentencing**

Hiromi Higuruma is a lawyer who got tired of the legal system and decided to manifest his mid-life crisis as a domain called Deadly Sentencing. This technique forces everyone inside into a non-violent courtroom where a shikigami named Judgeman acts as the judge and Higuruma as the prosecutor. The domain comes with a set of rules that prevent anyone from using violence, effectively putting the entire fight on pause while Judgeman reveals a crime the opponent committed. If the target is found guilty, they lose the ability to use their cursed technique, and if they get the death penalty, Higuruma receives the Executioner's Sword. This sword is a glowing light tube of doom that deletes anyone it touches instantly, regardless of how much health they have or how strong their armor is. Higuruma almost wipes out Yuji with this by stripping him of his cursed energy and forcing him into a legal battle he was totally unprepared for (*Chapters 164 – 166*). That whole conviction falls apart the moment Higuruma realizes it was actually Sukuna behind the crime, not Yuji Itadori, who was just acting as Sukuna’s vessel at the time. It is an absurdly broken ability because it bypasses physical strength entirely and forces monsters to play by the rules of a courtroom. The supposedly flawless legal trap of Deadly Sentencing runs into a massive problem when Higuruma tries to use it on Sukuna. Higuruma expects the domain to confiscate Sukuna’s cutting technique because that is his most dangerous weapon. However, the domain logic has a huge loophole that nobody saw coming. Instead of taking away Sukuna’s innate ability to shred people, Judgeman decides to confiscate Sukuna’s cursed tool, the Kamutoke, instead (*Chapter 245*). This unexpected turn of events means Sukuna keeps his main slashes and continues to be a menace.  Higuruma rushes Sukuna with the Executioner’s Sword, ready to end it in one hit. Sukuna instantly clocks the danger and, at the last second, cuts off his own hand before the blade can reach him. The strike still lands, but it only pierces the severed hand, so the instant-kill effect never reaches Sukuna himself. And right after pulling that off, Sukuna wastes no time and cuts Higuruma down (*Chapter 247*).

* **Comedian**

Fumihiko Takaba is a failing stand-up comedian who accidentally became the most terrifying reality warper in history thanks to his technique called Comedian. This ability is genuinely terrifying because it turns anything Takaba thinks is funny into absolute reality. If he thinks it would be hilarious for his opponent to get splashed with red wine or hit by a speeding truck, it just happens immediately without any cursed energy cost or complex hand signs. This technique makes him completely invincible because his body instinctively avoids damage as long as he finds the situation humorous (*Episode 58*). There are no clear rules, no limits, no power scaling. The technique just runs entirely on vibes and punchlines.  Against Kenjaku — a sorcerer with over a thousand years of jujutsu knowledge — Takaba renders Cursed Spirit Manipulation completely harmless, and Kenjaku himself admits that if he wasn't careful, all of that millennium of knowledge would have been useless against him. A thousand-year-old cosmic manipulator almost gets ended by a guy who wasn't even landing his jokes at open mic nights. Takaba then exorcises a special grade cursed spirit by running it over with a truck and transports himself and Kenjaku into various simulated locations for comedic effect (*Chapters 239 \- 240*). At this point, Kenjaku isn't even fighting a sorcerer — he's trapped inside a sketch comedy show. Kenjaku eventually discovers two major weaknesses to Takaba's technique. First is Takaba's confidence. Comedian can only be activated if the user remains confident and certain. Kenjaku figures this out and goes straight for Takaba's insecurities — criticizing his jokes and making him second-guess himself — and the moment Takaba loses confidence, the technique drops entirely, leaving him fully exposed to a hard elbow to the stomach (*Chapter 240*). But once Takaba regains his confidence, the technique is up at full bar once again. Kenjaku understands that if the battle continues, he will be defeated. This led him to realize that the funny simulations won't end until Takaba's desire for laughs is satisfied. Kenjaku prepares to end it by bringing Takaba into a scenario manifested through his thoughts, a stand-up comedy performance starring them as a comedy duo. As Takaba gets more caught up in the whole stage performance, he starts losing that carefree mindset that actually powers his reality-warping. Kenjaku then steps in and gives him the perfect comedy experience he’s always been chasing, hitting that creative sweet spot and making Takaba feel like he’s finally lived out his dream. The moment Takaba feels completely fulfilled and at peace, his technique simply stops because the desire for a "funny" reality disappears. This leaves Takaba lying on the ground in a state of bliss while Kenjaku stands victorious (*Chapters 242 \- 243*).

* **Star Rage**

Yuki Tsukumo is a special grade sorcerer who decided that physical strength should be a suggestion rather than a rule by using her technique called Star Rage. This ability allows her to add an infinite amount of virtual mass to herself and her shikigami, Garuda, without actually making her body heavy or slow. She’s basically carrying planet-level force in her fist while still moving at incredible speed and precision. This technique is conceptually broken because it ignores the laws of physics and allows her to hit things with so much force that the concept of durability becomes irrelevant. During her fight with Kenjaku, she uses Garuda to completely obliterate Kenjaku’s strongest cursed spirit with one hit and then proceeds to break both of Kenjaku’s arms with a punch (*Chapter 205*). It is an invincible power that turns every physical encounter into a total slaughter because nobody can reasonably survive getting hit by a lady who weighs as much as an entire solar system. The supposedly unstoppable momentum of Star Rage runs into a brick wall when Kenjaku reveals he has been hiding the perfect counter in his back pocket. Yuki hits him with everything she has, but Kenjaku survives by using an antigravity technique that he stole from Yuji’s mother. Even when Yuki decides to go for the ultimate move and turns herself into a literal black hole to delete everything in the area, the technique fails to finish the job. Kenjaku uses his own body as a domain and activates his antigravity powers at the perfect moment to stabilize his physical form against the crushing weight of the singularity. This results in Yuki burning through her entire life force and turning into a massive gravitational pit only for Kenjaku to crawl out of the rubble with his clothes barely even messed up (*Chapter 208*). Yuki ends up dying while Kenjaku just walks away. It is a frustrating way for a special grade to go out, but it shows that even a planetary-level punch can be stopped by a guy who has a counter for literally everything.

* **Time Cell Moon Palace**

Projection Sorcery is an inherited technique in the Zenin Clan, handed down from Naobito Zenin to his youngest son, Naoya Zenin. This technique essentially forces the world to run at 24 frames per second. The user must map out their movements for the next second and follow that path exactly, allowing them to move faster than the human eye can even track. Anyone they touch also has to follow the 24-frame rule, and if the victim fails to move correctly, they get frozen into a flat 2D frame for one second. It only lasts a second, but anything stuck in that frame is wide open for attacks (*Episodes 38, 48*). When Naoya returns as a terrifying cursed spirit and reveals his Domain Expansion, Time Cell Moon Palace, this takes the annoying 24-frame rule to a more terrifying level. Inside this domain, everything is viewed through a film lens, and the guaranteed-hit effect applies the 24-frame movement restriction to every single cell in the target's body. If the victim moves even a fraction of a millimeter in a way that doesn't align with Naoya’s scripted movement, their cells go out of sync and their body starts physically ripping apart. Naoya uses this to instantly immobilize Daido and Miyo, leaving them coughing up blood because their internal organs are failing the frame-rate check (*Chapter 198*). It is an invincible trap because you literally cannot breathe or blink without your own biology turning into a glitchy mess. However, Naoya forgets one tiny detail about his own domain rules. Maki Zenin enters the domain, but since she has zero cursed energy, the domain’s barrier treats her like an inanimate object. Naoya’s guaranteed-hit effect relies on tracking cursed energy to find its target, so it completely ignores Maki’s existence. Maki recognizes that she is invisible to the domain’s logic and takes the opportunity to end the fight, swinging the Split Soul Katana and slicing him right through his center. The domain shatters instantly because its user gets humbled by the one person who doesn't exist in the eyes of jujutsu sorcery. 

* **Construction: True Sphere**

Yorozu is a total obsessive from the Heian era who uses her Construction technique to create the most mathematically obnoxious weapon in the series called the True Sphere (*Chapter 219*). This thing is a perfectly smooth orb that has zero surface area, which means it exerts an infinite amount of pressure on anything it touches. Since a true sphere cannot exist in reality, Yorozu uses her cursed energy to force this glitch into the world. It is a completely broken ability because no material or armor can withstand infinite pressure. It deletes anything in its path instantly because the laws of physics say that a point with no surface area touching a solid object equals total annihilation. Yorozu dominates her fight by hovering this ball of death around, threatening to erase Sukuna from existence the moment it grazes his skin. It is an invincible win condition because you cannot block or parry something that literally ignores the concept of friction and resistance. The supposedly unstoppable True Sphere runs into a massive problem because Sukuna is the king of finding loopholes. Sukuna spends the entire fight using the Ten Shadows technique to let Mahoraga adapt to Yorozu’s liquid metal and construction habits. By the time Yorozu drops her ultimate domain and launches the sphere, Mahoraga has already finished its homework. The big wheel on the general’s head spins and Mahoraga simply shatters the sphere with a single strike. The most mathematically perfect attack in history ends up failing because Sukuna lets a giant monster do all the heavy lifting for him.

* **Mythical Beast Amber**

Hajime Kashimo is a lightning-obsessed battle maniac from the Edo period who spent his entire second life refusing to use his cursed technique on anyone except the strongest sorcerer in history, Sukuna. This ability, Mythical Beast Amber, is the definition of a one-time-only self-destruct move because it fundamentally rewrites Kashimo’s physical body into a form that can manifest any electrical phenomenon. He essentially stops being a human and starts being a living storm of charged particles. Once he activates it, he can vaporize things with electromagnetic waves, shoot concentrated electrical beams, and move with a speed that makes normal combat look like it is happening in slow motion. Kashimo dominates the initial moments of his final fight by blitzing a weakened Sukuna and landing hits that would have turned any other sorcerer into a pile of ash (*Chapter 237*). It is an invincible transformation because it grants the user total mastery over the laws of physics at the cost of their own life. But honestly, if you’re up against Sukuna, even an unstoppable cursed technique that costs your life starts to feel kind of pointless. Sukuna counters by revealing his true four-armed form, unleashes a world-cutting slash, and begins launching a massive grid of Dismantle that covers the entire area. Kashimo ends up getting diced into tiny pieces before his body can even finish the process of self-destruction (*Chapter 238*). The most hyped-up transformation in the series results in a total washout because the King of Curses basically treats the word “unstoppable” like it doesn’t apply to him at all.
