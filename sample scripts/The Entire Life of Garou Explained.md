**The Entire Life of Garou Explained**

Garou is the self-proclaimed "Hero Hunter" and he's probably one of the most complex characters you'll ever encounter in the world of *One Punch Man*. This guy isn't your typical villain \- he's a martial arts genius who became a major problem for both the Hero Association and the Monster Association. His story takes you through one of the most twisted character arcs ever written, where a bullied kid turns into something that could literally end the world.

* **History**

As a child, he was always on the outside looking in. While the other kids were busy playing “heroes and villains,” he kept to himself, usually with a book in hand. But even in games, he couldn’t escape being cast as the bad guy. One day, a popular boy named Tacchan was playing Justice Man with other students, and Garou tried to stop him from kicking someone. Instead, Tacchan flipped the narrative and labeled Garou the “villain” of their game. When he dodged an attack, Tacchan slipped and hurt himself. Tacchan furiously asked why he dodged and told the other kids to restrain Garou and kicked him in the face. In Garou’s eyes, Tacchan was everything wrong with “heroes” — popular, adored, always smiling, but underneath it all, just another bully who got away with anything. Teachers weren’t any better. When he tried to explain, his teacher ignored him, lecturing him on not fighting and scolding him for “confusing games with reality.” He was then forced him to apologize to the very kids who ganged up on him. To a young Garou, this felt like proof: the so-called “heroes” of the world were just society’s favorites, while anyone who didn’t fit in was cast as a villain. (*Season 2 Episode 12 03:10 – 06:41*)

Even TV drove the point home. Watching the Justice Man show, he found himself rooting for the villains. They always fought hard, but Justice Man showed up and crushed them every time. When he asked his father if a villain would ever beat Justice Man, the answer was a blunt no. That made no sense to him. If monsters and villains always tried their best but were destined to lose no matter what, then the system was broken. That frustration hardened into a goal: he would become the ultimate “monster,” someone who could rewrite the script and never lose. He wants to show the world that for once, the monster can actually come out on top instead of always losing like they do in every story. (*Season 2 Episode 4 00:01 – 01:17*)

As a teenager, he traveled incredibly far to reach Bang's dojo. The journey was so exhausting that when he finally arrived and rudely asked Bang if he was strong, he immediately passed out from the strain. Another student, Sour Face, wanted to throw him out, but Bang was impressed by the kid's determination. He told Sour Face to feed Garou until he was full when he woke up. (*Season 2 Episode 11 21:34 – 22:03*) Bang took him in as a student, and he quickly became the absolute best at the dojo. His talent was scary \- he could outperform students who had been training for years. During one memorable training session, he watched Sour Face struggling to break through a stack of ceramic tiles. When it was his turn, he smashed through the entire tower except for one tile at the very bottom. Bang pointed out that missed tile as proof that Garou still had room to improve, which served as a constant reminder that perfection was still out of reach. But Garou's hunger for strength was insatiable. Six months before the Super Fight tournament, he decided he had learned everything the dojo could teach him. So he went completely berserk. He rampaged through the entire facility and beat up most of the students, including Sour Face. (*Season 2 Episode 4 09:57 – 10:15*) Bang himself had to step in to stop the destruction, giving Garou a serious beating before kicking him out permanently. On his way out, Garou wasn't done causing trouble. He broke into a safe containing scrolls of secret techniques and managed to get his hands on the knowledge of Exploding Heart Release Fist. Then he entered the Super Fight tournament in disguise, knocking out the real "Wolfman" competitor and stealing his identity. He won the entire tournament while keeping his true identity hidden.

* **Garou Introduction Arc**

His first major appearance was at a Hero Association meeting where he casually strolled in and announced he could beat everyone there single-handedly, even if they all ganged up on him. The heroes probably thought he was just another delusional wannabe until he actually backed up his words. He proceeded to demolish three A-Class heroes who were borderline S-Class in strength, along with everyone else present except for Sitch and Speed-o'-Sound Sonic, who had already left the meeting. The beating was so thorough and one-sided that it left everyone stunned. As he walked out, he promised to return in six months after finishing his training journey. He planned to kick down their front door and maybe even fight King, the supposedly strongest hero. (*Season 2 Episode 2 00:01 – 03:38*) The Hero Association started calling him the "Human Monster," though most people didn't take him seriously as a real threat. Only Sitch seemed to understand the potential danger this guy represented. After leaving the meeting, Garou encountered Tanktop Vegetarian in an empty alley. The fight was disappointingly easy for him \- he beat the A-Class hero without breaking a sweat. When Tanktop Vegetarian tried to threaten him about messing with heroes, he simply knocked him unconscious, expressing excitement about eventually fighting someone at the monster level. (*Season 2 Episode 2 22:29 – 23:50*)

* **Hero Hunt Arc**

His campaign against heroes took shape quickly. He tracked down and dismantled opponents one after another, building his reputation as the Hero Hunter. He started by bumping into C-Class hero Mumen Rider. Before he could make a move, Tanktop Vegetarian returned with his Tank Topper Army, seeking revenge. Just when things were heating up, S-Class hero Tanktop Master stepped in. Finally, a serious challenge approaches him. Tanktop Master landed a solid punch that floored him, but he was thrilled. An S-Class hero was exactly what he needed. Mumen Rider tried to defend Garou, but Tanktop Master knew better — he recognized Garou’s threat and prepared to end the fight. That’s when Garou unleashed the Water Stream Rock Smashing Fist. He turned the tables instantly, defeating Tanktop Master with frightening ease. To make it clear, he revealed that he hadn’t even been fighting at full power yet. With Tanktop Master down, he proceeded to beat every hero in the area, including Mumen Rider and Charanko. Their bodies were later discovered by Bang and Bomb, who realized how far Garou had gone. The hunt didn’t stop there. He interrupted three C-Class heroes mid-conversation and took them apart too. (*Season 2 Episode* *3* *04-02 – 10:45*)  Soon after, he crossed paths with Tareo — a kid clutching a hero catalog filled with information about heroes and monsters alike. Intrigued, he flipped through the book, using it to pick his next targets. Golden Ball was first. (*Season 2 Episode* *3 12:11 – 13:59*) He found his target in a bar, and they agreed to settle things in a parking lot. But Golden Ball had a trick up his sleeve — he lured Garou into an alley and started the fight early. Garou dodged his bullets with ease, but Golden Ball managed to land a ricochet shot in Garou’s leg, thinking he had the upper hand. That confidence didn’t last. He danced through Golden Ball’s Golden Breakout attack, effortlessly dodging every bullet and even reflecting them back. Before he could finish him off, Spring Mustachio arrived. Mustachio’s swordsmanship forced him to adapt. Mustachio managed to stab his hand, but he still sent him flying into trash cans and crates. He revealed he already knew about Mustachio’s secret technique and declared himself used to fighting armed opponents now. Afterward, he set his sights on the A-class rank 1 hero Amai Mask. On his way, he overheard a corrupt Hero Association officer trying to use his title to pick up women. His disgust boiled over, and he beat the man senseless out of sheer annoyance. 

Then, fate played a trick. Outside a wig shop, he encountered Saitama who was looking for a wig. Mistaking him for a wannabe hero looking to make a name by capturing him, he attacked the bald hero first. He delivered a chop to his shoulder with the impact so strong Saitama’s feet sunk into the ground, but Saitama didn’t even budge. Thinking he was a robber, Saitama tapped him on the shoulder with a casual chop, instantly knocking him out. Saitama then went inside to buy a wig, leaving him unconscious in a pile of garbage bags. He woke up the next day with only fuzzy memories of what had happened. This encounter should have been a warning sign, but his memory was too unclear for him to understand what he'd run into. (*Season 2 Episode* *3 16:00 \- 22:38*)

* **Monster Raid Arc**

Even after taking a beating from Saitama, he wasn’t done. He went back to his hideout, patched up his wounds, and reviewed his wall of photos filled with heroes he planned to hunt. During this time, he ran into Tareo again, the kid with the hero catalog. Curious, he asked him about S-Class hero Watchdog Man — a target he was itching to test himself against. Their conversation was cut short when the city sirens blared, signaling the appearance of a Demon-level threat and Metal Bat being deployed. He told Tareo to get to safety while he rushed to find Metal Bat. He arrived just as the hero was sent flying hundreds of meters across the city. At first, he thought Metal Bat had been defeated by Elder Centipede, so he considered moving on to Watchdog Man. But when Metal Bat got back up and started heading toward Elder Centipede again, he decided to challenge him. (*Season 2 Episode* *4 14:25 – 21:54*) Their clash was intense. He quickly noticed that Metal Bat was already exhausted from his previous battles, which meant he wasn’t at full strength. He mocked Metal Bat, disappointed he couldn’t see the hero at his peak. But even in his battered state, Metal Bat kept pushing back with sheer determination. Every time Garou thought he had him figured out, Metal Bat’s speed and strength climbed higher, powered by his “Fighting Spirit.” Garou was impressed, even admitting that if Metal Bat landed one clean hit, it could’ve been over for him. After a brutal showdown, he finally manages to take down the ridiculously tough hero. And just as he started gloating over his big win, he suddenly heard someone and when he turned to look, Metal Bat was suddenly in front of him, aiming to crush his head with the bat. It turned out Metal Bat’s little sister intervened and stopped him before he could land the deadly blow. The little girl reminded him of his promise not to fight in front of her. Reluctantly, Garou backed off, deciding it wasn’t worth going against those terms.  On his way out, he was being watched by Phoenix Man and Sludge Jellyfish. They saw potential in him and tried to recruit him into the Monster Association. He wasn’t interested and made his stance clear: he wasn’t joining them. (*Season 2 Episode 5 03:24 – 09:12)*

He kept moving and finally reached Watchdog Man’s turf. Sitting calmly atop a pile of defeated monsters, Watchdog Man waited. Confident, Garou attacked — but it was a disaster. Watchdog Man’s wild, unpredictable style overwhelmed him completely. None of Garou’s Water Stream Rock Smashing Fist techniques worked. Every move was countered like his training under Bang was useless against something so animalistic. Beaten badly, he had no choice but to retreat. (*Season 2 Episode 8 10:19 – 11:11*)

Surprisingly, instead of discouragement, he felt thrilled. For the first time, he realized his training wasn’t designed for fighting monsters, and now he had a new angle to grow stronger. Even injured, he wanted more. But his excitement was short-lived. He spotted King on the street and moved in for an attack, only to miss Saitama nearby. Without warning, the bald hero punted him straight into a wall with one casual kick. Once again, his hunt ended with him unconscious and humiliated. This was the second time Saitama had casually defeated him, though he still didn't fully realize what was happening. (*Season 2 Episode 9 14:51 – 16:01*)

* **Monster Association Arc**

He eventually recovered and dragged himself back to his hideout. There, he crossed paths with Tareo again. The boy warned him that his friends wanted him to leave the shack, but Garou figured out the truth — Tareo was being bullied into saying it. At first, they kept him on the defensive. He dodged, countered, but still got nicked and cut. Glasses, the weakest of the group, hung back but occasionally threw pebbles to distract him. But he was no ordinary prey. Every second of the fight, he adapted, analyzing their patterns and breaking their coordination. He caught Chain’n’toad’s chain even though it came from his blind spot. He redirected Smile Man’s kendama right back into his face. He used Wild Horn’s body as a shield against Stinger’s attacks. One by one, the group started to crumble. In the chaos, his mind flashed back to his childhood, when villains were always unfairly crushed just because they were outnumbered. That memory fueled his rage. He dismantled the heroes with surgical brutality, knocking each one out until only Death Gatling remained. Desperate, Death Gatling unleashed his ultimate attack — Death Shower, an endless storm of bullets. But he blocked and redirected every single one, making sure the child in the building behind him wasn’t harmed. Then came Genos. The cyborg’s sudden arrival escalated everything. Their fists collided midair and shook the ground. Genos unleashed Machine Gun Blows, forcing Garou onto the back foot. But he countered with Water Stream Rock Smashing Fist, even ripping off Genos’ right arm in the struggle. Genos wasn’t done. The detached arm grabbed him by the neck and slammed him into a tree while being tied up. Genos called him a thug unworthy of serious effort. But he refused to yield and broke free. Low-rank monsters from the Monster Association began to appear and held down Genos to lend Garou a hand, despite his refusal for their aid. Genos quickly took care of them and upon confirming his alliance to the Monster Association, Genos prepared to finish him off with his Incinerator. Before Genos could unleash it, Bang and Bomb arrived, telling Genos they’ll handle Garou instead. While Genos fought off swarming monsters, Bang confronted his former pupil. Garou tried to counterattack with everything he had, but Bang’s mastery of Water Stream Rock Smashing Fist overwhelmed him. Bomb joined in, and the two brothers combined their arts to crush him. Garou, weakened and cornered, was pummeled relentlessly. His attempts to escape by copying Watchdog Man’s style or using Death Gatling as a shield failed. He remembered his childhood frustrations again, fueling one last desperate counterattack. He grabbed a tree, swung it wildly, and barely forced the brothers back. But it wasn’t enough. Phoenix Man swooped in at that moment, rescuing Garou and carrying him away as Elder Centipede covered their escape. Despite Garou’s protests, he couldn’t fight back. The Monster Association had claimed him, whether he liked it or not. (*Season 2 Episode 11 13:00 – Episode 12 09:55*)

Even though he knew it wasn't the real Bang, he accidentally got power from "God" when he slapped the entity's hand away. The power rushed through his body and almost knocked him out from how intense it was.

Powered by this new divine energy, he faced off with Saitama again, having taken on a completely changed look that he called "Awakened Garou: Cosmic Fear Mode." With his new cosmic powers, he'd gained knowledge of how the universe's energy worked and how all cosmic forces behaved. Using this understanding, Garou created a devastating new fighting style called "All Life Eradication Fist." He launched a catastrophic attack called Nuclear Fission, causing massive nuclear explosions followed by several more blasts that shot high into the sky. Towering mushroom clouds rose so huge that they covered a vast stretch of the sky, leaving the watching heroes completely amazed and terrified. The clash between him and Saitama got hidden within the mountainous clouds formed by these attacks, with only their silhouettes briefly visible between flashes of tremendous power. Despite the overwhelming force of the nuclear assault, Saitama was more worried about his outfit getting destroyed than any actual damage. Garou declared that he felt completely invincible and started explaining his new abilities to Saitama. He described how martial artists had secret techniques called Shakkei, which let them borrow power from nature by copying the movements of living creatures. He preferred calling these techniques "Modes" because he thought the term sounded cooler. He demonstrated one of these modes called "Mode: Saitama," which made Saitama's face appear in place of Garou's featureless void-like appearance. This surprised the hero quite a bit. Garou then copied Saitama's signature Consecutive Normal Punches, attacking him with his own technique. Saitama responded with his own Consecutive Normal Punches, resulting in an even exchange that created massive destruction around them. The exchange ended with both fighters landing punches to each other's faces at the same time, sending them crashing into the water below. Both quickly jumped out of the water, with Garou taunting Saitama about how he must be speechless after finding someone who could match him blow for blow. Thinking about his promise to Tareo not to get scratched, Saitama felt bummed out.

Prompted by Saitama's reaction, Garou decided to show just how powerless heroes really were. He charged up his energy, his body getting wrapped in a singularity, before launching an incredibly powerful attack called Gamma Ray Burst toward Saitama. Realizing how catastrophic it would be if this attack touched the ground, Saitama decided to jump high into the air to avoid it, though Garou mistakenly thought he was just trying to dodge. The attack connected, engulfing Saitama and sending him flying far away from the battlefield. With Saitama apparently taken out, Garou landed on a pile of debris and got ready to face the remaining heroes of the Hero Association. The surviving heroes quickly figured out that this transformed figure was behind the massive explosions they had just seen. Only the S-Class heroes held their ground against him, while lower-ranked heroes ran away in terror.

Bang, one of the heroes who chose to confront Garou rather than flee, asked if a monster had influenced him. Garou insisted that he hadn't become anyone's puppet, stating that his actions were entirely of his own free will. He decided it was time to conduct true evil and declared himself as the “Ominous Future.” As he stepped closer to the remaining heroes, some of them suddenly bled from their noses and collapsed unconscious.  At that moment, Blast, the S-Class Rank 1 hero, teleported into the area and introduced himself. He tried to reason with Garou, explaining that he was being controlled by the mysterious entity known as "God." Blast warned that in Garou's current form, any attack he launched could endanger all life on Earth. Although Garou kept acting casual, he seemed somewhat interested by Blast's words and asked what he should do about it. Blast offered to transport him to another dimension where he couldn't harm anyone, but Garou turned him down. He bragged that his mere presence was causing death and believed he had finally become the perfect symbol of fear. Bang tried to step in and stop Garou physically, but Genos suddenly appeared, using the last bits of his power to rush at the seemingly unstoppable opponent. He headbutted Garou in the chest, though it proved completely useless against his cosmic-powered form. He grabbed Genos by the hair, but before he could do anything more, Blast stepped in and fought him briefly. Although Blast managed to block Garou's initial attacks, the Hero Hunter dismissed him as ultimately unimportant. Realizing that Saitama would eventually return and that he was likely hiding his true strength, he decided to kill Genos to provoke the hero into revealing his full power. He impaled the cyborg through the chest and ripped out his power core, resulting in Genos's death.

Moments later, Saitama finally returned to the scene as black radioactive liquid began raining down due to the nuclear fallout from Garou's earlier attacks. He stood facing Garou with Blast positioned on the opposite side, while everyone else, including Bang, had succumbed to the cosmic radiation being emitted by Garou's transformed body. Seeing Genos's lifeless body, a deeply sorrowful Saitama questioned whether he truly possessed a hero's intuition for preventing disasters. Overwhelmed by grief and rage, he unleashed a devastating Serious Punch toward Garou. Garou responded with his own Serious Punch, having incorporated Saitama's striking power through his supernatural copying ability, Mode: Saitama. Blast immediately realized in terror that these two identical forces colliding would destroy the entire Earth. Blast quickly summoned a portal beneath both fighters, and just before their fists connected, they were transported far above Earth's atmosphere. When their punches met, representing the clash of two identical forces (Serious Punch²), the resulting energy was so immense that it overwhelmed even Blast's dimensional gate. Realizing that this destructive energy had to be redirected away from Earth, three of Blast's allies joined him in the effort. Together, they successfully redirected the blast away from the planet. As a result, Saitama and Garou were launched far from Earth due to the recoil of their own punches, while the redirected energy blast blew a massive hole in the cosmos, presumably destroying numerous star systems in the process.

Saitama and Garou crash-landed on Io, one of Jupiter's moons. Both were totally confused about their sudden change in location, with Saitama standing over Garou on a small hill and claiming he could finally go all out against someone who could handle it. However, despite the situation, Saitama showed no excitement about this possibility. Garou suggested they find a way back to Earth first, but Saitama brushed off this idea, insisting he would beat him before anything else. He was confident that he only needed one hand to pull this off. Unfazed by this boast, Garou summoned a portal beneath Saitama trying to teleport him away. To his complete shock, Saitama casually kicked the portal aside like it was a physical object. Before he could react to this impossible move, he got hit in the face by a punch that sent him flying off Io entirely. He quickly created a portal to reappear behind Saitama, trying to strike him from his blind spot. However, Saitama somehow flipped his portal upside down, causing the attack to miss completely. Saitama then landed a punch on his chin, sending him crashing to the ground. Unimpressed by these tactics, Saitama told him to stop using "tricks" and fight him with his fists. Offended by this taunt, he stood up and obliged, declaring that he had absorbed many martial arts styles and could use all of them in perfect harmony. He began dashing around Saitama at incredible speed through multiple portals, trying to disorient him. First, he attacked with Roaring Aura Sky Ripping Fist, bombarding Saitama with a barrage of punches. When Saitama fought back, he deflected the punch with Water Stream Rock Smashing Fist, then sent the hero flying with Exploding Heart Release Fist. He followed this up with Whirlwind Iron Cutting Fist that shredded Saitama's clothes, then ended the combination with Nuclear Fission Gravity Knuckle. This created a massive atomic explosion that got immediately absorbed into a singularity by the Gravity Knuckle technique. Standing amidst the smoke and debris, he bragged that his fists were completely unavoidable and that timing was irrelevant against his perfect techniques. To his complete shock, Saitama emerged from the destruction completely unharmed except for being totally naked. The hero casually stated that he could do something similar.

Saitama showed off his own devastating technique by digging his fingers into Io's surface with one hand and unleashing "Serious Table Flip." This attack launched massive chunks of the moon's surface into the air along with Garou, completely wrecking Io's entire landscape as he got helplessly thrown high into the floating debris. This lets Saitama use the broken moon pieces as stepping stones to attack him from everywhere at once. Moving at crazy speed, Saitama created tons of afterimages and directed them all toward Garou with his "Omnidirectional Serious Punch." Garou barely dodged this coordinated assault by jumping through a portal, only to find Saitama waiting directly below him with another Serious Punch ready. He bounced off debris from this punch, but Saitama intercepted him and punched him away just as he was about to hit another fragment. Saitama began moving around him at high speed, punching him across Io's devastated surface while creating geometric light patterns from the sheer intensity of his blows. Garou furiously blocked this relentless assault using Water Stream Rock Smashing Fist and managed to land on a large piece of debris. As he tried to regain his composure and find a way to escape the floating rubble, he created another portal. But his head immediately got smashed into another rock slab, leaving him stunned and disoriented. He realized that Saitama's overwhelming attack had completely messed up his sense of direction, leaving him feeling like a trapped insect. Before he could fully process this, Saitama delivered another Serious Punch directly to his face. Shocked by Saitama's seemingly endless strength, he stayed determined, declaring he would keep copying the hero's power until he won. He activated Mode: Saitama just as both fighters punched each other in the face at the same time. During this exchange, he realized that Saitama was mimicking his portal tactics, and the hero taunted him about his earlier claims of being able to copy and surpass anyone. As their battle escalated across Io's shattered surface, they exchanged increasingly powerful blows. But something troubling was becoming apparent to Garou. Despite using his copying ability Mode: Saitama to match the hero's striking power, Saitama was somehow hitting harder with each subsequent attack. After landing a punch to Saitama's solar plexus, he was met with an uppercut that sent him flying much farther than expected. It became evident that Saitama's strength was growing exponentially during their fight, leaving him unable to keep up despite his copying abilities. He realized that at this rate, he could be killed by a single punch from Saitama.

Fueled by emotion over Genos's death, Saitama's power continued surging while Garou fell further behind. The Hero Hunter dashed around at incredible speed, preparing for his next attack while Saitama noticed that his costume had been completely destroyed and began feeling cold in the vacuum of space. In a fit of rage and desperation, he threw a punch at Saitama, who casually caught it without even looking in his direction. Just then, Saitama felt a sneeze coming on due to the cold environment. Instinctively recognizing the danger, he dodged as Saitama unleashed a "Serious Sneeze." This casual sneeze not only cleared a huge area of debris from Io's surface but also blew away half of Jupiter, exposing the planet's core. The incredible show of power left him completely stunned as they both got launched away from Io and Jupiter by the sheer force of Saitama's sneeze. While soaring through space, he spotted the Sun in the distance and used his enhanced vision to clearly see both Earth and the Moon. This allowed him to create a portal for teleportation back home. Before escaping, he threw a Nuclear Fission punch at Saitama's face to provide himself with the opportunity to get through the portal. He celebrated his return to Earth and presumed victory over Saitama, only to discover to his absolute horror that the hero was standing right behind him. Saitama complimented him on his teleportation ability. When he asked Saitama how he caught up to him, Saitama responded that the coldness of space had given him a stomachache. He had initially tried to hold it in, but the Nuclear Fission punch had surprised him, prompting him to propel himself through the portal using his own fart. With this humiliating explanation delivered, Saitama punched him in the face as they dashed toward Earth's surface. Upon crash landing, the impact of Saitama's punch shattered Mode: Saitama completely, revealing Garou's battered and bruised human face beneath the cosmic transformation.

Finally defeated, he asked why Saitama didn't just kill him and be done with it. Saitama replied that it was because he was a hero, and more importantly, it had been Tareo's final request to stop him without killing him. He paused at the mention of "final request," and when he glanced to the side, he saw Tareo's lifeless body, which had succumbed to the radiation emitted by his Cosmic Fear Mode. In complete despair, he screamed and tried to run away, finally understanding that he was responsible for this tragedy. He stumbled and fell to the ground, unable to deal with what he had done. Seeing Tareo dead and the empty landscape around them, he sadly realized that he had indeed become the "Ominous Future" he had proclaimed himself to be. The very thing he thought would save people like Tareo had instead killed the one person who believed in him. He had intended to save someone like Tareo with his twisted ideals, but Saitama pointed out that he had merely been using the boy for emotional support rather than truly protecting him. As he prepared to angrily retort to that statement, he noticed that Saitama was still holding Genos's core, realizing that someone with such overwhelming power needed something to help maintain his sanity and humanity. The defeated Hero Hunter made a sad request. He wanted Saitama to copy his ultimate martial arts technique, believing that someone with the hero's power could perform it to its fullest potential. He explained that he intended to pass on his most powerful technique \- one that he hadn't been able to master even while wielding "God's" divine power. He believed that Saitama could succeed where he had failed.

Saitama agreed to learn the technique, and Garou began teaching it, knowing that if anyone could master the power needed to challenge "God," it would be this impossibly strong hero. Suddenly, "God" stepped in during the process, beginning to drain away the power and life force it had given to Garou. His body started breaking down and crumbling into salt as the entity took back its gift. Saitama got momentarily distracted by this supernatural event, but Garou urged him to stay focused on learning the technique. As Saitama successfully mastered the ultimate martial art, Garou's final words to the hero were a warning: he was the only one capable of defeating the "Ominous Future" that threatened everything. With these last words, Garou's body completely crumbled away. Using the technique Garou had taught him, Saitama gained the ability to travel backwards through time. He witnessed all the moments from their battle on Io and Jupiter, amazed by the complexity and power of Garou's ultimate technique. When he spotted the exact moment where Garou had declared himself the "Ominous Future" to the assembled heroes, Saitama punched through the timeline itself. As the present-timeline Garou looked up and prepared to say "thank you" to the entity that had granted him power, he got suddenly struck by an incredibly powerful punch from the time-traveling Saitama. This punch carried all the amplified power from their future battle on Io. Garou went flying, crashing to the ground with his face cracking on impact. Shocked by this sudden turn of events, he began losing his divine power as it escaped through the exposed parts of his damaged transformation.

* **Aftermath**

By this point, many of the heroes had recovered and were ganging up to beat the now-powerless Garou. Metal Bat tried to intervene and stop the violence, but Amai Mask showed no sympathy toward the defeated enemy and attempted to execute him personally. However, Tareo \- who was alive in this altered timeline \- grabbed Amai Mask's leg and tearfully told him that Garou had saved him many times when the heroes couldn't save him even once. Seeing the child crying and pleading for mercy, King stepped in and told everyone to stop for the boy's sake. Both Bang and Saitama worked to convince Garou to turn over a new leaf and use his incredible abilities to save people instead of hurting them. Inspired by their words and moved by Tareo's unwavering faith in him despite everything, Garou found the motivation to continue living. He ran away from the scene, moving so quickly that only Saitama could see where he went. This represented his choice to start over and find a better path forward. 

After the whole Monster Association mess, Garou ended up under Bang’s care. With Bang now retired from hero work, his plan is to guide Garou into becoming his successor.
